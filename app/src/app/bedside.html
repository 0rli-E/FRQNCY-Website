welcome to bedside

your phone, as a tuning fork

FRQNCY's bedside mode replaces the standard alarm clock with an arrival. The audio fades in over 90 seconds. Dismissing is one breath, held — not a tap, not a swipe.

1 set a wake time and tap arm for tonight.
2 plug your phone in, leave FRQNCY open, lock the screen.
3 when the tone fades in, hold the ring on screen for one breath to arrive.
One honest thing: phone alarms on Android can fail under aggressive battery management. If yours is a Vivo, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Oppo, or Samsung, FRQNCY will show you the three settings that fix it the first time you arm. For a high-stakes wake, pair with a backup the first week.
