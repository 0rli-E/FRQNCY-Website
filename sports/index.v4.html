A practice

Five small things that compound.

i

Move every day.

Even fifteen minutes. The body responds to the year-long average, not the calendar.

ii

Train one skill slow.

Five minutes of attention-full repetition of a basic movement, daily.

iii

Sleep eight, before midnight.

Hormonal recovery is non-negotiable for adaptation.

iv

Lift something heavy weekly.

Once is enough to keep the strength curve from declining.

v

Compete, gently, often.

The shape under stakes only grows under stakes.
