Formats

Per FIRST-PHYSICAL-MILESTONES — three event types, run on different cadences.

Format A · Monthly · ~15 people

FRQNCY Salon

A dinner + structured conversation. One host, one topic, one good restaurant. Lisbon, Berlin, New York, Singapore to start.

Format B · Quarterly · ~80 people

FRQNCY Day

A single day of talks, breakouts, and a closing dinner. Public ticketing, FRQNCY editorial bar applied to speakers.

Format C · Twice yearly · 20–30 people

The Retreat

Five days. Coherence as practice. Mornings for breathwork and meditation, afternoons for talks, evenings for food and ambient music. First one at Essência, Aljezur.
