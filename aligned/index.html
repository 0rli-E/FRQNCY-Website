Why these picks

Every entry is held against five questions. Used — does someone we trust actually use it. Clean — does the ingredient or material list survive a literate reading. Independent — is the maker free of the incentive to compromise. Verifiable — can the claims be checked. Durable — will it outlast its first replacement. One Editor's Choice per shelf. Everything else is held to the same bar.

No paid placement · Affiliate-free · Updated April 2026

True freedom is living in full alignment with oneself.

Every entry on this page meets a rubric — used, clean, independent, verifiable, accessible, durable. Picks are things we use ourselves. Aligned items are things we'd recommend. Nothing is here because someone paid for it.

On money flows. Every Aligned Goods entry carries a revenue_relationship field with one of four values: none (default — no money flows between FRQNCY and this entry), contributor (the brand contributes to the FRQNCY Fund or a Sanctuary operating budget; pick decision precedes the contribution), partner (a direct revenue-share or co-marketing agreement exists), or affiliate (a commission link — currently unused, kept for completeness). Where any of the latter three apply, a small badge will render on the entry. Today, every entry is set to none. We do not accept paid placement, and the pick decision always precedes any commercial relationship — see Editorial Standards for the full rules.
