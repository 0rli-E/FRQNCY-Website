Why these picks

Every entry is held against five questions. Used — does someone we trust actually use it. Clean — does the ingredient or material list survive a literate reading. Independent — is the maker free of the incentive to compromise. Verifiable — can the claims be checked. Durable — will it outlast its first replacement. One Editor's Choice per shelf. Everything else is held to the same bar.
