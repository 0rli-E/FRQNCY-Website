Newsletter

Pick the dispatches you actually want.

Not a single mailing list. A handful of topic-specific dispatches, each on the cadence the topic deserves. Choose only what's yours.

How it works

Most newsletters ask you to drink from the firehose. FRQNCY's asks you to choose. Pick the topics, get the dispatches on those — nothing else. Unsubscribe per-topic or globally, anytime, in one click.

Each dispatch goes out when there's something to say — usually weekly for the active areas (crypto, network state), monthly for the slow ones (philosophy, places).

Backend endpoint is a stub — wire to Klaviyo / Substack / your provider of choice. Schema-wise, each subscriber stores: email, topic_subscriptions[], referral_code, opt_in_date. Confirm via double-opt-in email.
