Building FRQNCY Social

A 24-month plan to take FRQNCY from a centralized MVP to a protocol-native community network — on the destination axis of Farcaster and Nostr, but routed through AT Protocol because that's where the audience already is.

Living document v1 · April 2026

TL;DR

FRQNCY Social is a small, values-driven, crypto-adjacent community network. Today it is a centralized app on Supabase + Astro + Preact. In 24 months it is an AT Protocol-native app with a Farcaster side-channel for crypto surfaces and a Nostr read-bridge. The bet: lean chronological feed, follow-graph primary, transparent re-ranker, project-anchored conversation as the headline differentiator, monetized by freemium + tipping — no ads, ever. Cooperation, not competition — no public leaderboards.

Phase 1 — Centralized, Portable Bones

Months 0 – 6 · Where we are today

Keep the Supabase + Astro + Preact stack exactly as it is. Use this phase to nail product-market fit on a tight community before any protocol work lands in production. Shipping discipline beats architectural purity.

Protocol

  • ✓Supabase source of truth
  • ✓Auth, posts, follows, DMs, comments
  • ○Lexicon spec repo xyz.frqncy.*
  • ○Lazy did:web per user
  • ○Nightly signed-JSON export

Ranking

  • ✓Reverse-chrono feed
  • ✓Profile feed filter
  • ○Project-anchored rooms
  • ○Points-only conviction v1
  • ○Full ranking formula behind flag

Economics

  • ✓Free core product
  • ✗No ads — ever
  • ○$5/mo Supporter tier
  • ○Stripe Connect tipping
  • ○Llama Guard 3 moderation pass
02

Phase 2 — Dual-Write Federation

Months 6 – 12 · Differentiation lock-in

Stand up a production PDS serving *.frqncy.social, dual-write every post / follow / like to the user's signed repo, consume the Bluesky firehose via Jetstream, and run a custom App View on Cloudflare Workers + D1. The reputation vector (Social, Conviction, Debate) replaces the scalar karma number on profiles.

Protocol

  • Production PDS
  • Jetstream firehose ingest
  • Custom App View
  • Cross-post to Bluesky
  • Cross-post to Farcaster (Neynar)
  • In-house Ozone labeler

Ranking

  • Discover surface (interest-graph)
  • Contribution signals on profile (posts, replies, helpful tags)
  • Project-anchored rooms w/ shared context
  • Weekly "projects to revisit" digest
  • A/B test ranking weights transparently

Economics

  • NFT Founding Member (500 seats)
  • Tipping fee 0% → 3%
  • Trust-level system (TL0–TL4)
  • Community mods per channel
  • Crypto-attorney review of v2 markets
03

Phase 3 — Protocol-Native

Months 12 – 24 · Platform-optional

Flip the source of truth: PDS records are canonical, Supabase demotes to index / cache. Users can bring their own DID, export their repo, move to another PDS host without losing followers. Publish the FRQNCY Lexicons so any ATProto client can consume xyz.frqncy.resonance. Ship a Farcaster Mini App for the 630-project rating surface. The network outlives the app.

Protocol

  • PDS becomes canonical
  • BYO-DID import
  • Public Lexicon registry
  • Farcaster Mini App
  • Nostr read-bridge

Ranking

  • FRQNCY ranker as Bluesky custom feed
  • User-facing feed-tuning sliders
  • Opt-in AI personal feeds
  • Reputation as portable PDS record

Economics

  • Staked markets — only if legal-cleared and community-aligned
  • Private personal conviction journal (no public ranking)
  • Per-channel mod rotation (6 mo)
  • Public transparency report

Non-Negotiables

  • · No ads, ever. Attention that heals, not attention that extracts.
  • · Points-only conviction at launch. No real-money markets until an attorney signs off.
  • · Portable by design. Every user can export their whole repo from day one.
  • · Transparent ranking. The formula ships in docs and as a tuning UI.
  • · Blameless post-mortems. Public write-ups when moderation fails.
