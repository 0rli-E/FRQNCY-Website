Three versions, each tuned for a different surface. All lead with the
cooperation-over-competition value, reference The Space / research / roadmap,
and invite people in without hype.

## 2. LinkedIn / long-form post

> Spent the last few months designing what a social network would look like
> if the whole premise was cooperation instead of competition.
>
> The thing I kept running into: every platform that was supposed to be for
> thinkers turned into a flexing contest. Leaderboards. "Who called it."
> Dunking culture. The structure itself produces the toxicity.
>
> So FRQNCY Social doesn't have any of those surfaces:
> — No ads. Written into the charter.
> — No public leaderboards ranking people against each other.
> — No framing posts as "calls" to be won or lost.
> — Transparent ranking you can see and (later) tune.
> — Portable from day one. Your posts, follows, and reputation are yours.
>
> The destination is a protocol-native network on ATProto, with a Farcaster
> side-channel for crypto surfaces and a Nostr read-bridge. 24 months,
> three phases. The full plan is public.
>
> If you want to see the thinking:
> → The Space (principles, roadmap, research): frqncy.network/social/space
> → The research paper (~7,000 words on protocols, feed ranking,
>   monetization): frqncy.network/social/space/research
>
> Town hall is open. Come post something.

## Which to use when

- Quick drop on Twitter/Bluesky → **#1 (alt version)**. It's the sharpest
  hook and names the thing that makes FRQNCY different in one line.
- Founder announcement for a wider professional audience → **#2**. Leads
  with the *why*, not the *what*.
- Your first post on FRQNCY Social itself → **#3**. Sets the tone for
  everyone who shows up after you.

## Copy guardrails (per brand memory)

- Don't say "calls" when you mean posts or stances. Prefer *perspectives,
  takes, views, stances*.
- Don't say "leaderboard," "top analyst," "score," "ranked."
- Cooperative framings preferred: *community, perspectives, shared
  thinking, town hall*.
