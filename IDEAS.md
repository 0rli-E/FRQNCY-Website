## Mobile / App Features

### Wake-Up Function
Let users wake up to FRQNCY audio content instead of a standard alarm.

- Schedule an alarm that plays a chosen audio when it triggers (no abrupt beep).
- Source options at alarm time:
  - A specific **meditation** (e.g., morning grounding, breathwork)
  - A specific **audio track** (soundscape, binaural, frequency session)
  - The next lesson in an active **course** (progress carries day-to-day)
  - A **smart pick** — FRQNCY chooses based on goals, recent activity, or time of day
- Gentle fade-in volume ramp (e.g., 0 → target over 30–90s) to avoid jarring wake.
- Optional "wind-down" pairing: schedule a sleep audio the night before, wake with a matching morning session.
- Snooze behavior: continue the track vs. restart vs. switch to a shorter "nudge" piece.
- Works offline — pre-cache the selected audio the night before.
- Respects Do Not Disturb / overrides system silent like a standard alarm clock.
- Analytics: track wake-up streaks, which content drives the best mornings (user self-report + session completion).

**Why it matters:** turns the first minute of the day into a FRQNCY touchpoint and increases daily active use without asking users to remember to open the app.
