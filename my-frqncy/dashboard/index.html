Moon · 13 Months
Moon Calendar

Thirteen lunar months of twenty-eight days plus a Day of Rest — the perpetual calendar the Gregorian one papered over. Every month is the same shape; every date falls on the same weekday, forever.

Mayan · Tzolk'in · Haab' · Long Count
Mayan Calendar

Three interlocking wheels — the 260-day Tzolk'in (galactic timing), the 365-day Haab' (solar year), and the Long Count anchored to 11 August 3114 BCE. Includes a live converter for today's kin.

Schumann Resonance · live
Earth's heartbeat — 7.83 Hz

The electromagnetic standing wave between Earth's surface and the ionosphere. Discovered by Winfried Otto Schumann in 1952; rises with solar activity and lightning. Spectrogram below updates every ~10 minutes from Tomsk State University's real-time monitor.
