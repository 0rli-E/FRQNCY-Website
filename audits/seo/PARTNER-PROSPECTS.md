# PARTNER-PROSPECTS — Phase 5.5 partner backlink program

*Created: 2026-05-13. Status: v1, not-yet-contacted across the board.*

The goal of this document is to identify aligned organizations where a real partnership produces reciprocal backlinks, brand mentions, and audience cross-pollination. Each candidate here is a relationship to build, not an SEO transaction. Link-swaps and paid placements are off-strategy (see `proposals/EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md` §2).

Tier-1 candidates are organizations FRQNCY already covers under `/places/`, `/orgs/`, `/people/`, or `/media/`. The relationship is already implicit; the work is to make it explicit and bilateral.

Where a candidate's current operating status in 2026 is uncertain, the entry is marked `[verify]` — Orlando should confirm via web search or direct contact before sending outreach.
