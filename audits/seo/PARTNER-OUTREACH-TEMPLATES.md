# PARTNER-OUTREACH-TEMPLATES — Phase 5.5

*Created: 2026-05-13.*

Five templates, one per partnership category. Each is a real letter, not a form. Each opens with a specific reason for writing (a particular piece of the partner's work, a specific shared question), names a concrete ask, and offers something concrete back. Length sits between 200 and 350 words by design — long enough to demonstrate FRQNCY isn't a scraper, short enough to respect the recipient.

Voice rules per `audits/seo/CONTEXT.md` §4: present-tense, declarative, conviction not hype, no "unlock," no "leverage," no spiritual cliches as direct self-description. Editorial-standards rules per `proposals/EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md`: no paid placement, no implied sponsorship, money flows disclosed.

Each template is meant to be edited per recipient — the bracketed placeholders are where the specificity lives.

## Notes on use

- These are templates, not form letters. The bracketed sections are the load-bearing specificity. If a partner outreach is sent without filling them in, it will read as spam and will fail.
- Send no more than 5 per day. The point is the relationship, not the volume.
- Log every send in `audits/seo/PARTNER-TRACKER.md` the same day.
- If a partner replies with interest, the next step is a call, not a longer email. The relationship gets warmer in voice and colder in text.
- When a partner's site links to FRQNCY, add the URL to the tracker's `reciprocal-link-confirmed` column and verify the link continues to resolve at 30, 60, and 90 days.
