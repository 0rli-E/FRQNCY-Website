## 1. Platform constraints (verified 2026-05-13)

- **Max post length, text-only:** 4,096 characters. Posts here are 200-1,200 chars; one drop (Post 7) is longer-form.
- **Max post length, with media attached:** 1,024 chars for free accounts, 4,096 for Telegram Premium. Posts that need a photo attached are kept under 1,000 chars to be safe.
- **Link previews:** Telegram fetches `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image` server-side and renders a full-width card when the image is ≥ 1280×640. FRQNCY's per-topic OG cards are 1200×630 (verified in `og/`), which Telegram displays as a banner. Posts that link to a FRQNCY page rely on the OG card; no separate image attachment needed.
- **Pages without per-topic OG cards** (verified missing: `/curation/`, `/abilities/`) inherit the site's default `og-image.png`. The preview still renders.
- **Username rules:** 5-32 chars, Latin letters + digits + underscore, no leading/trailing underscore. `@frqncy_network` (14 chars) is compliant.
- **Discoverability:** public channels appear in Telegram global search by username and by name. Channel name `FRQNCY Network` returns under both `frqncy` and `network` queries.

## 4. Sequencing logic

**Posts 1-3 (Days 1, 3, 5) — what the channel is.**
Charter, then editorial axis, then explicit standard. By Day 5 a new subscriber understands the cadence, the discipline, and the integrity test the discipline runs on.

**Posts 4-11 (Days 8-24) — the rhythm.**
Pairs that build on each other: Post 4 (Bitcoin Standard) pairs with the Network States Post 5 — money before geography. Post 7 (Conscious Capital topic) pairs with Post 8 (Unto This Last book) — frame, then foundational text. Post 9 (Meditation topic) pairs with Post 10 (Shi Heng Yi person) — practice, then a teacher who carries it. Post 11 (MCP) sits between as the technical-infrastructure surface, signalling the channel's depth without rotating tone.

The two manifesto lines (Posts 6 and 12) sit at the third and sixth Saturdays — the rest day in the Tue/Thu/Sat cadence. Short reads for the slower slot.

**Posts 12-14 (Days 26-31) — actions.**
The free-layer line earns the next two posts permission to point readers to the membership page (soft anchor), the newsletter, and the topic graph. Post 14 hands the reader back to `/explore.html` — the place the network lives in full.
