# HARO Pipeline — Inbound Press Operating Playbook

**Purpose.** Daily-routine pipeline for responding to journalist queries on platforms that succeeded HARO and adjacent inbound-press surfaces. Each successful response earns a quoted mention with a URL backlink in a published piece. Expected hit rate: 5–10% of substantive responses convert to a landed quote.

**Brand-collision discipline.** Per `audits/seo/MENTION-MONITORING.md`, "FRQNCY" is contested. Always sign and brand responses as **"FRQNCY Network"** or **"Orlando Eisenreich, FRQNCY Network (frqncy.network)"** — never as just "FRQNCY".

## 2. Recommended subscription stack for FRQNCY

The goal is broad coverage at zero or near-zero cost, with one paid lever if hit rate justifies it after 60 days.

### Tier 1 — sign up day one (all free)

1. **HARO (Featured.com)** — 3×/day digest. Broadest reach, easiest scan. Sign up at helpareporter.com.
2. **Source of Sources (SOS)** — separate digest from the same audience pool but with Shankman's editorial taste; lower volume, often higher quality. Sign up at sourceofsources.com.
3. **Qwoted free tier** — 2 pitches/month is the limit, but the queries-on-2-hour-delay feed is still worth scanning. Sign up at qwoted.com.
4. **MentionMatch** — for the harness / curation / AI-citation pitches only. Sign up at helpab2bwriter.com.
5. **#JournoRequest on X** — set a saved-search column or a Zapier alert on `#journorequest` + your beat terms. Free, no signup beyond an X account.

**Estimated daily inbound:** ~250–500 queries scanned, of which FRQNCY's beats touch ~5–15 per day.

**Monthly cost:** $0.

### Tier 2 — upgrade only if Tier 1 produces results

- **Qwoted Pro ($99/mo annual)** — upgrade after 30 days **only if** the free-tier feed shows ≥ 1 in-beat query per day that's already been claimed by the time the 2-hour delay clears, AND there's evidence the free-tier pitches are landing replies. Without that evidence, don't upgrade.
- **SourceBottle enhanced ($25/mo)** — only if the AU/UK angle matters for a specific campaign window (e.g., a partner sanctuary in Australia, a launch with UK press).

### Tier 3 — defer

- **ProfNet expert side** — institutional pricing through PR Newswire account; skip until FRQNCY has a publicist or PR firm involved.

**Recommended monthly cost at start:** **$0.** Re-evaluate at the 60-day mark.

## 3. The daily routine (5 + 5 minutes)

The whole point of inbound press is that the cost-per-attempt is small enough to do every day. The routine fits in a 10-minute morning block, calendar-locked.

### Calendar block

- **08:30–08:40 every weekday** (or another consistent slot — consistency beats volume). Block as "FRQNCY · inbound press". Recurring.

### Step 1 — Skim (5 min)

1. Open HARO email digest (one of the three sent that morning).
2. Open SOS email digest if it arrived.
3. Open Qwoted's queue (filter to FRQNCY beats — see triage below).
4. Glance at X #journorequest saved search; scroll the last 12 hours.

For each query, ask: **does FRQNCY have authoritative, real content that backs this answer?** If yes, flag it. If no, skip it. Do not respond to anything you can't substantiate from an existing FRQNCY page.

### Step 2 — Respond (5 min, only when something fits)

If a query passes triage, draft a response using the template in §5. Send it. Log it in `HARO-TRACKER.md`. Move on.

If nothing fits that day, close the tabs and reclaim the second 5 minutes. Most days will be skip days. That's correct.

### Cadence reality check

- Expect to send **1–3 responses per week** at steady state, not per day. The high-volume game is what burned HARO under Cision; the FRQNCY game is the opposite — fewer, more substantive responses, every one of them backed by a real page on the site.
- Expect to send the **first 10 responses across the first 2–3 weeks** to build momentum.
- Expect the **first landed quote 30–60 days from start**, not week one. Honest baseline.

## 4. Triage criteria — when to respond, when to skip

FRQNCY's authoritative beats — areas where FRQNCY has real, substantive, curated content that backs a quote:

1. **Consciousness & contemplative practice** — meditation, contemplative neuroscience, attention, presence. Backed by `/meditation/`, `/contemplation/` [verify], the curated reading list, and 89 vetted people pages.
2. **Money & conscious capital** — values-aligned investing, regenerative finance, B-Corp / mission-driven funds, the FRQNCY Fund pillar. Backed by `/conscious-capital/`.
3. **Network states & sovereign communities** — physical sanctuaries, digital nation-building, the FRQNCY Sanctuary pillar. Backed by `/network-state/`, the /places/ sector.
4. **Curation & editorial process** — why curated libraries beat algorithmic feeds, editorial standards, conflict-of-interest disclosure. Backed by `/curation/` and `/editorial-standards/`.
5. **AI / curation crossover** — AI-generated recommendations vs human curation, AI-citation surfaces (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude grounding), the harness layer. Backed by Phase 4 work and the `frqncy-content` MCP server documentation [verify exact public page].
6. **Regenerative living & well-being** — practice in the everyday, sleep, food sovereignty, urban farming, kitchen-table well-being. Backed by topic pages in `/regenerative-business/`, `/regenerative-farming/`, `/sleep/`, `/well-being/` [verify each before linking].
7. **Editorial bylines & founder-perspective stories** — Orlando speaking as a founder building under his own values, resisting paid-placement gravity, etc. Backed by `/about.html`, `/editorial-standards/`, and the founder bio.

### Skip criteria

- Anything outside the seven beats above.
- Anything requiring credentials FRQNCY doesn't have (clinical / medical advice, legal advice, regulatory specifics, anything where Orlando isn't qualified to be quoted).
- Anything that asks for a "tip" or "hack" or "secret" — FRQNCY doesn't traffic in those.
- Anything from a publication FRQNCY would not be proud to be associated with (the integrity test from `proposals/EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md` §7 applies here too).
- Anything where the only plausible answer would require fabricating a fact about FRQNCY.
- Anything where the deadline is so close that the response would have to be hasty.

### Soft-yes criteria (respond if the day is otherwise empty)

- Adjacent beats where FRQNCY has tangential content — e.g., a query about "personal libraries in the AI age" where the curation page is relevant even if it's not a perfect match.
- Founder-profile queries where the answer is biographical rather than topical.

## 5. The response template

Every response follows the same structure. ~150–200 words total. No more.

```
Subject: [Re: query subject line from the digest, exact match]

Hi [Journalist first name, if visible — else "Hi there"],

Re: your query on [one-line restatement of what they're looking for].

[Answer — 2–3 sentences. Substantive, declarative, present-tense.
 Names a specific position FRQNCY has actually taken or content
 FRQNCY actually publishes. No hedging, no spiritual cliches.]

[Optional 1 sentence: a second, sharper point if relevant —
 a concrete example, a counterintuitive observation, a number
 FRQNCY has actually published.]

For attribution:

Orlando Eisenreich
Founder, FRQNCY Network (frqncy.network) —
a curated public topic graph for consciousness, 146 subjects
across money, energy, mind, and matter. 766+ vetted resources.
Editorial standards published in the open: https://frqncy.network/editorial-standards/

Direct link to the FRQNCY page that backs this answer:
https://frqncy.network/[slug]/

Happy to elaborate, send a higher-res headshot, or point to
a specific resource on the page if useful for the piece.

— Orlando
frqncy.network · @0xOrli on X
```

### Voice rules that bind every response

- **Present-tense, declarative.** "FRQNCY publishes its editorial standards." Not "we try to" or "we aim to."
- **Conviction, not hype.** No "groundbreaking," "transformative," "ultimate," "10x," "unlock."
- **No spiritual cliches as self-description.** Consciousness and presence are topics, not adjectives for the answer.
- **Concrete facts only.** Every claim about FRQNCY traces to a real page. If the page doesn't say it, the response doesn't either.
- **No follower counts, no engagement numbers.** Cite the editorial substance, never the metrics.

### Assets to attach when requested

- **Headshot** — Orlando supplies; not yet on disk. Should be added to `/assets/press/` before scaling responses [Orlando action].
- **Logo** — `/favicon.svg` (export PNG at 512×512 on request).
- **Quotable bio line** — see §5 of `audits/seo/PODCAST-OUTREACH-KIT.md`.

## 7. Track-and-measure routine

Every response sent → one row in `audits/seo/HARO-TRACKER.md`. Every landed quote → status update + the landed URL.

Weekly review (Monday, 5 minutes, alongside the Plausible referrer check):

- Count responses sent that week.
- Count replies received.
- Count quotes landed.
- Note any platform producing zero results after 30 days — candidate for deprioritisation.
- Note any beat producing disproportionate hits — candidate for sharpening the related topic page.

Quarterly review (every ~90 days):

- Hit rate by platform (responses → quotes).
- Hit rate by beat.
- Median time from response → landed quote.
- Outlet quality distribution (tier-1 publication vs blog vs niche).
- Decision: continue, deprioritise, upgrade to Qwoted Pro, drop a platform.

The target after 90 days: **≥ 30 responses sent, ≥ 2 landed quotes with backlinks**. If 90 days passes with zero landed quotes despite ≥ 30 substantive responses, the templates need revision, not the volume.

## 8. What this pipeline is not

- **Not a press-release distribution channel.** FRQNCY does not push announcements through HARO/Qwoted. The pipeline is reactive to existing journalist demand, not promotional.
- **Not a follower-count play.** The point is the backlink + the audience seeing a substantive answer, not Orlando's profile.
- **Not a daily must-respond grind.** Some days nothing fits. That is the correct outcome on most days. Volume of responses is a vanity metric; substance per response is the real one.
- **Not a guarantee.** 5–10% hit rate is the realistic ceiling. Some weeks land nothing. Some months land three. The compound is over quarters, not weeks.

## 9. Escalation triggers

A landed quote deserves immediate follow-up when:

- It's from a **tier-1 publication** (NYT, FT, Forbes, Atlantic, Wired, etc.) — add to `audits/seo/PRESS-TRACKER.md`; thank the journalist; consider a warm follow-up offering further collaboration.
- It's the **first quote** of the program — add to homepage "As featured in" strip once a tier-2-or-above lands [Orlando decision].
- It surfaces a **factual misquote or misattribution** — correct politely, in writing, with citation.
- It generates **measurable referral traffic** (Plausible shows ≥ 10 visits in 24h from the outlet's domain).

Otherwise, log it, link it from the tracker, move on.

## 10. Sequencing — the first 30 days

- **Day 1:** sign up for HARO (Featured.com), SOS, Qwoted free, MentionMatch, set up #journorequest saved search on X. Block the 08:30–08:40 calendar slot daily.
- **Days 2–7:** run the routine. Don't force a response. Log every day, even if the log says "scanned, nothing fit."
- **Days 8–14:** by now there should be 2–4 substantive responses sent. Refine the template based on what felt natural.
- **Days 15–30:** target 8–12 cumulative responses sent. Watch for first replies. Don't upgrade Qwoted yet.
- **Day 30:** quarterly-style mini-review. Adjust platforms, refine triage, decide on Qwoted upgrade based on evidence.
