# Podcast Outreach Tracker

Pairs with `PODCAST-OUTREACH-KIT.md`. Update on every send, response, and air.

## Status legend

- `not pitched` — on the list, no contact yet
- `pitched` — email/DM sent, awaiting reply
- `responded` — host or producer replied; mid-conversation
- `accepted` — booked but not yet recorded
- `scheduled` — date confirmed, recording on the calendar
- `aired` — episode is live
- `passed` — formal no, archive the relationship
- `cold-relationship-maintain` — passed once but worth re-pitching in 6+ months with a fresh angle

## Quarterly review

Every quarter: count of pitched / responded / accepted / aired. If the conversion rate from pitched → responded is < 20%, revisit the pitch text. If responded → accepted is < 50%, the kit's angles aren't landing — refine the topics list.

Total appearances after one year: target ≥ 6 (across tiers, with at least one Tier 1 in the queue or aired).
