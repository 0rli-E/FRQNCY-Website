This is a guest-on-other-shows kit. The FRQNCY Podcast (the show Orlando hosts) has a separate distribution playbook.

## Brand-collision reminder

Per `audits/seo/MENTION-MONITORING.md`, "FRQNCY" is contested. **Always pitch as "FRQNCY Network" or "frqncy.network"** in subject lines, bios, and intros. Producers will otherwise confuse FRQNCY-the-network with FRQNCY Media (the Atlanta podcast studio) and reject for "audience mismatch."

Alongside the network, Orlando builds @frqncy-network/harness — the open-source LLM infrastructure that powers FRQNCY's agent surfaces.

**Three-paragraph:** [as above paragraph 1]

Orlando's central thesis is that curation depth — not virality, not engagement, not algorithm-chasing — is the durable moat in the post-2024 information environment. FRQNCY's editorial standards are explicit about this: every pick is personally vetted, sponsored content is not a category, and the membership model funds the free layer rather than gating it.

The harness layer underneath the network represents the same thesis applied to AI infrastructure: provider-indifferent, trace-first, never compacted. The open-source library lets any builder swap providers and keep ownership of their reasoning history. Orlando is exploring how curation networks and agent infrastructure compound when designed together.

## Sequencing recommendation

Don't pitch 30 at once. Suggested cadence:

- **Week 1:** pitch 5 from Tier 2-3 (warmer odds, lower stakes) to validate the kit and refine messaging
- **Week 2-4:** pitch the rest of Tier 2-3 + 5 from Tier 4
- **Week 5+:** pitch Tier 1 only after at least 2 Tier 2-3 appearances exist as social proof
- **Ongoing:** re-pitch any "cold-relationship-maintain" with a new angle every 6 months

Tier 1 hosts get pitched only after FRQNCY has at least one shipped episode appearance + ideally some press coverage. Cold-pitching Tim Ferriss with no audio out is low-percentage.

## What to do after each appearance

After airs:

1. **Update the tracker** with episode URL, air date, download count when known
2. **Add a backlink note** — does the show notes link to frqncy.network? Capture the exact URL
3. **Reciprocal share** — Orlando shares the episode on @0xOrli (or @frqncy_network if that exists by then)
4. **Capture the audio quote** — find the strongest sound bite and use it as a teaser for future pitches
5. **Watch Plausible referrers** in the 72 hours after airing — quantify the traffic spike per episode
6. **Add the host to the "people Orlando appeared with" list** — useful for warm-intro pitches to other shows the host has been on

## What NOT to do

- **Don't mass-blast the same email.** Each pitch references a specific episode and specific thread; otherwise it reads as templated and gets passed.
- **Don't pitch as just "FRQNCY"** — always "FRQNCY Network" given the brand collisions.
- **Don't pitch tier-1 first.** Build social proof in Tier 2-3 before going for the moonshots.
- **Don't promise content** that requires editorial work to back up. If pitching the curation thesis, make sure the standards page is live (it is — /editorial-standards/).
- **Don't ghost-follow-up** on rejections. A "thanks, not the right fit" deserves a "totally — appreciate the consideration" closer; that's how the relationship survives to be revisited later.
- **Don't optimize for downloads.** Optimize for the right audience match. A 5K-download episode in front of FRQNCY's actual audience compounds more than 100K downloads to people who aren't the target.
