# NEWSLETTER-PROSPECTS — Phase 5.9 newsletter exchanges

*Created: 2026-05-14. Status: v1, not-yet-contacted across the board.*

The goal of this document is to identify newsletters whose readers genuinely overlap with FRQNCY's. Outreach is one-by-one and personal — no swap mechanics, no paid placements, no mass blasts. Per `proposals/EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md` §2, if money ever flows from any of these relationships it gets logged as `revenue_relationship: contributor | partner` and disclosed on the relevant page.

The strongest first wave is the newsletters whose author **already appears in FRQNCY's library** (a `/people/`, `/orgs/`, or `/media/` entry that links to their work). Those are the warmest possible openers — Template C below is built for them.

Where 2026 status is uncertain, the entry is marked `[verify]`. Cross-references to Category E in `PARTNER-PROSPECTS.md` are noted; this doc deepens the newsletter-specific layer rather than duplicating.

Outreach mechanics: brand the network as "FRQNCY Network" or "frqncy.network" per `MENTION-MONITORING.md`, never just "FRQNCY" (collision with FRQNCY Media + others).
