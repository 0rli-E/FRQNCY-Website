## Pitching protocol

1. **Brand-handle first.** Subject lines and intros always say "FRQNCY Network" or "frqncy.network", never just "FRQNCY". Per `audits/seo/MENTION-MONITORING.md` — five other entities use the name.
2. **Real story, not press release.** Each of the three pitches in `PRESS-PITCHES.md` is a real angle the journalist can write. Don't dilute by adding "and also we have a podcast, and also..." — one pitch, one story.
3. **Verify [verify] before sending.** Two entries above need Orlando's confirmation of current outlet and beat before pitching.
4. **Track every send in `PRESS-TRACKER.md`.** Even non-responses are signal.
5. **Sequence Tier-1 last.** Per the same logic as the podcast outreach plan — build social proof in Substack-tier first, then go for NYT/Atlantic/Bloomberg with a "as covered by..." line.
6. **Don't pitch the same pitch to the same beat twice.** Pitch A goes to attention-economy writers. Pitch B goes to conscious-capital writers. Pitch C goes to AI-and-tech writers. Cross-pollination dilutes both.
