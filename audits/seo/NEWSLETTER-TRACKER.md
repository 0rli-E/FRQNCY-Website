## Operating cadence

- **Weekly review (Monday):** scan the tracker; bump anything that's been in `sent` for >14 days to `replied` (if a reply came) or note as `passed` (if no reply after 21 days and a single gentle follow-up).
- **Send no more than 3 outreach emails per week.** This is relationship work, not a campaign. Quality of opener > quantity of sends.
- **Log every send** to `audits/seo/runs/<YYYY-MM-DD>-newsletter-outreach.md` with the email body (or a link to the draft) and the template version used. Helps future agents see what's been tried.
- **When a reply lands:** capture the response (lightly redacted if private), update status, and decide the next move. Most replies don't lead to anything immediate; that's fine.
- **When a feature lands:** capture the mention URL, take a screenshot, log to `audits/seo/MENTION-MONITORING.md` so it becomes part of the baseline mentions record.

## What success looks like at 90 days

Per `PHASE-5-DISTRIBUTION.md` Task 5.9 verification: 10 prospects identified (over-shipped to 22), 3 conversations started, 1 exchange happens within 90 days.

Realistic 90-day target from the first wave (rows 1, 7, 16, 14, 6):

- 5 outreach emails sent in the first 14 days (template-matched per row)
- 2 replies expected (40% rate is generous for this kind of outreach; could be lower)
- 1 exchange-active by day 90 — a guest essay drafted, a reading-list mention live, or a podcast slot booked.

If zero replies land in the first 21 days, the issue isn't the prospects — it's the openers. Rewrite the first three.

## What failure looks like, and how to avoid it

- **Templated-feeling sends.** If the first paragraph could go to any of the 22 prospects unchanged, the email isn't ready. Rewrite the specific-issue line.
- **Bundled asks.** Template B explicitly says pick one of three options. A reader who sees all three at once reads "transactional opportunist". Pick one.
- **Premature follow-up.** A single gentle nudge after 14 days is the maximum. After that, leave the relationship alone — it'll either re-open later or it won't.
- **Bulk-sending Template C.** Template C is for writers already in the library. If FRQNCY hasn't actually picked their work editorially, do not send Template C — it would be dishonest.
