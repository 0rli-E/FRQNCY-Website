# Phase 5.8 — Publication Brief: "The reading list for network states"

*Companion to the essay draft at `2026-05-13-phase-5.8-reference-essay-reading-list-network-states.md`. This brief covers why the topic, where to publish, what schema, what OG, and the cadence for the rest of the Phase 5.8 calendar.*

---

## Topic chosen and why

**"The reading list for network states."** FRQNCY is uniquely shaped to win on it: the `/network-state/` topic page already carries the canon; matching `/people/`, `/books/`, `/orgs/` pages are live; Lugano is a real first node; the curation predates the essay rather than being assembled for it. No canonical synthesis of the field exists publicly — Balaji's own list is partial, Wikipedia is short, Substacks are episodic. That gap is where this lands.

Deferred candidates: "What is a topic graph" (MCP architecture still in flight); "Conscious capital primer" (topic page thinner — Q3 slot); "How to read more deeply" (weakest uniqueness — everyone writes it); "Regenerative agriculture primer" (`/regenerative-agriculture/` topic page does not yet exist).

## Where it should publish on FRQNCY

**Recommended URL:** `/essays/reading-list-network-states/`

FRQNCY has no `/essays/` route today. Phase 5.8 is the case to introduce one — the deep-essay format is structurally different from a topic page (an opinionated authored long-form, not a curated library). `/writing/` reads as personal/blog; `/essays/` reads as serial publication, which is what Phase 5.8 becomes with one entry per quarter.

Fallback if a new top-level route is too much friction: `/network-state/essay/reading-list/` nested under the existing topic page. Structurally weaker — the nested essay competes with the topic page for the same anchor authority.

Do NOT merge into the `/network-state/` topic page body. Topic page is curated library; essay is argument. Conflating damages both.

## Schema requirements

`Article` JSON-LD with: `headline`, `author` (Person with `@id` pointing to `/people/orlando/`), `publisher` (FRQNCY Organization), `datePublished`, `dateModified`, `mainEntityOfPage`, `image` (the OG card), `about` (Network state, Decentralised governance, Sovereign individual), and a `citation` array carrying the four canon books as `Book` entities. Pair with sitewide `BreadcrumbList` (Home > Essays > Reading list for network states) per Phase 2 pattern.

`dateModified` must update every time the essay is re-versioned. Non-negotiable for AI-engine re-crawl and Google freshness — the "annual re-versioning" pitch only works if schema reflects it.

## OG image

Typographic card, not a photo. The five canon titles set in Cormorant italic, stacked or spread, on the `/network-state/` palette (background `#0B1C3D`, accent `#3D7B7E`). No human figures, no city skylines, no Praxis-style coastal-utopia renders — the essay's voice is sober and the card should match. Library-card aesthetic, not vision-deck aesthetic.

Specs: 1200×630, generated to `/og/essays/reading-list-network-states.png`. Adapt the existing `og-image.svg` template; the topic-page OG generator pipeline handles it once `/essays/` is in its allowlist. Title in Cormorant 300 italic ~64px; subtitle "FRQNCY · essay · 2026" in Jost 400 caps ~18px with 0.34em letter-spacing, matching the homepage nav-logo.

## Internal cross-link recommendations

The essay carries 26 unique FRQNCY internal links — appropriate density (≈ one per 110 words). Cross-links from FRQNCY *back* to the essay are what compound:

1. **`/network-state/` topic page** — add an "Essays" section linking to the piece. Highest-leverage cross-link; converts the essay's anchor weight into pillar authority.
2. **`/books/the-network-state/`, `/books/the-sovereign-individual/`, `/books/the-dawn-of-everything/`, `/books/exit-voice-and-loyalty/`** — "Further reading" link on each.
3. **`/people/balaji-srinivasan/`, `/people/vitalik-buterin/`, `/people/david-graeber/`** — "Featured in" pointer.
4. **`/dao/` and `/cryptocurrency/` topic pages** — "Adjacent reading" link, lower priority.
5. **Homepage** — feature in the `featured` slot for 4-6 weeks post-publication if traffic justifies.

All cross-links must ship via the generator pipeline (per the MEMORY directive that direct injections into v2/topic pages get wiped), not by static HTML edit.

## Success criteria

**90-day:** indexed in GSC within 14 days; first impressions on "network state reading list / books on / where to start" within 60 days; cited by at least 2 of {Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini} on the relevant queries; 5+ FRQNCY cross-links live within 30 days of publication.

**12-month:** page-one ranking on at least one "[network state] [reading list / books / where to start]" query; 3+ external sites linking to the essay as their cited reading list; at least one long-form publication (Aeon, Noema, Palladium) citing it in passing.

If none of the above lands by 12 months, the failure is distribution rather than content. Re-prioritise Phase 5.4 podcast outreach and 5.9 newsletter exchanges before shipping another essay.
