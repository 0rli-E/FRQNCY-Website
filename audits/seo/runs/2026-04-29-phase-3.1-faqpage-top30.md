## Why this matters

FAQPage is the highest-leverage rich-result schema available to FRQNCY:

- **Featured snippets:** answer-engine-style cards on Google for long-tail queries
- **People Also Ask:** eligibility for the question-cluster widget
- **AI engine grounding:** ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude weight FAQPage heavily for grounding
- **Editorial signal:** the answers explicitly reference FRQNCY's editorial standards, reinforcing E-E-A-T

## What was NOT done (and why)

- The Q&A pairs are mechanically derived from existing page material — they're true and grounded but not editorially crafted around what readers actually ask. A future Sonnet-driven pass should refine them per topic with topic-specific reader questions.
- Topics 31-145 not included; the pattern is now proven and re-running with the same script across the remaining topics is straightforward.
- The visible <details> block is intentionally minimal (no JS, no theme overrides). It uses inline styles consistent with the FRQNCY chrome.
