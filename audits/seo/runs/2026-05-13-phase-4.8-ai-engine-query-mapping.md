## Key findings from the research

### Cross-engine confirmed signals (the levers that pay back)

- **FAQ schema** correlates with ~2.1× citation lift on Perplexity + ChatGPT browse and ~3.2× on Google AI Overviews. The schema doesn't fake the shape — it labels it. Google deprecated visual FAQ rich results on May 7 2026 but the schema itself remains useful for AI citation.
- **First-third positioning** — 44% of ChatGPT citations come from the first third of a page.
- **Domain authority** correlates strongly with ChatGPT citation (3.5× at >32K referring domains) but less so with Perplexity and Claude, which weight passage-level authority.
- **Page-load speed** — FCP <0.4s averages 6.7 ChatGPT citations vs 2.1 for >1.13s.

### Cross-engine confirmed snake oil

- **llms.txt has no measurable citation lift in 2026.** Multiple large-scale studies (ALLMO 94K URLs, SE Ranking 300K domains) find no statistically significant correlation. Google has stated AI Overviews don't use it. FRQNCY's llms.txt is fine to keep (cost was a half-day) but no further effort on it as a citation lever.
- **"Submit to AI search engines" services** — no such submission portal exists for ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini/Perplexity. Bing Webmaster Tools is the only one with a real AI Performance report (Feb 2026).
- **Generic E-E-A-T optimization without editorial substance** — the substance is the editorial standards page; surface signals without it won't ship.

### Per-engine 2026 state (one-line each)

- **Perplexity**: 3-layer ML reranker, Reddit-heavy (~47% of top citations), freshness-aggressive, FAQ-schema rewarded, has a Comet Plus publisher revenue-share program (Jan 2026) optimized for newsroom-scale publishers (FRQNCY not eligible this round).
- **ChatGPT search**: Bing-backed (~87% alignment with Bing top results), Wikipedia + Reddit dominate US citations (>25% combined), cites ~15% of retrieved pages.
- **Claude (claude.ai)**: FRQNCY's highest-fit engine. Avoids Reddit + YouTube + wire services. Leans NYT/Atlantic/New Yorker/Economist. Author authority weighted heavily.
- **Gemini / AI Overviews**: Multi-stage filter, Gemini 3 (Jan 2026) replaced 42% of cited domains — citation graph is re-shuffling. Knowledge Graph entity alignment is critical; FRQNCY needs Wikidata entry first.
- **Microsoft Copilot**: Bing-backed. AI Performance dashboard in Bing Webmaster Tools (Feb 9 2026) is the single best AI-citation analytics surface in 2026.
