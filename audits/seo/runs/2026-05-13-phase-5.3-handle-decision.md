# Phase 5.3 — Twitter / X canonical handle decision

Date: 2026-05-13
Status: **decision pending Orlando**

## TL;DR

The homepage Organization schema asserts `https://twitter.com/frqncy_network` as a sameAs URL. That handle is not verifiable in WebSearch (it is either unclaimed or dormant). Orlando's actual active handle is `@0xOrli` with display name "Orlando.FRQNCY". This is a soft inaccuracy in the schema that needs resolving before Phase 5.3 closes.

**Two clean paths.** Both work. **Recommendation: Path A — register `@frqncy_network` as the brand handle and keep `@0xOrli` as the founder handle.** The institutional / personal separation is worth the small extra effort. Consolidation (Path B) is acceptable but trades long-term clarity for short-term simplicity.

Sed commands for the schema update under each option are at the bottom of this brief — apply once Orlando picks.

---

## Why this decision matters

Per the Phase 5.10 baseline (`audits/seo/runs/2026-04-29-phase-5.10-baseline-mentions.md`), the schema has been claiming a handle that may not exist. Google's entity-resolution algorithm treats unresolved `sameAs` URLs as either a) the entity doesn't actually exist on that platform (small negative signal), or b) the entity is impersonated (medium negative signal, depending on what's on the linked URL). Either way it costs ranking points on the Knowledge Graph entry.

The decision sits inside the visibility plan's Days 1-30 task **"Confirm brand handles"** (`proposals/VISIBILITY-PLAN.md`). The plan flags this as a foundation move — none of the downstream visibility work compounds correctly until the canonical handles are nailed down.

The cost of being wrong is asymmetric: a wrong choice costs maybe a quarter of confusion before the second handle is registered or retired. A non-choice keeps the soft inaccuracy live indefinitely.

## Path A — Brand handle `@frqncy_network`, founder handle `@0xOrli` (RECOMMENDED)

The institutional voice and the personal voice live on different accounts. FRQNCY Network publishes drops, picks, and links from `@frqncy_network`; Orlando publishes founder-style threads, philosophical posts, and personal updates from `@0xOrli`.

### Why this is the recommended path

1. **Brand longevity.** FRQNCY Network outlives any single individual's relationship to it. The institutional handle survives leadership changes, sabbaticals, or whatever else. The founder handle is forever Orlando's.
2. **Voice separation already exists.** `@0xOrli` is already personal and exploratory ("Shining light in the darkness"; mixes crypto/Web3/spiritual content). Forcing it to also carry institutional weight (curated topic drops, FRQNCY-voice announcements) will dilute both audiences.
3. **Knowledge Graph clarity.** Google's entity-resolution algorithm reads two separate FRQNCY-related accounts as two entities (Organization + Person), which is correct and exactly what the homepage schema and the `/people/orlando/` schema already claim. Consolidating to one account confuses the algorithm — it has to decide whether `@0xOrli` is the Organization or the Person.
4. **Cross-promotion is cheap.** The brand account pins Orlando as founder; Orlando pins the brand. Two accounts amplify each other and double the reach without overlap.
5. **Recovery from impersonation is cheap.** If someone squats `@frqncy_network` later, the cost of recovery scales with how committed FRQNCY is to that handle. Registering now is the cheapest moment.
6. **The schema already assumes it.** Reverting the schema is more work than just registering the handle.

### Honest costs

- **Maintenance overhead.** Two accounts mean two posting schedules. The visibility plan calls for 3 posts/week on X — that's two cadences to keep going.
- **Audience dilution risk early on.** Until the brand account hits ~500 followers, posts there get less reach than the same post from Orlando's more-established account.
- **Mitigation:** post on the brand account FIRST, then quote-tweet from `@0xOrli` with founder context. The brand account compounds; Orlando's account amplifies. After 90 days the brand account stands on its own.

### Action items for Path A

1. **Register `@frqncy_network` on X today** (5 minutes). Use the bio drafted in `SAMEAS-MATRIX.md` §1.
2. **Update `@0xOrli` bio + link target** (10 minutes):
   - New bio: *Building FRQNCY Network — the topic graph for consciousness. 146 topics, 766+ vetted resources, editorial standards published.*
   - New link: `https://frqncy.network/people/orlando/` (not the substack)
3. **Update homepage `Organization` schema sameAs** to also include `@0xOrli` as a secondary reference (founder identity), and keep `@frqncy_network` as the primary.
4. **Update `/people/orlando/` Person schema** to point sameAs to `@0xOrli` (the real handle).
5. **Pin a tweet on `@frqncy_network`** linking to `/about` within 24h of claim.
6. **Cross-link both accounts** (founder mention on brand, brand mention on founder).
7. **Update Wikidata Entity 1** (FRQNCY) property P2002 to `frqncy_network`, and Wikidata Entity 3 (Orlando Eisenreich) property P2002 to `0xOrli`.

## Path B — Consolidate to `@0xOrli` (acceptable alternative)

Keep `@0xOrli` as the single FRQNCY surface on X. Update the display name and bio to make the FRQNCY connection unambiguous. The schema gets updated to point both Organization and Person sameAs at `@0xOrli`.

### Why someone might prefer this

1. **One cadence to maintain.** No second account to feed.
2. **Existing audience.** `@0xOrli` already has 1,772 followers and 4,818 follows — small but real network. A new `@frqncy_network` account starts at zero.
3. **Founder-led brands often work this way.** Patrick O'Shaughnessy → @patrick_oshag, Tom Morgan → @tomowhat, Paul Millerd → @p_millerd. The personal handle IS the brand, and that's coherent.

### Honest costs

1. **Knowledge Graph collision.** Both Organization and Person entities pointing at the same X URL forces Google to fuse them, which is wrong. FRQNCY Network is not Orlando Eisenreich; Orlando founded it.
2. **Schema accuracy regression.** The Organization sameAs is supposed to be the Organization's surface. Using a Person handle there is a misrepresentation Google does eventually notice.
3. **Future cost if FRQNCY ever has employees or other contributors.** The brand handle becomes hard to retrofit later — you can buy `@frqncy_network` cheap today, but if FRQNCY blows up over 12 months without a brand handle, you're stuck.
4. **Disambiguation cost.** Every podcast pitch, press email, and partner intro that wants "FRQNCY's Twitter" now has to qualify "Orlando's handle — he's the founder" each time.

### Action items for Path B

1. **Decline to register `@frqncy_network`** (or register it defensively and park it pointing at `@0xOrli`).
2. **Update `@0xOrli` to be the brand voice** (15 minutes):
   - Display name: "FRQNCY Network · Orlando" or just "FRQNCY · Orlando Eisenreich"
   - New bio: *FRQNCY Network — the topic graph for consciousness. 146 topics. 766+ vetted resources. Founded + curated by Orlando Eisenreich. frqncy.network*
   - Link: `https://frqncy.network/`
3. **Update homepage `Organization` schema sameAs** to point at `@0xOrli`.
4. **Update `/people/orlando/` Person schema** to also point at `@0xOrli` (same URL — acceptable here since they're now identical).

## Decision matrix

| Factor | Path A (separate) | Path B (consolidate) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand longevity | Strong | Weak |
| Voice clarity | Strong | Medium |
| Knowledge Graph entity separation | Correct | Confused |
| Schema accuracy | Strong (after registration) | Strong (after edit) |
| Cadence overhead | 2× | 1× |
| Reach at week 1 | Lower (new account) | Higher (existing audience) |
| Reach at week 12 | Equal or higher | Same |
| Recovery cost if wrong | Low (5 min to register) | Medium (rebrand + migrate followers) |
| Wikipedia / press disambiguation | Easy ("see @frqncy_network for the network, @0xOrli for the founder") | Harder ("@0xOrli is both") |

## Recommendation

**Pick Path A.** Cost is one 5-minute account registration and ~10 minutes of bio/link updates. The structural correctness compounds. The schema gets fixed cleanly. Orlando's personal voice keeps its exploratory range without dragging FRQNCY-the-network into it.

If Orlando's strong preference is a single voice (the Patrick O'Shaughnessy / Tom Morgan model), Path B is defensible — it just trades long-term clarity for short-term simplicity. Document the choice and move on.

The non-choice (leaving the schema asserting a vacant handle) is the only path that's actually bad. Pick within the week.
