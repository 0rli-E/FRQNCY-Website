## Why this matters

Phase 5 is the slowest of the five phases and the highest absolute ceiling. Podcast appearances compound:
- Each appearance is a backlink in show notes → SEO value
- Each is a brand mention to a high-trust audience → first-time discovery
- Each is audio that gets re-distributed (clips, transcripts, YouTube uploads) → durable presence
- Each is a relationship that opens doors to future appearances

The kit makes the work systematic. Without it, outreach is one-off and easy to drop. With it, sequencing is clear and the press kit is reusable.

## What this does NOT include (and why)

- **Actual outreach.** Sending the emails is Orlando's job — the kit is the substrate. Targeted outreach with personal references can't be ghost-written.
- **Real headshot / asset URLs.** Orlando supplies these; the kit flags where they go.
- **Tier 1 pitches yet.** Sequencing recommends pitching Tier 2-3 first to build proof, then Tier 1 with at least one aired episode as social currency. Don't burn cold capital on Tim Ferriss with no audio out.
