## Why this matters

- Explicit AI policy signals to bot operators that FRQNCY is intentional about AI crawling — well-behaved crawlers respect it; abusive ones get blocked at the WAF level (Cloudflare can enforce).
- Mirroring into robots.txt covers crawlers that don't yet check ai.txt.
- Referencing llms.txt from robots.txt is the de-facto discovery mechanism while llms.txt is still in proposed-spec status.
- Adding `/audits/` to disallow keeps strategy docs out of indexing — they shouldn't be public-search artifacts.
