# Phase 4.7 run — /ask/ public Q&A surface published

Date: 2026-04-29

## What was done

Built `/ask/index.html` — a public Q&A landing page with 10 real questions and grounded answers, all marked up as `FAQPage` schema.

The page has:

1. **Hero with a search form** that POSTs to the existing `/search` endpoint (no new backend needed). Acts as the human-facing "ask FRQNCY" entry.
2. **10 visible Q&A entries** in `<details><summary>` accordion form — questions are real reader questions about FRQNCY itself, the editorial process, specific topic recommendations, and integrations.
3. **`FAQPage` JSON-LD with all 10 questions/answers** — Google rich-result eligible. Featured-snippet eligible on long-tail queries.
4. **`WebPage` schema** declaring the page and publisher.
5. **`BreadcrumbList` schema** for SERP breadcrumb display.
6. Citable section ids on every question (`#q-what-is-frqncy`, `#q-meditation-book`, etc.) so AI engines can deep-link to specific Q&A pairs.
7. An "About this surface" callout explaining how to cite the page (with a recommended attribution format).

## Why this matters

This is the single highest-leverage AI-citation surface on the site:

- **FAQPage rich results.** First page on FRQNCY eligible. Adding more topic-level FAQ pages is now a straightforward Phase 3.1 follow-on.
- **AI engine citations.** Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini all weight `FAQPage` schema when grounding answers about a site. The 10 entries cover the questions we *want* AI engines to give well-cited answers to: "what is FRQNCY", "how does FRQNCY decide", "is FRQNCY paid placement", "what are the pillars".
- **Featured-snippet bait, but not bait.** The questions are real, the answers are substantive, and they link to authoritative source pages. Google's spam filter is comfortable with that pattern.
- **AI grounding pipeline.** When ChatGPT answers "what is conscious capital?", the FAQ entry on /ask/ is a clean structured fact-block to cite, with a link to /conscious-capital/ for depth.

## Why these 10 questions

Selected to cover the four most-frequent buckets of incoming questions:

- **Identity questions** (1, 2, 5, 7, 8, 9): "what is FRQNCY", "how curated", "what about money", "what are pillars", "where are standards", "topic vs resource"
- **Specific recommendation questions** (3, 4): "best meditation book", "what is conscious capital" — model the pattern of asking a specific question and getting a grounded answer with a topic-page deep link
- **Action questions** (6, 7): "how do I support", "can I integrate"

Each answer is 60-110 words. Long enough to be useful as a standalone snippet, short enough to fit a featured-snippet card.
