## Why this matters

- **Freshness becomes a function of cadence, not heroics.** Without a calendar, freshness depends on remembering to do it. With one, it's a recurring item.
- **The rubric prevents reward-hacking.** Setting `dateModified` without actually doing editorial work would damage trust over time. The rubric makes that explicit.
- **Capacity math validates the plan.** ~4 reviews/week = sustainable for the current network size. Scales naturally when the picks committee forms (~25 contributors, per editorial-standards/§3).
- **Topic-level dateModified affects rankings.** Google reads `dateModified` from Article schema (Phase 2.4 added this to all 174 topic pages). Quarterly bumps on fast-moving topics signal active maintenance.
