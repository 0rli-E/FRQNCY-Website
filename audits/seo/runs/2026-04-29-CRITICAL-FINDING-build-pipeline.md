# CRITICAL FINDING — Topic-page generator wipes static-HTML injections

Date: 2026-04-29 (discovered post-Session 4)

## Summary

The FRQNCY topic pages and item pages are **regenerated by build pipelines** that overwrite the static HTML on every run. My approach across Sessions 1-4 was to inject schema, bylines, FAQs, and anchored-topics blocks directly into the static HTML files. Those injections **did not survive** subsequent generator runs.

## The generator

`scripts/generate_topic_page.py` — 754 lines. Reads briefs from `data/topics/<slug>.yaml`, applies treatment overlays from `v2/_chrome/treatments/`, emits `v2/<slug>/index.html`.

It has two protection mechanisms my injections didn't use:

1. **`LOCKED_TOPICS` set** — slugs in this set are refused on regen. Used for hand-edited topic pages that shouldn't be touched.
2. **`<!-- BESPOKE:slot -->...<!-- /BESPOKE -->` markers** — content between these markers is preserved across regen.

There's almost certainly a similar generator for item pages (books / people / orgs / media / places) — maybe `generate.js` (2347 lines at root). Confirms but didn't deeply audit.

## Why this matters

**Every static-HTML SEO injection is fragile.** The next time someone runs the generator, my work is gone. Phase 2 (BreadcrumbList, Article, ItemList), Phase 3 (FAQ, byline, anchored-topics), Phase 4 (section anchors, WebPage hasPart) all need to be re-shipped *through* the generator, not around it.

This is the most important SEO finding from the day. The dashboard currently shows historical numbers that don't reflect reality.

## Three paths forward (in priority order)

### Path A — Add schema generation to the generator (recommended)

Modify `scripts/generate_topic_page.py` to emit:
- `Article` JSON-LD with author + datePublished + dateModified
- `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD
- `FAQPage` JSON-LD when the YAML brief includes a `faq:` block
- The visible byline element
- The anchored-topics block (cross-link to `data/topics/<slug>.yaml` `linking.topics[]`)
- `WebPage` hasPart with section anchors

Same for `generate.js` (or whatever creates item pages).

**Effort:** ~4-8 hours of careful editing, tests against a few topics, then `--all` regen.

**Why this is the right path:** the generator becomes the source of truth. Schema becomes part of the data pipeline. Future content edits keep the schema correct automatically.

### Path B — Use BESPOKE markers

Wrap each injection in `<!-- BESPOKE:schema -->...<!-- /BESPOKE -->` markers. The generator preserves these blocks across regen.

**Effort:** ~30 min to retrofit my existing scripts to emit BESPOKE-wrapped output.

**Why this is good but not best:** survives regen, but each piece of schema becomes per-page editorial content rather than system-generated. Drift becomes a risk over time.

### Path C — Add all touched topics to LOCKED_TOPICS

Brittle and brutal. The locked list grows unboundedly as more topics get touched. Future content updates require pulling from LOCKED_TOPICS, editing, and re-locking. **Don't do this.**
