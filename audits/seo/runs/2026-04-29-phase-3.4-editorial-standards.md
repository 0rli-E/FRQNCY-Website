## Why this matters

**E-E-A-T signal.** Editorial standards published in the open is the single biggest trust signal for YMYL-adjacent topics like consciousness, well-being, money, regenerative living — categories where Google looks for evidence the site has an editorial process, not just hot takes. Publishing this doc moves FRQNCY from "another curation site" to "a curation site with documented editorial values."

**Required for Phase 3.4 (bylines).** The Phase 3 plan calls for adding `Edited by Orlando · Standards: FRQNCY Editorial · Updated <date>` to every topic page. The link target needs to exist before bylines can ship. It now does.

**Knowledge graph signal.** The `Organization` schema on the homepage (Phase 2.5) declares `publishingPrinciples: https://frqncy.network/editorial-standards/`. That URL now resolves and serves real content. Google can confirm the claim.

**AI engine signal.** llms.txt references the page as a primary discovery surface. AI engines weight sites with public editorial standards higher when grounding answers — particularly on contested topics.
