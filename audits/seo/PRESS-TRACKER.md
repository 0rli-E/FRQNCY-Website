# FRQNCY Network — Press Outreach Tracker

**Purpose.** Live tracker for every press pitch sent, every response received, every landed piece. Companion to `PRESS-LIST.md` (targets) and `PRESS-PITCHES.md` (story angles).

**Brand-handle rule.** Per `audits/seo/MENTION-MONITORING.md`: every pitch goes out as "FRQNCY Network" or "frqncy.network", never as just "FRQNCY".

**Status legend.**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `not pitched` | Identified target, no pitch sent yet |
| `verify` | 2026 status / outlet unconfirmed — Orlando to confirm before sending |
| `pitched` | First pitch email sent |
| `responded` | Journalist replied (regardless of yes/no) |
| `interested` | Journalist asked for more / agreed to talk |
| `accepted` | Journalist committed to a piece or interview |
| `scheduled` | Interview / call booked |
| `published` | Landed piece is live |
| `passed` | Journalist declined |
| `cold-relationship-maintain` | No reply after 14 days + 1 follow-up; revisit in 6 months |

**Pitch-letter legend.** `A` = curation network, `B` = membership funds free layer, `C` = AI-citation as next link economy. See `PRESS-PITCHES.md`.

**Follow-up rule.** No reply after 14 days → one follow-up. No reply after another 14 days → status moves to `cold-relationship-maintain`. Revisit at 6 months with a new angle.

## When a piece lands

For every piece published that mentions or covers FRQNCY Network:

1. **Add the URL and date to this tracker.**
2. **Log the citation in `audits/seo/CITATION-TRACKER.md`** (per the Phase 5.1 dossier — hard / soft qualifying classification for the eventual Wikipedia notability case).
3. **Update the homepage Organization JSON-LD's `sameAs`** if the piece's outlet is a high-authority domain.
4. **Add the piece to `MENTION-LOG.md`** per the schema in `MENTION-MONITORING.md`.
5. **Thank the writer.** Personal email + a one-time X note. Never templated.
6. **Watch Plausible referrers in the 72-hour window** — capture the traffic spike for the metrics file.

---

## When a pitch is passed

A pass is not a closed door:

1. **Reply with a thank-you closer.** "Totally — appreciate the consideration. If anything in the FRQNCY Network space ever fits a future piece, you know where to find me." Don't ghost. Don't argue. That sentence is what keeps the relationship live.
2. **Update status to `cold-relationship-maintain`.**
3. **Note the reason in the tracker.** Patterns inform pitch refinement (e.g., if three writers all pass with "we already covered curation" — pivot to Pitch C; if three pass with "too small still" — wait for more traction).
4. **Revisit at 6 months** with a new angle. The same writer with a different pitch isn't repetition; it's relationship.
