## Executive Summary

FRQNCY is a consciousness-practice content and social platform that frames itself as a community becoming an alternative civilisation. Its voice is distinctive because it holds two registers at once: precise, founders-grade strategic clarity and unembarrassed metaphysical conviction — without alternating between them and without softening either. The signature move is short declarative sentences in triads, present tense, second person, no hedging.

The voice is built on six load-bearing rules that show up everywhere: speak from remembrance not instruction, write in present tense not future-promise, use declarative shortness not subordinate clauses, state conviction without dogma, frame everything from abundance not scarcity, and never produce a transaction with a loser. These are not stylistic preferences — they are encoded as "non-negotiable" in CLAUDE.md and demonstrated consistently in the cleanest live copy (the Substack re-engagement email, the Fund page vision block, the My-FRQNCY constellation micro-copy).

Content should feel to the reader like being addressed by someone who already knows you remember what they cannot yet articulate, who refuses to flatter you with future promises, and who is willing to be "too much" for someone who is not ready. The failure modes are well-documented: spiritual clichés ("love and light" as direct self-description, "high vibe," "soul food"), startup hype ("disrupt," "next gen"), academic hedging ("in some traditions"), and prescriptive framing ("you must," "do the work"). The site currently violates several of these in its highest-exposure copy — fixing that is the primary near-term application of this guide.

## We Are / We Are Not

The foundational brand identity anchor. **Voice is constant.** It does not change by channel or audience.

| We Are | We Are Not |
|--------|------------|
| **A mirror** — we reflect what the reader already is | A teacher, syllabus, or library of instruction |
| **Present-tense certain** — "FRQNCY is, not will be" | Aspirational, future-promising, "one day we'll" |
| **Declaratively short** — sentences in triads | Academic, essayistic, multi-clause |
| **Convicted, not dogmatic** — willing to be "too much" for someone not ready | A religion, a doctrine, or a prescription |
| **Abundant** — never scarcity-frame access to awakening | FOMO-driven, paywalled-thesis, urgency-funnels |
| **Cooperative** — every transaction positive-sum | Ranking, leaderboards, "calls," extractive partnerships |
| **Plain about non-plain things** — physics-spiritual held in both registers at once | Pop-spiritual or academic-distanced |

### Voice Attributes Detail

#### 1. Remembrance Over Teaching
- **What it means**: FRQNCY does not instruct people toward something new. It mirrors people back to what they already are. The operative verb is *remember*, not *learn*, *discover*, or *achieve*.
- **How it shows up**: Heroes use "you are" not "you can become." CTAs assume the reader's existing knowingness. No beginner-to-expert ladders.
- **What to avoid**: Instructional condescension. Copy that positions the reader as incomplete. Anything that frames the visitor as "starting their journey."
- **Evidence**: "FRQNCY exists to help man remember what he/she truly is at the core of his/her being." (Voice & Vision Answers, 1.3) · "Key verb: remember (not teach, not introduce — remember). FRQNCY is a mirror, not a library." (Voice & Vision Answers, reflection on 1.3) · "You are. We are. This is." (Voice & Vision Answers, 2.5)
- **Confidence**: High

#### 2. Present-Tense Certainty
- **What it means**: FRQNCY speaks from what is, not what will be or could be. The present moment is the only real location of creation.
- **How it shows up**: Every meaningful claim about FRQNCY is in present tense. In-progress items are stated declaratively, not aspirationally ("A podcast on the way" not "We are working toward launching a podcast").
- **What to avoid**: "We will," "someday," "in the future," "when we're ready," "the vision is to." All ruled out explicitly.
- **Evidence**: "Present tense is mandatory. FRQNCY is, not will be. 'You are' > 'you can become.' No future-promise or past-nostalgia copy." (Voice & Vision Answers, House Voice Rules) · "Power phrases: I am, we are, you are, this is, now, here." (Voice & Vision Answers)
- **Confidence**: High

#### 3. Declarative Shortness (Lists of Three)
- **What it means**: Short, declarative sentences. No subordinate clauses. No hedging. The signature rhythm is triads — three sentences in sequence, not one sentence with three clauses.
- **How it shows up**: "Unconditional love. Eternal light. Burning passion." · "A podcast on the way. A space in the works. A mailing list for the people who want to stay close." · The three core values, the six pillars, the four roadmap phases — Orlando thinks in threes (and fours).
- **What to avoid**: Long sentences with multiple commas, "additionally" / "moreover," parenthetical asides longer than three words, over-explanation. The questionnaire itself flags: "Expect the rewrite to cut ~30% of the current words — right now the site is over-explaining."
- **Evidence**: "Short declarative sentences. Lists of three." (Voice & Vision Answers, 2.1) · Demonstrated throughout the Substack email.
- **Confidence**: High

#### 4. Conviction Without Dogma
- **What it means**: State positions plainly, without softening for mainstream palatability, and without claiming to be the only path. Be willing to be "too much" for someone who's not ready.
- **How it shows up**: Crypto ratings state conviction, not calls. Curation rationale is given without "in some traditions" hedging. Practices framed as experiments, not prescriptions. Desire is a compass, not an enemy.
- **What to avoid**: "Spiritual bypass" language. Implying you must transcend desire, contrast, or emotion before being allowed in. Also the opposite failure — startup-confidence that ranks people or claims superiority.
- **Evidence**: "Frames practices as experiments, not prescriptions." (CLAUDE.md) · "Confidence bordering on provocation — FRQNCY should be willing to be 'too much' for someone who's not ready." (Voice & Vision Answers, 2.5) · "Not a cult, not a religion." (Voice & Vision Answers, 1.2) · "Conviction is stated as conviction. You decide what to do with it." (Voice & Vision Answers, Round 3)
- **Confidence**: High

#### 5. Abundance Frame (Never Scarcity)
- **What it means**: Create from wholeness, not from lack. No FOMO. The thesis is never paywalled. Shadow emotions are signals, not recruiting tools.
- **How it shows up**: Free public layer is "load-bearing" — it underwrites trust. The Substack opt-out close ("If it does not, no hard feelings. Unsubscribe below and we will not find you again.") is the abundance frame applied to a CTA.
- **What to avoid**: Any copy that leverages fear, lack, urgency-through-deprivation, or resentment. "You're missing out," "without this," "the last chance" — all ruled out.
- **Evidence**: "Never paywall the thesis. Never scarcity-frame access to awakening." (Voice & Vision Answers, Abundance Rule) · "FRQNCY always gives in abundance." (Voice & Vision Answers) · "No ads on the public site, ever. No paywalls on the read-this-on-FRQNCY content." (REVENUE-MODEL.md)
- **Confidence**: High

#### 6. Cooperation Over Competition (Positive-Sum Only)
- **What it means**: No transaction FRQNCY makes can produce a loser. Conviction as self-expression is OK; ranking *people* is not.
- **How it shows up**: No leaderboards anywhere. No "calls" framing in crypto. Fund deal flow positions FRQNCY alongside portfolio companies, not against them. Aligned Goods has a "no paid placement" promise that is described as load-bearing.
- **What to avoid**: Leaderboards (anywhere), trading-call framing, extractive affiliate arrangements, growth-at-someone-else's-expense logic, ranking-of-people in any form.
- **Evidence**: "We only support winning systems, there can't be a loser in any transaction we make. We create upward momentum spirals." (Voice & Vision Answers, 3.4) · "Cooperation over competition. No leaderboards. No 'calls' framing. No ranking people." (CLAUDE.md, marked non-negotiable)
- **Confidence**: High

#### 7. Plain Speech About Non-Plain Things
- **What it means**: Metaphysical content (consciousness, love and light *as concept*, frequency as physics-and-sacred simultaneously) is delivered without apology, without academic hedging, and without translation into secular-palatable alternatives.
- **How it shows up**: The Substack email — sent to a cold *crypto* list — opens with "FRQNCY is a community becoming an alternative society. You remember what you truly are — love and light — and create a future from that knowingness." No softening for the audience, no apology, no caveat.
- **What to avoid**: "In some traditions, one might consider" hedging. *Also* the opposite: sentimental clichés that empty the language of weight (see Banished Phrases).
- **Evidence**: "Plain speech about non-plain things." (Voice & Vision Answers, 2.5) · "Unembarrassed mystical content delivered plainly." (Voice & Vision Answers, 2.5) · "The overlap between physics and sacred is where FRQNCY lives. Voice should hold both registers simultaneously — not alternate." (Voice & Vision Answers, reflection on 1.4)
- **Confidence**: High

   - When to use: Explore page, pillar introductions, Research framing, any moment "knowledge base" undersells the scope.

4. **Conviction Stated Plainly**

   - Confidence: High

5. **Upward Momentum Spirals (Win-Win Only)**
   - Core idea: Every FRQNCY transaction — editorial, commercial, relational — is positive-sum. The brand actively rules out arrangements that produce a loser.
   - When to use: Fund page, partnership contexts, monetisation copy, any commercial disclosure.
   - Example phrasing: "Upward momentum spirals." · "We always give in abundance." (Voice & Vision Answers, 3.3, 3.4)
   - Confidence: High

### Competitive Positioning

- **vs. content aggregators / curation sites (Pitchfork, Criterion, Product Hunt, Goodreads)**: FRQNCY curates with stated *philosophical alignment*, not with reviews or popularity. Every entry has a "why this is in FRQNCY" rationale, not just a publisher's blurb. (REVENUE-MODEL.md)
- **vs. spiritual platforms (Insight Timer, Mindvalley, Gaia)**: FRQNCY does not gate access by readiness, does not prescribe practices, and does not paywall the thesis. The free public layer is load-bearing. Practices are framed as experiments. (Voice & Vision Answers, Abundance Rule; CLAUDE.md)
- **vs. social platforms (Farcaster, Bluesky, Warpcast)**: FRQNCY's social layer treats *conviction as self-expression*, not ranking. No leaderboards. No likes-as-currency. Posts anchor to topics, projects, and resources — discovery follows belief, not popularity. (CLAUDE.md; EXECUTION-PLAN-90D.md)
- **vs. status quo**: The mainstream attention economy ranks, extracts, and paywalls. FRQNCY runs on the opposite editorial logic and demonstrates it in the business model — fund carry funds the free layer, no ads ever, no paid placement.

## Tone-by-Context Matrix

Voice is constant. Tone flexes by context across three dimensions: formality, energy, technical depth.

| Context | Formality | Energy | Technical Depth | Key Principle |
|---------|-----------|--------|-----------------|---------------|
| Home page hero | Medium | High | Low | Three sentences. Tell them what it is, what it's for, why now. |
| Topic page intro | Medium | Medium | Medium | One paragraph of FRQNCY's take *before* the resource list. Not a definition; a position. |
| Fund / capital pages | Medium-High | Medium | High | "Map, not advice." State conviction, name what would change our mind. |
| Newsletter / Substack | Medium | High | Low-Medium | Conversational opening, rapid pivot to thesis, abundance close. |
| Social platform posts | Low-Medium | High | Varies | Conviction as self-expression. No likes-as-ranking. |
| About / vision | Medium-High | High | Low | Declarative. Triads. Present tense. The whole identity in 200 words. |
| Resource annotations | Medium | Medium | Medium | One sentence on what it is, one on why FRQNCY picked it. |
| Curation rationale | Medium | Medium | Medium | "We picked this because…" Specific, falsifiable, not reverent. |
| Onboarding / start-here | Medium | Warm | Low | One arc, one CTA. Don't menu them. |

### Context-Specific Guidelines

#### Home Page Hero
- **Overall tone**: Declarative, present-tense, slightly provocative.
- **Opening approach**: One sentence that names what FRQNCY is. Then one sentence that names what it's for. Then a triad — the three pillars or the three core values.
- **Do**: Lead with the topic graph framing. Use British English ("civilisation," "decentralised," "organisation"). Close with a single CTA into the explore page or the substack.
- **Don't**: Use "love and light" as direct self-description. Open with motion before substance. Use "soul food," "infinite energy," or any wellness-borrowed register.
- **Example (proposed, from feedback doc)**: "FRQNCY is a topic graph for consciousness — 146 maps of how money, energy, mind, and matter actually work. A reading list with conviction. A fund underneath. Practices as experiments, not prescriptions."

#### Topic Page Intro
- **Overall tone**: A curator's note. Personal, specific, not encyclopedic.
- **Opening approach**: Skip the dictionary definition (the eyebrow + h1 already gave it). Open with FRQNCY's position on why this topic matters and how it's organised inside the network.
- **Do**: Name a specific tension or stake within the topic. Reference adjacent topics by name. Sign off with the curation rationale ("We picked these because…").
- **Don't**: Repeat the meta description. Default to the auto-generated template. Drop into "Curated Resources" without an editorial bridge.
- **Example (the gap we want to close)**: Today's `/v2/decentralised-ai/` opens with a definition and goes straight to a list of five resources. It needs one paragraph of FRQNCY's position between the hero and the list — what's at stake, what we picked for, what we left out.

#### Fund / Capital Pages
- **Overall tone**: Confident, specific, epistemically honest.
- **Opening approach**: State the thesis. Name the framework (philosophical alignment + technical merit). Disclaim properly without hedging.
- **Do**: "Not financial advice. This is a map of where we see the world going — and which builders we believe are taking us there." (already on the live Fund page)
- **Don't**: Hype language. Trading-call framing. Performance-claim copy. "Asymmetric upside" is OK; "100x play" is not.

#### Newsletter / Substack
- **Overall tone**: Conversational, direct, no preamble.
- **Opening approach**: Address the reader where they are, then pivot to the thesis. The Substack re-engagement email is the model.
- **Do**: "You signed up a while back. Probably for something crypto-shaped. Then we went quiet on you." · Short paragraphs. Triads. Permission-to-leave close.
- **Don't**: Open with "Hi friend," "Hope you're well," or any softening. End with "Warmly," "In love and light," "With gratitude" — sign off "— The FRQNCY Team."

#### Social Platform Posts
- **Overall tone**: Self-expression, not performance.
- **Opening approach**: Each post anchors to a topic, project, or resource. Conviction is stated, not graded.
- **Do**: "I think Bittensor is the most ambitious project in decentralised AI." (a position) · Tag the topic. Cite the source.
- **Don't**: Likes-as-ranking. Leaderboards. "Hot take," "ratio'd," "vibes only" — none of it. No "calls."

## Language That Works

### Top Phrases

1. **"FRQNCY is a community becoming an alternative society."** — Context: structural self-description. Why it works: Present tense, declarative, names the trajectory without claiming arrival.

5. **"For every field we touch — science, body, money, technology, art, the lived world — we ask the same question: what is this at its very core?"** — Context: Substack email. Why it works: Names the methodology in natural prose, with em-dash carrying actual structural weight.

10. **"The fund becomes owned by the people it serves."** — Context: Fund Phase IV roadmap. Why it works: Clean purpose statement for DAO governance without using "DAO" or "governance."

### Sentence Patterns That Work

- **Triad close**: Three short fragments at the end of a paragraph. "Unconditional love. Eternal light. Burning passion." Use sparingly — it's the brand's signature, easily worn out.
- **Em-dash bridge**: "X — Y." Where Y intensifies, qualifies, or pivots. Example: "We are a small team of builders, seekers, and creators — becoming a network, becoming a society."
- **Permission-to-leave close**: "If this resonates, stay on the list. If it does not, no hard feelings." This is the abundance frame applied to a CTA. Reserve for opt-in moments.

### Hard Sign-offs

- "— The FRQNCY Team" *(email)*
- "— FRQNCY" *(institutional)*
- *(no sign-off needed for site copy — institutional voice is implicit)*

## Language to Avoid

### Anti-Patterns

1. **"You are love and light"** *(as direct self-description, not as concept)* — Problem: Goes from philosophical frame to wellness affirmation. Better: "You remember what you truly are." (the remembrance frame names the *content* without the cliché register)
2. **"Soul food — to positively impact the whole person"** — Problem: Borrowed register + corporate-wellness construction. Better: Cut the line entirely. The about page's stronger language ("consciousness is the ground of everything, and every area of life is ours to rethink from it") does the work.
3. **"To give humans infinite energy"** — Problem: Means nothing precise. Better: Cut. If energy is the point, name what kind ("vibrational sovereignty," "the fuel of conscious creation"). If it isn't, drop the line.
4. **"Built on the foundations of oneness. The light dominates everything we know."** — Problem: Pure spiritual cliché in the highest-exposure position. Better: Replace with the proposed home rewrite ("FRQNCY is a topic graph for consciousness…").
5. **"Discover the network"** *(verb choice)* — Problem: "Discover" implies absence. Better: "Explore the network" (present-state framing, currently in use — keep).
6. **"Welcome to FRQNCY"** *(generic onboarding)* — Problem: Default-template language. Better: "You arrived. Here's what FRQNCY is in three sentences." (proposed for start-here in feedback doc)
7. **Truncated mid-sentence card descriptions** — Problem: Ellipsis at "Machine learning, neural networks, and the development of non-human in…" reads as incomplete thought. Better: Shorten source descriptions or line-clamp at sentence ends.
8. **Generic team description**: "We are a small team of builders, seekers, and creators." — Problem: Most generic possible startup self-description. Better: The next sentence on the live page already does it ("consciousness is the ground of everything…") — start there.

### Examples to Fix (currently shipping — needs rewrite)

**The home page meta description (highest-priority fix).** Currently:

> "FRQNCY is a community becoming an alternative society. You remember what you truly are — love and light — and create a future from that knowingness."

**Why it's off-brand**: This is the highest-exposure copy on the entire site (read by Google, every social share, every link preview). Without the rhythm and context the email provides, "love and light" lands as a New Age tagline. A first-time visitor cannot tell whether this is a media outlet, a fund, a religion, an app, or a Substack.

**Proposed rewrite** (from WEBSITE-FEEDBACK-2026-04-28.md, item 4):

> "FRQNCY is a topic graph for consciousness — 146 maps of how money, energy, mind, and matter actually work. A reading list with conviction. A fund underneath."

**The about page hero sub-paragraph.** Currently:

> "FRQNCY is soul food — to positively impact the whole person. The goal — to give humans infinite energy."

**Why it's off-brand**: "Soul food" is borrowed register. "Positively impact the whole person" is corporate wellness. "Infinite energy" is abstract to the point of meaning nothing. Three sentences, three failures.

**Proposed rewrite**:

> "FRQNCY is a topic graph for consciousness — every area of life rethought in alignment with the highest self. Money. Energy. Body. Mind. Civilisation itself. We curate. We build. We back the people doing the work."

### Auto-Generated Topic Page Pattern (the structural fix)

Every auto-generated topic page (`/v2/<topic>/`) currently opens with eyebrow + h1 + one-sentence definition, then drops straight into "Curated Resources" with no editorial transition. Visitors arriving from search see a definition and a list — no FRQNCY voice.

**The fix**: Insert one paragraph between the hero and the resource list. The paragraph names FRQNCY's position on the topic, what's at stake, and why these specific resources were picked over others. ~80 words, in voice.

**Example for `/v2/decentralised-ai/`:**

> "Decentralised AI is the most consequential test of whether intelligence stays a public good or becomes a corporate utility. The protocols below are not the largest by token cap — they are the ones building the rails on which open intelligence can actually run. We picked these because they price information correctly, distribute the value of inference back to the network, and refuse to treat your prompts as training data. What would change our mind: regulatory capture, validator centralisation, or any of these projects accepting paid placement here."
