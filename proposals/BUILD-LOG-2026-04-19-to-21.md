## Voice notes that emerged during the iteration

A few rules of thumb that came out of revising these posts. Useful for any
future agent writing in Orli's voice.

- **Open in first person, not dev-log voice.** "Build log:" works once;
  "The last two hours on FRQNCY." or "Yesterday on FRQNCY." lands more
  human across repeats.
- **Lead with the headline ship, then a "why I'm building this / why I
  brought it in-house" beat.** Values come right after the what. This is
  the line that earns follows on LinkedIn — the values statement, not the
  feature list.
- **Close with a small-detail brag.** SCROLL centering. Merkle tree
  flipped bottom-up. The pattern is: list one thing the author is
  *disproportionately proud of* that nobody asked for. This is the
  signature move.
- **Concrete > abstract.** "395 principles" beats "lots of principles."
  "21 category sub-hubs" beats "the crypto section." Numbers earn trust
  on LinkedIn.
- **Length is OK if every paragraph earns its place.** Posts up to ~2,300
  characters land fine if there's substance per beat. The "see more" cut
  forces a strong opener; everything after it is reward for the engaged
  reader.
- **Avoid all banished phrases per `proposals/FRQNCY-VOICE-PLAYBOOK.md`
  and the cooperation-over-competition memory**: "calls" (for posts),
  "leaderboard," "signal over noise," "librarian" (the noun, not the
  building), "light-aligned," "movement," "thought leader," "peak
  performance," "authentic self," "makes the unable able."
- **Em-dashes are fine in moderation.** They match Orli's natural
  rhythm. Don't overuse.
