# FRQNCY Content Rights Playbook

> What we may legally read, clip, and reuse across the social network — verified 2026-07-17 against the Stanford Copyright Renewal Database and primary sources. Not legal advice; the mission-critical items deserve a $200 Copyright Office search report before building a flagship series on them.

## The rules in four lines

1. **Published before 1931 (US)** → public domain, full stop. Read it, film it, monetise it.
2. **US works 1931–1963** → PD *only if* the copyright wasn't renewed. Verify per title — never assume.
3. **Death doesn't free a work** — estates inherit and often enforce harder than the author did.
4. **A PD text ≠ PD recordings of it.** Someone else's audiobook of a PD book is still their copyright. Record your own (or use LibriVox).

## GREEN — safe to read/film today (verified)

**Pre-1931 classics (PD by term):**
As a Man Thinketh · The Science of Getting Rich · The Master Key System · In Tune with the Infinite · It Works! (1926) · The Secret of the Ages (1926) · The Richest Man in Babylon (**1926 text only**) · Acres of Diamonds · Creative Mind and Success (1919) · The Secret Teachings of All Ages (1928) · The Kybalion · Meditations · Tao Te Ching / Gita / Yoga Sutras (**old translations only**) · Hamlet · Flatland · Unto This Last · Etiquette (1922) · Extraordinary Popular Delusions · Scientific Advertising · The Go-Getter

**1931–1963, verified unrenewed → PD:**
- **Neville Goddard — all nine on our list** (At Your Command 1939 → The Law and the Promise 1961). Zero renewal records. *Not* Resurrection (1966) or any "revised" edition.
- **Think and Grow Rich — the 1937 text only.** Unrenewed. The 1960 revision IS copyrighted, and the Hill Foundation holds a live **trademark** on the title — don't brand a series with it.
- **Autobiography of a Yogi — 1946 first edition only.** Court-tested PD (SRF v. Ananda, 9th Cir. 2000). SRF's later editions are copyrighted.
- **Prosperity — Charles Fillmore (1936).** Unrenewed; Unity placed his work freely.

**Open-licensed people:**
- **Andreas Antonopoulos** — CC BY-SA on nearly all talks/books. Clip, remix, even monetise; credit him; derivatives inherit CC. Check the licence field per video.
- **Bitcoin & Ethereum whitepapers** — openly licensed.

**Free media pools:** LibriVox (PD audiobooks, usable in monetised video) · Prelinger/Internet Archive PD films (b-roll) · NASA media · Wikimedia Commons (per-file) · Pexels/Pixabay (free licence, monetised OK).

## RED — do not touch without written licence

- **The Magic of Believing (Bristol)** — renewed 1976; copyrighted to **2043**. The big PD myth on our list.
- Clason's **1955 revised** Richest Man in Babylon; Hill's **1960 revised** TAGR; Neville's **Resurrection**; SRF's current Autobiography editions.
- **Alan Watts audio** (monetised) — aggressive enforcement; official 50/50 revenue-share licensing program exists at alanwatts.org/licensing if we ever want it.
- **Terence McKenna** (Lux Natura permission required) · **Dolores Cannon / Ozark** (formal Content Use Application; they actively police — Instagram clips are unlicensed, not free) · **Sadhguru/Isha** · **Eckhart Tolle** · **Sai Maa** (all-rights-reserved + trademarks) · **Mike Maloney / Hidden Secrets of Money** (free to watch ≠ free to license; embed instead) · **Erik Voorhees conference talks** (owned by conference producers, not him).
- **Anyone else's audiobook/recording of a PD text** (except LibriVox).

## Reaction/commentary format (US fair-use posture, Wyoming entity)

Defensible: short excerpts (10–30s bursts), your voice ≥ ~70% of runtime, real analysis, viewer can't get the original's value from your short, credit + link. Not defensible: their clip running continuously with your face in the corner. Platform strikes land before legal outcomes — fresh accounts have no strike cushion.

Per-figure clip risk (done properly): Trudeau **green** (authorized — get it in writing) · Voorhees **green-ish** (ask; talks owned by conferences) · Maloney **yellow** (ask; affiliate-friendly) · Cannon/Sai Maa **yellow-red** (apply/request first) · Robbins **red** (keep out of the clip pipeline; teach the frameworks in your own words).

## The play

1. **"FRQNCY reads the canon"** — own-voice readings of the green list on books.frqncy / audios.frqncy; infinitely clippable, we own every second. Pair each with the Bookshop.org affiliate link.
2. **Antonopoulos clips** for crypto.frqncy — the one free win among living figures (CC BY-SA, attribute).
3. **Permission pipeline** — written confirmation from Trudeau; Ozark Content Use Application for Cannon; requests to Sai Maa org + GoldSilver; Watts program only if wanted.
4. **Everything else** — teach the ideas in our own words (ideas are not copyrightable; expression is), embed originals, never re-upload.
