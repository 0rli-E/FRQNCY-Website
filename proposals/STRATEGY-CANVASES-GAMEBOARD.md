# FRQNCY Strategy Canvases — the Question Game Board
> The reusable Q&A board for building sharp Personas, Empathy Maps, Value Proposition Canvases, a Business/Lean Canvas, and Customer Journeys — all interlocked. Built from 12 cited research streams (Revella 5 Rings, Cooper, HubSpot, JTBD, StoryBrand, Jung, Osterwalder/Strategyzer, Maurya Lean Canvas, NN/g journey mapping, Schwartz, AARRR, Hero's Journey). Created 2026-07-22. Companion: PERSONAS-AND-FUNNEL.md.

---

## HOW TO PLAY (read first)

### The interlock — five resolutions of one human
One nested telescope, fuzzy human → testable business. Each artifact feeds the next, and arrows run backwards too (each downstream artifact exposes gaps upstream).

**Empathy Map → Persona → VPC (customer profile) → VPC (value map) → Business/Lean Canvas → Journey Map.**

- **Empathy map** = raw observation (verbatim quotes, real behavior). Messy, evidence-first, one per real person.
- **Persona** = the summary. Several rhyming empathy maps collapse into one named archetype. Kills the "elastic user."
- **VPC customer profile** = the persona's needs made rankable: Jobs / Pains / Gains, ordered by importance. The deep, structured version of the persona.
- **VPC value map** = our offer (products, pain relievers, gain creators). "Fit" = lines from map → top-ranked profile items.
- **Business/Lean Canvas** = viability around the fit. VPC customer profile becomes a Customer Segment; value map becomes the Value Proposition.
- **Journey map** = the time dimension. Lays the persona across stages, plots emotion + touchpoints, and validates the whole thing against reality.

One line: **observe → summarize → structure & rank needs → design & fit an offer → make it viable → play it out in time.**

### The recommended sequence (don't build 30 canvases)
- **Round 0 — collapse channels (30 min).** Test each of the 6 channels: *same core goal + same primary pain = one persona.* money+success may be one seeker; some may merge. Do this BEFORE building anything.
- **Round 1 — one deep persona end-to-end.** Pick the best-evidenced channel. Run the full chain once (empathy → persona → ranked VPC → value map → light journey). This is slow and becomes the template + quality bar.
- **Round 2 — batch the rest, one LAYER at a time.** All empathy maps, then all personas, then all VPC profiles. Batching the same task beats stack-per-persona and surfaces duplicates. Time-box 20–30 min per persona per layer.
- **Round 3 — rank & gate.** Mark top-3 pains and top-3 gains per persona. Build value-map items only against those. Delete the rest.
- **Round 4 — ONE Business/Lean Canvas** for the whole network (multiple segments in one canvas), not six.
- **Round 5 — journeys only where they earn it.** Full journey for the 1–2 launch personas; a 5-stage skeleton for the rest.
- **Round 6 — riskiest-assumption pass (stop condition).** Tag every box `[F]` fact / `[H]` hypothesis; plot the `[H]`s on confidence × impact; test only the top 2–3. **Stop when** new evidence stops spawning new personas — then test, don't add boxes.

### Validation & hypothesis labels (ask of every box)
- Is this a **[F]act** (a real quote / metric / observed behavior — cite it) or a **[H]ypothesis** (inference)?
- If I'm wrong about this, does the idea collapse or is it cosmetic?
- Riskiest-assumption test: which assumption is **high-impact + low-confidence**? What's the cheapest experiment to *disprove* it this week?
- Am I testing **desirability** (do they want it?) or **feasibility** (can I build it?)? Test desirability first — it's riskier.

### Common mistakes to avoid
Guessed/fictional personas · too many personas (cap 3–5) · demographic-only personas · the elastic user (one persona for "everyone") · value props with no ranked pain · skipping the VPC layer (BMC value prop becomes a slogan) · canvases never tested · confirmation bias (leading questions, interviewing friends) · frozen documents · loving problem-solution fit while ignoring the business model.

# BANK 1 · PERSONA

### Identity (kept lean — scaffolding, not the building)
1. Name + one-line archetype that makes them real. Age, life stage, location, income band.
2. What are they responsible for day to day? What does a normal day look like — the tools, apps, rituals that fill it?
3. How did they get here — the career/life path that led to this moment?

### Goals & aspirations (the goal, not the task — Cooper)
4. What end-state do they actually want (distinct from the features/tasks to get there)?
5. What does success look like *in their own words*, this year?
6. Life goals, experience goals, end goals — how do they want to *feel*, not just what done?

### Identity & self-concept (StoryBrand from→to)
7. One honest word for them today — and the word they wish described them.
8. What identity are they quietly desperate to claim but don't feel entitled to? (creator, free, awake, whole)
9. "From ______ to ______" in one line.
10. What part of who they really are have they shrunk to succeed on someone else's terms?

### The internal problem (they buy the solution to this, not the external one)
11. Surface want vs. the feeling underneath it — what are they actually trying to escape?
12. The sentence that's true but they'd never post ("I've built a life I don't recognize").
13. The low-grade background emotion — restlessness, numbness, "is this it?", homesickness for a place they can't name.
14. What would it mean about them to admit they're unfulfilled despite "having it all"?

### The philosophical stake (StoryBrand / Sinek)
15. What do they believe is *wrong* about how the culture tells people to succeed?
16. "It's not right that a person can ______ and still feel ______."
17. Do they believe outer follows inner, or the reverse — and since when?

### Pains, frictions & the current alternative (JTBD)
18. Most frustrating part of their situation right now?
19. What have they tried but not cracked? What are they "hiring" today, and what do they hate about it?
20. What obstacles/fears/risks stand between them and the goal?

### Jobs-to-be-Done
21. "When ___ [situation], they want to ___ [motivation], so they can ___ [outcome]."
22. The functional, emotional, and social dimensions of the job.
23. What would make them "fire" the current solution? What pulls them back to doing nothing?

### Triggers (Revella's most underused ring)
24. What happened on the *day* they decided to look for a solution like this? Why now, not last year?
25. What event/pressure/life-change starts the clock?

### Buying & decision behavior
26. What do they do first to find solutions? Who else is involved in the decision?
27. What builds trust in a brand for them? What red flags make them walk?
28. Which 3–5 things matter most — and what do they *mean* by each (e.g. what does "real" mean to them)?

### Information habits & voice
29. Where do they go to learn about this? Which creators/podcasts/newsletters/communities do they trust?
30. What was the last piece of content that actually changed something for them?
31. The exact words, phrases, metaphors they use — and where they get emotionally worked up. (Mine verbatim.)

### Worldview, archetype & "home"
32. 3 values they'd refuse to trade even for more success.
33. Which archetypal energy — Seeker/Explorer, Creator, Sage, Magician, Innocent — and why?
34. When they say "home," is it a place, a state, people, or a relationship with themselves? Describe the felt sense.
35. Who are "their people," and do they have access to them now?

### What they'd never say out loud
36. The thought about their own life they've never told anyone.
37. What they secretly believe they're capable of that would sound arrogant to say.
38. What they're grieving but don't feel allowed to, because their life "looks good."
39. If they fully trusted they're a creator (inner first) — the first thing they'd stop tolerating, and the first thing they'd begin.

# BANK 2 · EMPATHY MAP
*Fill GOAL first, then the observable outside (See/Say/Do/Hear) before inferring the inside (Think & Feel). Outside = observed; inside = guessed — label guesses.*

### Goal / Who (center)
- Who exactly, named and specific? What life-moment brought them here?
- The one thing they need to DO or resolve or feel — stated observably.
- What does success feel like from inside their skin? What would make them feel "home" vs. "one more thing to keep up with"?

### See (their environment — not what we show them)
- What does their daily environment/feed look like? What are peers-a-step-ahead doing or having?
- What do they see in the spirituality/money marketplace that turns them off?
- What visible contradiction do they live inside (success outside, hollow within)?

### Hear (ask per source — different mouths, different messages)
- What are friends/partner saying about how they live? What do admired teachers/podcasters say?
- What does the culture/work say about "enough," ambition, rest?
- Whose voice do they trust when lost — and whose have they stopped believing? What are they NOT hearing anywhere that they wish someone said plainly?

### Think & Feel (inferred — the core insight lives here)
- What occupies their mind when the day goes quiet? What matters most that they'd rarely say?
- Pains: fears, frustrations, 3am anxieties. What are they afraid is true about themselves?
- Gains: what "depth"/"home" would feel like in the body — and what they'd change to get it.
- Where do stated beliefs and private feelings quietly disagree?

### Say & Do (observable — keep the two honest against each other)
- Verbatim quotes we've actually heard. What shifts between public and safe?
- Real routines, rituals, avoidances — what they open, skip, return to, abandon.
- Where does behavior contradict words? (That contradiction is treasure.)

*Facilitator note: the gap between Says and Thinks is the finding — then ask WHY they won't say it (unsure, ashamed, afraid). A sparse quadrant = go get data, don't invent.*

# BANK 3 · VALUE PROPOSITION CANVAS

## 3A — Customer Profile (rank everything)
### Customer Jobs (weight emotional + social for FRQNCY)
- Functional: what task/problem are they trying to complete?
- **Emotional:** what state do they want more of (peace, aliveness, self-trust) and less of (anxiety, numbness, emptiness)?
- **Social:** how do they want to be *perceived* through this? What identity would they hate to project ("woo-woo," "gullible," "midlife crisis")?
- The deeper job behind the surface request (not "listen to an audio" but "become someone who lives deliberately").
- Supporting jobs: as buyer (vetting whom to trust), co-creator (participating), transferrer (recommending).

### Pains (before / during / after — rate severity: extreme vs moderate)
- Before: what stops them even starting? (overwhelm, cynicism from being burned, "no time," don't know whom to trust)
- During: what frustrates them doing it? (feels like homework, no accountability, abstract/cliché, can't tell if it's working)
- After: what disappoints once "done"? (high fades, nothing changed, felt sold-to)
- Risks/fears: scam, manipulation, wasting years, looking foolish, judgment from a skeptical partner.
- Which pains are *extreme* (would pay to remove) vs. moderate (shrug)?

### Gains (required / expected / desired / unexpected — rate relevance)
- Required/expected: the minimum to be taken seriously (credibility, no manipulation, real depth, respect for their intelligence).
- Emotional gains to *sustain*, not feel once (grounded self-trust, peace, felt meaning).
- Social gains: to be seen and to see themselves as someone with depth/integrity/quiet authority.
- Desired: what they dream of but assume they can't have (lasting change, not another false start).
- Unexpected delighters: belonging, being genuinely known, a lineage/canon to grow into.

### Prioritization
- If they could solve only ONE job this year, which? Which pain would make them act *today*? Which single gain would make them tell a friend?
- Where do top jobs + severe pains + essential gains *cluster*? (Aim the value proposition there first.)

## 3B — Value Map + Fit
### Products & Services
- The full list by type: physical / digital (free audios, PD ebooks, app, Sanctuary) / financial (affiliate: GIN, Bookshop) / intangible (the curation, the lineage, the felt experience).
- Which item serves which segment's which job? (An item serving no named job = cut candidate.)
- Owned vs. borrowed (LibriVox, PD, affiliate) — does borrowed still let us deliver something distinctive, or is it commodity anyone could assemble?

### Pain Relievers (relief, not features)
- Which of the top-3 pains does each item remove, and *how* (mechanically)?
- Standard relief types: saves time/money/effort, better outcomes, less risk, less negative emotion, fewer mistakes, removes a barrier.
- Any pain reliever that's a claim with no mechanism? Which pains do we deliberately NOT relieve?

### Gain Creators
- Which top-ranked gains does each item produce (required/expected/desired/unexpected)?
- Emotional/identity gains: growth, belonging to a lineage, self-trust, agency (experiments not prescriptions).
- Any "gain" that's really just a restated feature ("146 topics" → "always find the next teaching that fits where you are")?

### Fit test
- Does each pain reliever connect to a *ranked* pain? Each gain creator to a *ranked* gain?
- Which value-map items map to nothing (cut/reframe)? Which top pains/gains have nothing pointed at them (dangerous blanks)?
- **Painkiller vs vitamin:** is each item something they'd act on *now* (severe pain / essential gain) or pleasant-but-postponable? Which FRQNCY offers are vitamins today — can any be reframed to a painkiller ("another meditation app" → "the one place you trust that won't sell you a scam")?
- Three fits: problem-solution (evidence they want it) → product-market (real traction) → business-model (profitable/scalable). Which do we actually have evidence for?

# BANK 4 · BUSINESS MODEL / LEAN CANVAS
*Recommendation for FRQNCY: start with **Lean Canvas** (early-stage, high uncertainty), keep BMC for later coordination/investors. Run one Lean Canvas per surface (content network / app / My FRQNCY / Sanctuary). Sketch the affiliate/teacher PARTNER side on a second mini-canvas — FRQNCY is genuinely multi-sided.*

### Lean Canvas additions (the risk-first boxes BMC drops)
- **Problem:** top 1–3 problems the audience actually has (in their words, not "they lack FRQNCY").
- **Existing alternatives:** what are they hiring today (YouTube gurus, Headspace, therapy, doomscroll)? True competition = who *they* think it is.
- **Solution (kept small):** the minimum offer that tests the problem before building the app/Sanctuary. What are we over-building because we love it?
- **Unique Value Proposition:** the one-line promise at the intersection of problem and solution.
- **Key metric:** the ONE number that says it's working (candidate: emails captured / weekly returning listeners).
- **Unfair advantage:** what can't be copied (founder story, the 146-topic curated graph, "free at the source" trust, network effects). Blank on day one is fine — what are we building toward?

### BMC nine blocks (fill segments + value props first; cost last)
1. **Customer Segments:** for whom; most important; mass/niche/segmented/multi-sided (audience + affiliate partners); who we're NOT serving.
2. **Value Propositions:** value per segment; which problems solved; source (newness, "getting the job done," design, brand/status, risk reduction, accessibility).
3. **Channels:** awareness → evaluation → purchase → delivery → after-sales; which work best/cheapest; are they integrated?
4. **Customer Relationships:** personal / self-serve / automated / community / co-creation; acquisition vs retention vs upsell; cost.
5. **Revenue Streams:** what value are they *really* willing to pay for; one-time vs recurring; pricing mechanism; each stream's contribution.
6. **Key Resources:** physical / intellectual / **human** / financial. (For FRQNCY the email list/audience IS the key resource.)
7. **Key Activities:** production / problem-solving / **platform-network**.
8. **Key Partnerships:** partners/suppliers; what resources/activities from them; motivation (optimization, risk, acquisition).
9. **Cost Structure:** biggest costs; cost-driven vs value-driven; fixed vs variable; economies of scale.

### Platform / multi-sided design
- Which side pays, which is subsidized (audience free; who ultimately pays)? Is the cross-subsidy stable and mission-aligned?
- The network effect: does it get more valuable to the audience as it grows (not just to us)?
- Bait & hook: the free audio is bait — what's the honest hook (email/app/membership)?
- Partner-side value prop: why would an affiliate/teacher want to be on FRQNCY vs. reaching the audience directly?
- Trust-as-inventory: every affiliate placement spends trust — what's the governance for how much we monetize per unit time? (No ranking of people.)

### Revenue-model mix
- Owned (email, app, membership, curated books) vs rented (IG payouts, a single affiliate like GIN) — reliability skews to owned.
- Mission filter: does each stream pass "free at the source / highest good"? What's the disclosure rule?
- Sequencing: what must be true (list size, engagement, a distinct member value prop) before launching membership, so we don't paywall the free teaching too early?
- Concentration risk: what share can any single partner/offer represent before it's a dependency? (A GIN-heavy mix is fragile.)

### Audience-as-asset & flywheel
- Managing to *asset value* (what the list is worth) as well as *revenue value* (what it earns now)?
- Attention → Trust → Transaction: which step is leaking?
- LTV of one subscriber; CAC:LTV discipline (~1:3); retention as the main LTV lever.
- Platform-independence: what % of audience is migrated to owned channels (email/app)? (The real health metric.)

### Key metrics (the instrument panel)
- Email capture/week (macro). · Keyword-comment rate/post. · DM→click-through (benchmark ~30–40%). · Funnel step conversions with named drop-offs. · Affiliate conversion + revenue per subscriber. · Membership-readiness signal (replies expressing willingness to pay).
- **Structural gap flagged by research:** strong free rung + distant high-ticket rung, but *no owned low-ticket or core rung between* — the biggest revenue question to answer.

# BANK 5 · CUSTOMER JOURNEY
*One actor, one scenario, one map. Current-state first (from research), future-state second — never blend. The emotion curve is the spine.*

### Map setup
- Business goal this map serves (diagnose a leak vs design new)? Current- or future-state? Who owns the fixes?
- Actor: whose POV (one persona)? Their life before FRQNCY, their guard/cynicism going in.
- Scenario + bookends: "discovers a reel → goes deep → becomes a long-term member." Channels in scope (reels, 146 topic pages, app, email).

### Per stage, run every swimlane: Actions · Thoughts · Emotions · Touchpoints · Pains · Opportunities
**1. Awareness/Discovery** — first 3 seconds: curiosity vs suspicion? Do they finish/save/bounce (at what second)? First moment of trust we can earn? Make the teaching itself the hook.
**2. Consideration/Going deep** — how far into the topic graph? Where does depth become overwhelm? Does every teaching live on-site (external link = trust leak)? Shortest honest path to the "oh, this is real" aha.
**3. Decision/Joining** — the threshold: peak or dip? What reassurance at the moment of commitment? Make crossing feel like an arrival, not a purchase. No "call"/ranking framing.
**4. Onboarding/First practice** — first value moment, how fast? Post-decision dip / buyer's remorse — engineer an early high. Frame practices as experiments from the first one.
**5. Retention/Deepening** — what makes a practice sticky *without* gamifying or ranking? Mark growth as self-expression, not leaderboard. Catch the churn signal (unopened streak) before they're gone.
**6. Loyalty/Advocacy** — sharing as generosity/self-expression, never a referral bribe or competition. What turns a member into a steward/gardener? The moment that makes someone say "you have to see this."

### Funnel view (AARRR) mapped to FRQNCY
- **Acquisition:** scroll-stop → watch → follow → comment-keyword. Metric: IG analytics + keyword-comment count.
- **Activation (aha = free audio):** DM → click → subsite → free audio → email opt-in. Metric: CreatorFlow + subsite + email opt-in rate. The free audio must over-deliver (the free rung).
- **Retention:** email nurture → app/My FRQNCY. Metric: open/click over the sequence, return sessions.
- **Referral:** IG shares/saves/forwards. On-brand = "a practice that helped me," never "beat your streak."
- **Revenue:** affiliate (GIN, Bookshop) → future core/high-ticket (Sanctuary/My FRQNCY). Metric: affiliate dashboards.

### The long transformation arc (months, not an afternoon — brand = guide, person = hero)
- **Ordinary world / Before:** the outer-successful, inner-hungry Tuesday-night ache; what they've already tried and filed as "not it."
- **Catalyst / Call:** the disruption (burnout, loss, "is this it?"); the one sentence that names their problem so precisely they feel *seen*.
- **Meeting the guide / Trust (load-bearing):** what must they feel to trust us as guide not guru? Empathy + authority without hierarchy. The smallest no-risk experience of real depth.
- **Threshold / First commitment:** the first unambiguous yes that feels like a beginning; the identity claim invited ("I'm someone doing the inner work"); the guaranteed early win.
- **Trials / Practice:** the weeks 3–6 plateau; making invisible inner progress *visible*; the welcome-not-shame re-entry when they ghost.
- **Transformation / Awakening:** the felt "remember what you are" moment — design conditions, don't promise; mark the peak (peak-end rule).
- **Integration / New identity:** the new identity they claim unprompted; rituals/milestones that make gradual change a dateable event; design so they need us *less*.
- **Return / Advocacy & belonging:** becoming a node because giving back is now who they are; peers not hierarchy; the ongoing ritual that deepens belonging for years.

### Moments that matter to design (peak-end rule — concentrate energy here)
The trust moment (first time seen not sold-to) · the threshold welcome · the awakening peak · milestone rituals · and *every ending* (end each session/cohort/cycle deliberately high — never trail off into silence). Because inner progress is invisible, engineer moments that reflect their own progress back to them (doubles as retention insurance).

# PART 2 · AUDIT ADDITIONS (from the 10-agent review, 2026-07-22)
> Everything below was added after a 10-agent audit. It supersedes gaps in Part 1. The companion file GAMEBOARD-AUDIT-RISKS.md holds the strategic red-team and the decisions that need Orlando, not just questions.

## Evidence first — the label upgrade + how to gather it pre-launch
FRQNCY is pre-launch, so almost every box starts as a guess. Label honestly with THREE states, not two:
- **[F]** = evidenced — a real quote / metric / observed behavior. Cite it inline: `[F: IG comment, money reel, 2026-07]` or `[F: DM interview, Rob-type, n=3]`.
- **[A]** = assumption we're acting on but haven't tested (plausible, untested).
- **[H]** = speculative inference.
Evidence hierarchy (rank every [F]): observed behavior (what they *did*) > their past-tense stories (what they *actually did last time*) > stated future intent (weakest) > our inference.

**Round 0.5 — gather evidence before Round 1** (cheapest first): (1) **comment & review mining** — scrape comments on the masters'/competitors' content the audience already watches + the 1–3★ reviews of the canon books; harvest verbatim into the empathy map. (2) **5–8 DM/voice interviews per persona** using the Mom Test (Bank 7); start with Rob (most reachable + highest intent). (3) **IG story polls** — good for *ranking* pains you already found, bad for discovering them. (4) **fake-door / waitlist smoke test** for the riskiest offer (the missing owned rung).

## HOW TO RUN THE Q&A GAME (facilitation)
- **Ask 3–5 questions per volley**, grouped by theme — never dump a whole bank. Reflect back one sentence + the sharpest verbatim phrase, then the next volley.
- **One bank per sitting** (~25–40 min, hard timer). Don't chain banks; the interlock reads better after the last settles.
- **"I don't know" → convert on the spot:** `[H] best guess — because <reason> · find out by <cheapest signal> · confidence low/med`. Force a provisional bet; attach the cheapest way to learn it; roll it onto the riskiest-assumption grid. The AI proposes, the founder confirms — the AI never invents to fill a box.
- **Verbatim over polish** — capture the ugly real sentence, don't upgrade it to marketing copy. If 3 boxes in a row come back thin, stop filling and design the experiment that gets the data.
- **Async team option (Miro):** one frame per bank; sticky colour = label (green [F] / yellow [A] / pink [H]); a wall of pink = go get evidence. Silent solo passes → dot-vote to rank → one 30–45 min Meet to resolve + pick the 2–3 riskiest assumptions. Telegram carries raw quotes (with source) between sessions; Drive holds frozen snapshots.

### Definition of Done (per canvas)
| Canvas | Done when… |
|---|---|
| Persona | ≥3 rhyming empathy maps collapse into it; every ring has ≥1 [F]; Trigger ring not blank; one verbatim voice sample; reads like *one* nameable person. |
| Empathy Map | Goal first; Say/Do (observed) + Think/Feel (inferred) populated; ≥1 Say-vs-Think gap named with a *why*; sparse quadrants tagged "NEED DATA", not invented. |
| VPC | Top-3 pains + top-3 gains ranked with evidence; every value-map item links to a *ranked* item; no dangerous blanks; each offer labeled painkiller/vitamin. |
| Lean/BMC | UVP = one testable sentence; the ONE key metric named; single riskiest assumption circled with a this-week test; the "no owned core rung" gap addressed or accepted. |
| Journey | One actor, one scenario, current-state from evidence; emotion curve drawn with ≥ the trust-moment + one dip; each stage has ≥1 opportunity tied to a real pain. |

## PRIORITIZATION RUBRIC (rank, don't list)
Score each Job/Pain/Gain 1–5 on two axes, multiply for a 1–25 **Priority Score**:
- **Severity** (how much it hurts / they'd pay to fix): 5 extreme → 1 shrug.
- **Frequency** (how often / how many share it): 5 daily-nearly-everyone → 1 rare-edge.
- **Priority = Severity × Frequency.** Sort descending. High×high = painkiller; low×low = vitamin (postponable).
- **Gains:** swap Frequency for **Reachability** (1 = they assume it's impossible/no one delivers → 5 = clearly deliverable). High-importance + low-reachability = a *desired* gain (high leverage, nobody's serving it).
- **Gate:** keep top-3 pains + top-3 gains; build value-map items only against survivors; a top-3 with no reliever = dangerous blank. Rough line: ≥12 worth building against; <6 is a vitamin. **No value proposition without a ranked pain behind it.**
- **Assumption Ledger** (a Sheet or Miro table): one row per [A]/[H] — `id · assumption · source box · confidence 1-5 · impact 1-5 · cheapest disproof · signal watched · result · verdict`. Round 6 = this sheet sorted by impact÷confidence. A test flips the box to [F] (cite the ledger id) or kills it.

## Per-bank quick corrections (apply while filling)
- **Persona:** collapse the three "interior" clusters (identity / internal problem / never-say-out-loud) into one lean section to cut duplication — reinvest the space in the new rings below.
- **Empathy Map:** it's the *6-part* map — give **Pain** and **Gain** their own bottom band (raw, verbatim, UNRANKED; the VPC does the ranking). One map per real person, never a composite.
- **VPC:** one profile per *segment* AND one value map per *monetised offer* (the free-audio map ≠ the affiliate/membership map — mapping only the free rung shows false "fit"). Keep **type** (required/expected/desired/unexpected) separate from **rank** (essential→nice). State the focus rule: name the pains/gains you will NOT address.
- **Journey:** split the single "Decision" stage into the **micro-yes** (comment keyword → open DM → tap link, inside Awareness) and the **email-yes** (opt-in) — the biggest volume drop is at the micro-yes. Give each stage an emotion word + a +/− so the curve is plottable. Build **current-state** (what happens today — mostly nothing/leaks) before future-state.

## New persona rings (add to BANK 1)
- **Spending power & WTP:** what have they *actually paid* for growth in 2 years, and what felt like a scam? What would they pay monthly/one-time for something trusted — where's the "let me think" number? Who controls the spend (partner/household gate)?
- **Media diet & platform behavior:** which platform, how many minutes, what time (night scroll?)? Listen/watch/read — and what makes them save vs finish vs bounce in 3 seconds? On resonance, what do they *do* (save silently, screenshot, send to one person)?
- **Tech/app fluency:** which practice apps are on their phone, which did they abandon and why? Eager or in subscription fatigue about one more app? (The "home" destination is an app.)
- **Money story:** what did they learn about money growing up, what belief still runs (scarce / dirty / freeing)? Guilt, pride, or emptiness about what they've achieved?
- **Spiritual biography & authority:** history with religion/spirituality/self-help — raised in it, left it, burned by a teacher? Drawn to lineage/masters or allergic to anything guru-shaped?
- **Consciousness & remembrance:** have they had the "I already knew this" recognition, or is FRQNCY the first mirror? Which masters would they already trust vs close the tab on? Do they believe consciousness creates reality? What would "for the highest good" mean *in their words*?
- **Anti-persona:** who *looks* like a fit but isn't (guru-worshipper, bypasser wanting comfort not change, $2k-funnel chaser, free-only tire-kicker)? What belief makes someone a bad fit *even if they'd pay*?
- **Signature quote:** one verbatim sentence that captures the whole person — pin it to the top.

## New empathy-map dimensions (add to BANK 2)
- **Influences — who they trust / wrote off:** the voices they'd take advice from unprompted; whose authority broke and why; what has to be present before they trust a new source.
- **Objections in their exact voice** (capture BEFORE the pitch): "here comes another guru," "if it's free what's the catch," "I've read all this before." Which objection is fair vs a wound from being sold to?
- **The moment of scroll:** physical setting + emotional state when the reel appears; what they watched just before; does the first 3s read as curiosity or suspicion; the smallest true thing that earns the next 5 seconds.

## New VPC checks (add to BANK 3)
- **Type-match / mismatch check:** each pain reliever removes a *pain* (not a disguised gain); each gain creator produces a *gain*. Are we over-indexed on aspiration (gain creators) while severe pains (scam-fear, no-time, burned-before) go unrelieved?
- **Message-market fit:** draft the value statement per segment — *"We help [persona] who want [top job] by [reliever], unlike [existing alternative]"* — and pressure-test the words against their verbatim + the FRQNCY voice.
- **Proof/evidence per reliever:** the mechanism AND the proof it works (Orlando-as-living-proof, the master's authority, "free at the source" as risk-reversal, a demonstrated early win). No mechanism + no proof = a slogan.
- **Existing-alternative delta:** for each top job, what are they hiring today? A reliever only counts if it beats the current alternative on a *ranked* pain.
- **Identity/spiritual gains** (usually outrank functional for the core persona): the *creator* identity ("live deliberately"), remembrance ("I'm not broken, I forgot — met as already whole"), lineage (a real canon to grow into), sovereignty (practices as experiments they run).
- **Highest-good fit filter:** any reliever that only works by amplifying fear, urgency, or inadequacy is cut — even if it converts. (No "vitamin→painkiller" reframes that manufacture urgency.)
- **Per-persona fit scorecard:** run the three-fits test per persona; expect Rob furthest on business-model fit, the depth-seeker furthest on problem-solution fit — name that asymmetry, it's the strategy.

## New BMC/Lean additions (add to BANK 4)
- **Unit economics:** actual commission per stream (GIN/KT %, payout lag; Bookshop ~10% on ~€15). Contribution per subscriber. Cost-per-email measured in **founder-hours**, not ad spend. The real ratio = hours-of-content ÷ euros-of-commission (business vs hobby).
- **Break-even / runway:** monthly cash burn vs the ~€100 budget; conversions/month to break even; the founder-energy runway before burnout.
- **Legal/compliance (was entirely missing):** FTC endorsement disclosure on every affiliate post (#ad / "I earn a commission"); no health "cures"/guarantees; no income claims (the exact theory Trudeau was prosecuted under); GIN due-diligence (lawful in EU+US? any pyramid/MLM structure?); a pre-written reputational-liability answer for "the convicted fraudster's affiliate."
- **Tax/entity:** Wyoming LLC vs EU entity (EU-based founders); EU VAT on future digital products; bookkeeping from euro one.
- **Pricing psychology (for the missing owned rung):** anchor/tiers; "founding member / lineage-belonging" frame, never false scarcity.
- **"What stays free forever" — a published contract:** which teachings are free at the source permanently, and what the paid layer buys (curation, container, belonging, tools — never the teaching).
- **Sustainability:** bus-factor = 1 (Orlando is content engine + living proof + trust layer) — what breaks if he's out a month? The Gardener/steward model as the labor-scaling answer.
- **Source-purity as the unfair advantage:** the truth-filter (real masters, real editions, credited) is the moat competitors can't fake — name it in Unfair Advantage.

## New journey additions (add to BANK 5)
- **Channel/device hand-offs (the cliffs):** IG → comment → DM → tapped link → external subsite → email → app. How many surfaces/taps between scroll-stop and free-audio-playing? Which hand-off loses the most — friction (login/new tab) or doubt (leaving the trusted feed)? Does the subsite feel like the same world?
- **Back-stage / service blueprint:** for each front-stage touchpoint, what's backstage (CreatorFlow automation, hosting, source-vetting, affiliate portal we don't control)? Which silent failure breaks the promise? Where is Orlando in the loop vs automation pretending to be human at a trust moment?
- **Name the churn cliffs (don't average):** keyword-comment cliff · off-platform bounce · opt-in cliff (asked before value?) · weeks 3–6 plateau · the monetization cliff (does trust survive the first affiliate ask?).
- **Re-engagement of lapsed members:** define "lapsed" per surface; welcome-not-shame re-entry as an actual triggered message; the last thing a fading member experiences — a gift, not silence or a sales ask; honor the dormant-but-reading member as a valid way to belong.
- **Cross-channel measurement:** can we trace one person across hand-offs (email = the identity key), or do platforms silo it? Baseline each cliff before "fixing" — tag [F]/[H].
- **Two clocks:** which touchpoints must deliver in one sitting (reel, free audio, first practice) vs pay off over months (perception shift, identity, becoming a node)? Does the cadence assume a rhythm their real life can keep, not a daily-streak that guarantees a shame-drop?
- **Remembering moment (not "awakening" as a status):** design the conditions for the lens-flip; never certify or rank who's had it. **Belonging without hierarchy:** home among peers, no ladder, no "more awakened" tier. **Orlando = the mirror, never the hierarch.**

# BANK 6 · JTBD SWITCH / FOUR-FORCES INTERVIEW
*The interview that generates the [F] quotes everything else needs. Reconstruct one real switch, in order, from a real person. Timeline first, forces second. Mine verbatim.*
- **First thought:** when did "there has to be more than this / I should look into this" first cross your mind — and what were you doing that day?
- **Push:** what was broken about the old way that made it stop being enough (numbness, another failed app, a guru who felt off)?
- **Pull:** what did you see/hear that made the new direction attractive — the exact promise that pulled you?
- **Anxiety:** what almost stopped you (scam, woo-woo, wasting more years, a skeptical partner)?
- **Habit:** what was comfortable about NOT changing — what kept you doomscrolling/busy instead?
- **The trigger day:** what specific event started the clock (burnout, loss, a birthday, a sentence someone said)? Why then, not a year earlier?

# BANK 7 · THE MOM TEST (interview hygiene — so evidence isn't confirmation bias)
- Am I asking about their *actual past behavior* ("walk me through the last time you tried to…") not a hypothetical ("would you use…")?
- Did I mention FRQNCY before they finished describing their own world? (If yes, contaminated.)
- What are they *currently spending* time/money/effort on — and what do they hate about it? (Real spend = real pain.)
- When they compliment the idea, what's the fact behind it — or is it politeness to discard?
- Commitment test: did they give time, a referral, or their email — or only kind words?
- Which of my questions were leading, and how would I rephrase so a stranger who disagreed would still answer honestly?

# BANK 8 · BLUE OCEAN VALUE CURVE (differentiation — the board's missing outward axis)
*Plot FRQNCY vs the field it's confused with: guru funnels, meditation apps, money-bro pages, generic spirituality accounts. Factors on X, how high each scores on Y.*
- What 6–10 factors does this market compete on (price, production polish, credentialed teacher, community, streaks/gamification, breadth, the $2k program)?
- **Eliminate:** what does every guru/app take for granted that FRQNCY drops (leaderboards, streaks, ranking, upsell funnels)?
- **Reduce:** what should we score *below* the field (paywalling, hype, personality-cult intensity)?
- **Raise:** what do we push *far above* (source purity, free-at-the-source, curation/truth-filter, "it already feels like home")?
- **Create:** what does no one offer that we introduce (a credited 146-topic canon; every teaching on-site; cooperation-not-competition)?
- Does our curve visibly *diverge* from the pack — or are we a slightly-nicer version of the same shape?

# BANK 9 · POSITIONING (Dunford — do this BEFORE the value curve; it sets which market judges us)
- If FRQNCY didn't exist, what would our person *actually* do instead (not "nothing" — YouTube gurus, Headspace, therapy, unread books)?
- What can FRQNCY truly do that those can't (credited real-master canon, unlimited legal free audio, one curated world)?
- What value does that unlock that they care about most — and who cares most intensely?
- What market **category** should we file under so the value is instantly obvious ("meditation app" vs "modern mystery school" vs "curated library")? Which frame makes us best-in-class, not also-ran?
- Is there a rising trend to ride (consciousness-mainstreaming, distrust of gurus, AI-era hunger for the real) without becoming a gimmick?
- One line: *for [person] who [situation], FRQNCY is the [category] that [unique value], unlike [alternative].* Does it survive a stranger reading it cold?
