Define it that way and the contradiction dissolves.

## The single riskiest assumption the whole business rests on
**That the "outer-successful, inner-hungry" depth-seeker — our core persona — will emotionally accept teachings routed through Kevin Trudeau and paid GIN affiliate links, rather than reading exactly that as the manipulation they came to FRQNCY to escape.** It's simultaneously the revenue engine and the audience most primed to reject it. If this persona bounces off Trudeau, the money model and the mission collapse in the same motion.

**Cheapest test this week:** two near-identical reels on one uncontested master (Yogananda or Neville), from one small test account. Version A ends with a Trudeau/GIN-adjacent CTA; Version B ends with a pure "teaching free at the source, join the email" CTA, no affiliate. Same audience/spend. Measure save/follow/positive-comment delta — and read the comments: is the skeptic showing up warm, or is anyone saying "wait, isn't this the Trudeau guy?" Cost: two reels and a weekend. It directly tests the one assumption everything stands on, before building seven channels around it.

## The nine weaknesses (red-team) + the fix for each
1. **Trudeau is a legal/reputational time bomb, and he's load-bearing.** Convicted fraudster (~7 yrs, $37M FTC judgment), named the "primary pillar" + "revenue engine" in the two most regulated categories (money + health). → *Fix:* get an FTC/affiliate-law opinion, write the exact disclaimer + income-disclosure language, and set a **maximum revenue share** Trudeau/GIN may ever represent, before any placement ships. If you can't calmly answer "what happens to FRQNCY the next time Trudeau is in the news," he can't be the pillar.
2. **"Source purity / only realised masters" collides with the canon itself.** A convicted infomercial marketer + channeled (Hicks) + past-life regression (Cannon) sit beside Yogananda. → *Fix:* name the **falsifiable** criterion for "genuinely realised." If nothing realistic could fail it, delete the purity claim — an unfalsifiable filter is branding that will be used against us.
3. **Affiliate-first vs "for the highest good."** When the revenue engine is a paid affiliate for a convicted fraudster's network, "highest good" risks becoming the wrapper on a lead-gen funnel — and the core persona is the one most likely to notice. → *Fix:* write the disclosure rule + trust budget now (max N affiliate placements per M reels, each labeled, each with a "why we recommend this" note). Would the core skeptic feel *served* or *sold*? If sold, it doesn't ship.
4. **The personas are archetypes with zero real evidence — the board's own rules forbid that.** Daniel/Mara/Rob/Jonas are introspection, none tagged [F]/[H]. → *Fix:* freeze the persona doc as **[H]**, run 5–8 real conversations (not friends) per archetype, fill ONE empathy map from real quotes. A persona doesn't exist until a real quote sits in its Say/Do quadrant.
5. **Six-to-seven channels for a solo founder with ~€100 and no audience is over-engineered.** → *Fix:* cap at **one channel for 90 days** — the highest purchase-intent, lowest-content-cost lane — and prove the full funnel (reel → email → one sale) before opening a second.
6. **Money + Success + Spirituality are probably not three people.** Success ≈ Spirituality (same human); Daniel (building wealth, lower purchase power) is the genuinely different one. The split is asserted, not tested. → *Fix:* three reels, one angle each, from ONE account; see which pulls the same people. Merge on evidence.
7. **The structural money gap is named then walked past:** strong free rung, distant high-ticket rung, **no owned thing to sell in between** — so the business model IS the affiliate dependency. → *Fix:* define ONE owned product (€19–49: a guided course from PD masters, a practice pack, a low-tier membership) and ship it before Sanctuary.
8. **Persuasion vs highest good.** The journey engineers "the sentence that makes them feel seen," "the awakening peak" — the exact machinery of the $2k funnel the persona is allergic to, pointed at producing conviction rather than testing it. → *Fix:* add a test to the "aha" — does it leave them *freer to walk away*, or manufacture conviction by bypassing skepticism? If the latter, it fails "for the highest good" by our own definition.
9. **"Home" is a placeholder the whole funnel terminates in.** The arc points at the app / My FRQNCY / Sanctuary, which don't exist yet. → *Fix:* don't run traffic to "HOME" until it's a real v1 (minimum: an email sequence + one web page). If it's nothing yet, the funnel currently ends at "email" — say so.

## Voice check — 5 rules to keep the canvas ANSWERS on-brand
1. **Write from remembrance, not deficit.** "He forgot he's a creator," never "he lacks/needs/is missing." No human positioned as incomplete.
2. **Present tense, no future-promise.** "She creates from plenty," not "she'll learn to."
3. **No ranking language, ever — including "awakened."** No level/score/tier/status on any person. Strike "awakened" as a persona attribute.
4. **Highest-good filter on every insight.** Before a pain/wound enters the value map: is it used to serve or to pressure? If it only works by amplifying fear/scarcity/inadequacy, it stays out. No urgency reframes.
5. **Source purity in the vocabulary.** Name real masters plainly; no spiritual clichés ("high vibe," "love and light" as label), no startup hype ("unlock," "level up," "disrupt"); British spelling; practices are experiments, not prescriptions.
