# LEFT SIDE — SUGGESTIONS (Value Map, built strictly from the post-its)

## Products & Services
1. **The Coach App (My FRQNCY)** — HD + Gene Keys + NLP personal coach: knows who you are, adapts to your communication style ("FRQNCY spricht mich an und passt sich an"), tells you your next step. *The identity + guidance engine.*
2. **The Progress Tracker** (inside the app) — visible, measured growth: goals, habits, rules you can actually keep, "better every day" reflected back. *Directly demanded by 3+ post-its.*
3. **The Filter / One Platform** — the curated canon (146 topics, vetted teachers, real editions): one place instead of 5 apps; says what to IGNORE, who's worth listening to, what to buy. *The trust layer.*
4. **Free teachings library** — audio-first, real masters, credited, free at the source. *The door + proof of authenticity.*
5. **The Community (membership)** — home-feeling, likeminded people, HD-based matching ("likeminded people treffen — Human Design etc."), be-yourself-without-judgement space. *The Required gain "community/home" productized.*
6. **Purpose→Income track** — courses + experts + coaching on finding what you truly love AND making a living from it (incl. KT/GIN + aligned offers as accelerators). *Answers "I need money" + "make a living of what they love."*
7. **Morning & evening practices** — designed rituals for the day's two hinges. *Answers "joy waking up / going to bed" and its dark twin.*
8. **(Parked) Community token with voting rights** — future unexpected delighter only; never a lead offer.

## Gain Creators (mapped to the gain categories)
- **Required:** one knowledge platform (information + knowledge) · community-as-home · time-saving through curation (efficiency).
- **Expected:** guidance (the coach) · "I know myself better" (HD/Gene Keys identity profile as an early deliverable) · likeminded matching · the relatable voice · vetted teachers that actually help · FRQNCY adapts to YOU (NLP-driven communication matching).
