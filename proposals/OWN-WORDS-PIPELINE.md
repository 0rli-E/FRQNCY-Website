# The Own-Words Pipeline — video → transcript → summary → your script → camera

> The legal engine: ideas are free, expression is protected. This pipeline extracts the ideas and rebuilds them in FRQNCY voice. ~45–60 min per source video once practiced.

## Step 1 — SOURCE (5 min)
Pick the source video (e.g. Hidden Secrets of Money Ep. 2). Note: creator name, video title, link — these go in your description for credit.
⚠️ You may download for personal reference/transcription. You will NOT reuse the footage, charts, or audio.

## Step 2 — TRANSCRIBE (5 min, automated)
Options (pick one):
- YouTube's own transcript: open video → ⋯ → "Show transcript" → copy-paste (free, instant)
- Riverside: upload the audio → export transcript
- Whisper via any free tool (tactiq.io, youtube-transcript.io) for cleaner text

## Step 3 — SUMMARISE (10 min, AI-assisted)
Paste transcript into Claude with this prompt (save it as a snippet):

"Summarise this transcript into: (1) the 3–7 core concepts/frameworks, each in one plain sentence; (2) the single most surprising claim; (3) two short quotable lines worth quoting verbatim WITH timestamps; (4) what the speaker gets right and where the argument is weakest. Do not preserve the speaker's sentence structures — state the ideas plainly."

## Step 4 — OWN-WORDS SCRIPT (15 min, the human step — this is where legality lives)
Write YOUR script from the summary, not the transcript. Rules:
- **Close the transcript. Never look at it while writing.** You rephrase ideas, not sentences.
- Open with credit: "This is my read of [Name]'s [work]."
- Structure: Hook → credit → the concepts in your words → YOUR take (agree/disagree/apply — the FRQNCY conviction) → practice/CTA
- Quotes: max 1–2 short lines, spoken as quotes ("as Maloney puts it, '…'")
- FRQNCY voice pass: present tense, declarative, triads, no hedging. Banished words stay banished.
- Health topics: supports/promotes phrasing only.

## Step 5 — DELIVER (10 min)
Two modes:
- **Camera mode (2x/week):** you read/deliver the script on camera → full pillar treatment
- **Voice-clone mode (dailies):** paste script into ElevenLabs → audio → CapCut over YOUR visuals (Pexels/NASA b-roll, your Canva charts, your hand sketching the framework) → narrated short

## Step 6 — VISUALS RULE
Never their footage/charts. Recreate: redraw any chart in Canva from the underlying data (data is uncopyrightable) — label "based on data from [source]".

## Step 7 — PUBLISH
Credit in description: "Ideas discussed from [Name]'s [Title] — watch the original: [link]". Keyword CTA in caption → CreatorFlow DM → site/email.

## The 4 legality checks before posting
1. Did I write from the summary, not the transcript? (no sentence echoes)
2. Are quotes ≤2 lines and credited?
3. Is every visual mine/licensed?
4. Does it read as MY teaching about their idea — not a substitute for watching them?

If all 4 yes → post. This works for Maloney, Robbins, Cannon, Sai Maa, anyone — alive or dead, aggressive or friendly.
