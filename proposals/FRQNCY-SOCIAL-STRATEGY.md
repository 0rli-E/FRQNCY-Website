## 5. Growth playbook (from the 10-agent sweep)

- **New-account mechanics 2026:** IG Trial Reels for cold testing (flops delete penalty-free); TikTok gives fresh accounts a cold-audience test on every post; YT Shorts seeds every video regardless of subs. Keyword captions > hashtags (3–5 max). 60–180s beats 15s on TikTok now.
- **Network rules:** never post the identical file across accounts (fingerprint detection = cluster shadowban) — vary edits/hooks per channel; **IG Collabs between own accounts are explicitly allowed** (one post, up to 5 profiles, pooled engagement) — the safe cross-promo mechanism. Clean masters only — watermarks cost up to ~40–70% reach.
- **Retention is the only god metric:** >60% completion triggers distribution; saves >3% and shares >2% are breakout signals; follows/1K views ~5–10 is healthy.
- **Benchmarks (plan on the power law):** day-90 median 300–800 followers/channel, day-180 median 1,000–2,500, good = 4–10K. ~70–80% of shorts land near median — track median, not average. **Kill rules:** nothing dies before 12–15 posts; kill a format after 6–8 straight sub-median posts with weak leading indicators; at day 180 demote channels <300 followers with no 3x-median hit.
- **Paid ($200/mo test budget):** Dollar-a-Day boosts only on organically-proven posts ($60), niche theme-page shares sub-100K with ≥3% ER ($60), one Spark Ad ($50), nano-creator seeding ($30). Skip Meta Verified and YT Promote. Never boost unproven creative.
- **Chat layers:** Telegram = community engine (folders, cross-promo swaps, 1–3 posts/day, ~90% open rates; pin "we never DM first" — crypto impersonation is the top attack). WhatsApp Channels = low-noise broadcast mirror (reach ≈ 25–60% of followers; push the share link from video CTAs).
- **Email is the owned layer:** stay Substack now (40% of new subs come from its network), re-evaluate beehiiv at ~2.5K. Capture via comment-keyword→DM automation (46% CTR vs <2% bio links); quiz lead magnet ("which practice fits you") converts 30–40%; weekly dispatch is growth-mode target.

## 9. Optimisation addendum — affiliate & faceless-network sweep (2026-07-17, 5 agents)

**The meta-lesson from every winner studied: one format moat + one distribution loop per channel — not five of each.** Wirecutter's moat is methodology ($1B+ GMV/yr); Project Farm's is own-footage testing (solo, ~3M subs, zero rights risk); Motiversity's is *licensing* speakers, not stealing them. The faceless pages that survive either license, transform heavily, or curate user submissions; bare reposting dies by strike or stays too small to matter. Our permission pipeline IS the professional version of this — validated.

**Applied to FRQNCY:**
- **Format moats (already ours):** own-words master summaries (read by Orlando or his voice clone), canon readings, "FRQNCY picks" single-best curation (the Wirecutter format matches our editorial DNA exactly — "the best of everything, curated").
- **Distribution loop (add day 1):** comment-to-DM automation (ManyChat). "Comment WISDOM" → auto-DM with the site/affiliate link: 12–18% CTR vs 2–3% for link-in-bio, 70–90% open rates. Affiliate links live in DMs and video-adjacent disclosures, not bios.
- **Revenue reweighting:** affiliate beats short-form ad revenue by 10–100× per view (Shorts RPM ≈ $0.01–1 vs affiliate $500–2K/mo at 50K followers/2% CTR). Ads are a byproduct; the funnel (view → comment → DM → site/email → affiliate) is the business. Long-form YouTube on money/crypto is the one ad-worthy surface (finance RPM to ~$28).
- **FTC sharpened:** disclosure must sit *in the video adjacent to the link moment* — "paid link" suffices; penalties $50,120/violation; applies to individuals, not just the LLC.
- **Two formats worth stealing outright:** (1) sub-$30 product demos for Aligned-Goods-adjacent content (TikTok Shop affiliate = 42% of a $15B GMV platform); (2) the torture-test/methodology video for tools-of-consciousness reviews — 100% own footage, zero rights exposure, inherently credible.

**The one-line strategy (v2):** *Two recordings a week, four rights engines, thirteen templated channels, one owned list, one DM funnel — format moat + distribution loop per channel, provenance as the brand, the site as the destination, the masters as partners not victims.*
