# Clipping Outreach Kit — direct permission deals with aligned creators
> The system: check campaign marketplaces weekly (contentrewards.com/discover, Whop feed — filter podcast/education/wellness), run Gadzhi's as baseline, and build 3–5 direct permission deals — one per FRQNCY subsector. Direct deals > marketplace for us because our niches are too specific for open campaigns. Only project/education crypto campaigns, never casino. 2026-07-26.

## The DM template (v1 — first touch)
Keep it under 100 words. Lead with what THEY get. FRQNCY voice: relatable, direct, zero hype.

```
Hey [Name] — I run FRQNCY, a network of pages curating the best consciousness/[their topic] content (IG: [handle], [X]K and growing).

Your [specific episode/video — name it] is exactly what our audience needs. I'd like to clip it into 3–5 shorts per month — full credit, tagged, linked to the original, and I'll send you everything for approval before posting.

You get distribution to a new audience, we get to share work we genuinely respect. No cost, no catch.

Open to it? Happy to send an example clip first so you can see the quality.
```

## Variant — per-view / paid deal (for creators who ask "what's in it for me beyond credit?")
```
Totally get it. Two options:
1) Free + credit: we clip, tag, and link every post; you approve everything first.
2) Small per-view deal: [€X per 10K views / flat €Y per month] for clipping rights to your catalog — same approval flow.
Either way you keep full veto. Which feels better?
```

## Follow-up (7 days, one time only — never chase twice)
```
Hey [Name] — floating this back up once. We made a sample clip from [episode] to show what it'd look like: [link]. If it's not for you, all good — we'll keep recommending your work anyway.
```
*(The last line is the FRQNCY posture and also the strongest close — abundance, no scarcity.)*

## Deal terms one-pager (what we always agree, in writing — a DM reply saying "yes" to these counts)
1. FRQNCY may clip [channel/catalog or named episodes] into short-form for IG/TikTok/YT Shorts.
2. Every clip: credit on-screen + in caption, tag, link to original.
3. Creator gets approval before first post (or approval of a style sample, then ongoing).
4. Either side can end it anytime; existing clips stay up unless creator asks.
5. No endorsement implied — we never present them as affiliated with FRQNCY.
6. [If paid: rate + reporting: we send monthly view screenshots.]

## Ops rhythm
- **Weekly (15 min):** scan contentrewards.com/discover + Whop for podcast/education/wellness campaigns; join anything that fits.
- **Weekly (30 min):** 2 new outreach DMs (from the target list) + 1 follow-up. Goal: 3–5 standing deals within 6 weeks, one per door.
- **Rule:** every clip passes the own-words/credit checks (OWN-WORDS-PIPELINE.md) and the compliance spine — health/money framing rules apply to clipped content too.
- **Tracking:** one row per creator in the registry sheet — status: researched / DM'd / follow-up / deal / declined.
