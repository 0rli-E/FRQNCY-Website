# Protocol Lessons — Farcaster, Lens, Ethos (April 2026)

## TL;DR

The most useful thing decentralized social is teaching us in April 2026 isn't about social graphs — it's about onboarding. Ethos Network is doing more for non-crypto-native UX with Privy than Farcaster ever did with Warpcast or Lens did with Family Wallet, and it's doing it on a smaller, sharper, more focused product surface. The single biggest takeaway for NRG is: **steal Ethos's auth flow (Privy + sponsored gas + invite tree), keep the social graph in Supabase as the pivot proposal recommends, and ignore the protocol-purity debate for another two quarters.** Identity portability is achievable cheaply via signed-message export; protocol federation is not the differentiator FRQNCY needs in 2026.

## Why this note exists

The companion document `proposals/NRG-ONCHAIN-PIVOT.md` (2026-04-29) is the decision framework — five definitions of "onchain," three credible architectures, and a sequenced recommendation. This note is the empirical complement: what the three most-watched protocols actually look like in the wild this month, what's failing, what's working, and what specific patterns NRG should copy or avoid. The pivot proposal answers *when*; this note answers *what to learn from each*. If the pivot proposal is the strategy, this note is the field-trip notebook.

## Farcaster in April 2026

Farcaster in April 2026 is the most architecturally interesting and the most strategically wounded of the three protocols. The protocol is real and well-built: FIDs are integers in a smart contract on Optimism, signed by a custody key (ECDSA via EIP-712), with delegated Ed25519 signer keys producing every cast and follow as a protobuf message replicated through Snapchain — the Rust-based, ~10K TPS data layer that hit mainnet in April 2025 and made independent hub operation realistically affordable (under $1K/month per BlockEden). FID registration costs ~$5–10 in ETH plus ~$7/year for one storage unit ([Farcaster docs](https://docs.farcaster.xyz/reference/contracts/), [BlockEden](https://blockeden.xyz/blog/2025/10/28/farcaster-in-2025-the-protocol-paradox/)). Frames v2 — now branded as Mini Apps — shipped in early 2025 and is the most genuinely 2026-feeling primitive in any of these protocols: full-screen embedded apps with persistent state, push notifications, wallet-aware transactions, and an in-feed app store that Warpcast surfaced in April 2025 ([Bankless on Frames v2](https://www.bankless.com/read/farcaster-frames-v2), [Dynamic.xyz](https://www.dynamic.xyz/blog/farcaster-mini-apps)).

Then the strategic part. On 2026-01-21, Merkle Manufactory sold the Farcaster client and Clanker to Neynar in a deal valued near $1B, and Merkle announced it would return ~$180M of unspent capital to investors ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/01/21/farcaster-founders-step-back-as-neynar-acquires-struggling-crypto-social-app), [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/386549/haun-backed-neynar-acquires-farcaster-after-founders-pivot-to-wallet-app)). On 2026-02-09, founders Dan Romero and Varun Srinivasan joined Tempo, Stripe and Paradigm's stablecoin payments project ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/02/09/farcaster-founders-join-stablecoin-startup-tempo-after-neynar-acquires-social-protocol), [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/389076/farcaster-founders-dan-romero-and-varun-srinivasan-join-stablecoin-startup-tempo)). Neynar — a developer-tooling company already running most of the ecosystem's hosted infrastructure — is now the steward, and its pitch is "open-source the client, simplify Snapchain operation, focus on developers." That pitch is honest but it is not a consumer-growth pitch.

The numbers explain why. Farcaster MAU peaked around 250K in late 2024, fell below 20K on the official client by late 2025, and DAU was around 34K on the day of the Neynar announcement, with revenue down 85% YoY ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/farcaster-billion-dollar-dreams-fade-080518572.html), [Phemex](https://phemex.com/news/article/farcaster-acquired-by-neynar-amid-strategic-realignment-55504)). Frames v2 was supposed to catalyze a 250K–500K DAU breakout in 2026; instead the realistic case is the niche-sustainability one — 60K–100K engaged users, profitable creator economy, no breakout. The founders calling time and walking with $180M to return is not a quiet vote of confidence.

What's working: the protocol itself, Frames v2 as a UX primitive, Warpcast's payment plumbing (Warps as in-client currency, ~$0.01/cast offset by reward mechanisms, channel creation at ~$25), and Snapchain making independent infra realistic. What's broken: the network is small and getting smaller, the cultural center of gravity is crypto-trading-and-memes rather than anything FRQNCY's audience cares about, the founders are gone, and the demographic mismatch with conscious-living-curious users is severe. **Lesson for NRG: build *for* Frames v2 as a secondary surface when it makes sense, but don't build *on* Farcaster as a primary network.** The Snapchain migration is technically complete — that's not the question. The question is whether Neynar can rebuild consumer momentum a developer-tools company isn't natively good at, and the honest 2026 answer is "probably not, and FRQNCY shouldn't bet on it."

## Lens Protocol in April 2026

Lens has the strongest engineering story and the weakest distribution story in the cohort. Lens v3 launched through 2024–25 with smart-contract-based Lens Accounts (account abstraction baked in), modular primitives (Accounts, Usernames, Graphs, Feeds, Groups), and a migration off Polygon onto **Lens Chain** — its own zkSync-based L2 with Avail DA underneath, GHO (Aave's stablecoin) as the gas token, and "sub-cent" fees per transaction ([Lens news](https://lens.xyz/news/migrating-the-lens-ecosystem-to-lens-chain), [Avail blog](https://blog.availproject.org/lens-chain-goes-live-scaling-socialfi-with-avail-and-zksync/), [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/nfts-and-web3/lens-chain-adopts-aave-s-gho-stablecoin-for-gas-fee-payments)). The Family Wallet acquisition (originally Avara/Aave, 2023) gave Lens the best embedded-wallet UX in any of the three: email or phone, no seed phrase, signless and gasless via account abstraction, single sign-on across all Lens clients ([Lens onboarding](https://onboarding.lens.xyz/)).

That's the win. The loss is the network and the governance. On 2026-01-20, Aave handed operational stewardship of Lens to **Mask Network** in what was carefully framed as "not an acquisition" — IP, treasuries, and governance stayed with Aave/Avara, but consumer execution moved to Mask, and Mask immediately acquired Orb (one of the biggest Lens-native clients) and started bringing Firefly under the same umbrella ([Lens news](https://lens.xyz/news/mask-network-to-steward-the-next-chapter-of-lens), [defi-planet](https://defi-planet.com/2026/01/mask-network-acquires-lens-protocol-stewardship-to-scale-socialfi-adoption/), [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/mask-network-takes-over-lens-protocol-as-web3-social-vision-falters)). The KuCoin headline — "Mask Network Takes Over Lens Protocol as Web3 Social Vision Falters" — is the ungenerous reading. The generous reading is that Aave is a DeFi company that built a social protocol it could never finish operationalizing, and handed it to a team whose job is consumer execution. Either way, Lens is now a third-party-stewarded protocol with a confused governance story, and that's a hard sell to founders making protocol bets.

User numbers reflect the confusion. Lens has ~650K total accounts, ~45K weekly actives, and DAU "in the tens of thousands" — meaning tens of thousands across **all** Lens clients combined ([CryptoAdventure 2026 review](https://cryptoadventure.com/lens-social-platforms-review-2026-lens-chain-user-owned-social-graph-and-top-apps/)). The client ecosystem is intentionally fragmented: Hey (Twitter-like, the de facto desktop client), Orb (mobile, now Mask-owned), Phaver (mobile, gamified, points-based, biggest mobile Lens app), Buttrfly, Kaira, Firefly. Fragmentation is sold as a feature ("multiple clients, one graph") but at this scale it functions as a liability — no client has the gravity to pull non-crypto users in, and the demographic is overwhelmingly DeFi-native.

What's working: the Lens Account UX (genuinely the best onboarding in any onchain social system right now), Family Wallet as the embedded-wallet pattern, the GHO-gas-fee idea (predictable USD-denominated fees), and XMTP's February 2026 collaboration to bring E2EE messaging to Lens/Orb/Firefly. What's broken: ecosystem fragmentation, post-Aave-spinoff governance ambiguity, and a network that has been "about to break out" since 2022 and hasn't. **Lesson for NRG: copy the Lens Account UX pattern (email/phone → embedded smart wallet → optional self-custody upgrade), but copy it via Privy or Coinbase Smart Wallet, not by building on Lens itself.** The smart-contract social graph is a beautiful artifact and the wrong abstraction for FRQNCY's cost structure — every follow is a transaction, and even at sub-cent gas, that's a constant tax on a low-volume product. The pivot proposal's reading still holds: signed-message export (Farcaster's model) beats smart-contract-record (Lens's model) for FRQNCY's scale.

## Ethos Network in April 2026

Ethos is the smallest of the three, the youngest (mainnet on Base, 2025-01-22), and the one most worth copying. Ethos is not a social network; it is an **onchain reputation primitive layered on top of Twitter/X**. Users review each other (thumbs up/down/neutral, with optional written review), vouch for each other (stake ETH on a profile — the vouch is itself a financial signal), invite each other into the network (with the inviter's score affected by the invitee's behavior), and slash each other (a 48-hour community vote with a deposit at risk for the accuser if the vote fails). All of this produces a Credibility Score that a Chrome extension overlays on Twitter handles ([Ethos whitepaper — Vouch](https://whitepaper.ethos.network/ethos-mechanisms/vouch), [Ethos blog — Four Mechanics](https://www.ethos.network/post/the-four-mechanics-of-onchain-credibility-review-vouch-slash-invite)).

The architecture: attestations live onchain on Base via Ethereum Attestation Service-style contracts, the scoring engine is offchain, and the social graph emerges from the review/vouch/invite primitives rather than being an explicit follow graph. The auth story is what separates Ethos from the other two: **Privy from day one**. Email or social login, Privy provisions an embedded smart wallet, gas is sponsored by the platform, and writing a review or upvoting a comment never asks the user to sign a transaction or hold ETH. Users can optionally bring an external wallet, but the default path is "log in with X, write a review, done" ([Ethos help — Connecting](https://help.ethos.network/en/articles/9765868-connecting-to-ethos), [Ethos Privy launch post](https://www.ethos.network/post/ethos-is-live-on-base-mainnet)).

Adoption is small — under 5K eligible users at the Contributor XP claim, growth from 100 → 500 in the first month — but the *velocity per user* is what matters: the June 2025 Humanity Protocol incident (a founder caught using someone else's face for "Proof of Humanity" verification) was surfaced and slashed by Ethos in real time, right before that project's TGE ([Observers](https://www.observers.com/reputation-is-not-static-in-can-be-shorted-on-ethos/)). That's a smaller network than Farcaster's worst week and it produced a more consequential output than Farcaster's best year. The lesson is that reputation primitives are useful at small N if the primitive is sharp.

What's working: the Privy onboarding (gold standard for non-crypto-native onboarding to a chain product), the deposit-based vouching mechanism (skin-in-the-game makes the signal real), the invite-tree growth model (an inviter's score depends on invitees' behavior — Reed's Law dynamics aligned with quality not just growth), and the Twitter-as-substrate decision (don't try to be a network; be a layer on the network people already use). What's broken: the network is small, the scoring math is inevitably gameable at small N, sybil resistance via invite-trees is only as strong as the seed set, and "speculate on people's reputation scores" via Ethos.Markets (LMSR-based AMM, January 2025) is a UX that will eventually produce a public-relations incident. **Lesson for NRG: the Ethos auth flow is the single most copyable thing in any of these protocols, and the *layer-on-top-of-existing-network* posture is more interesting strategically than "build a new social graph from scratch."**

## Decision matrix

| Axis | Farcaster | Lens v3 | Ethos | NRG (current) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Identity: who controls it | User custody key + delegated Ed25519, FID on Optimism | Smart-contract account on Lens Chain (AA-managed) | Privy-managed embedded wallet on Base, optional self-custody | Supabase row, email-based; no portable identifier |
| Social graph: where it lives | Signed messages in Snapchain hubs (offchain replicated) | Onchain records on Lens Chain | Attestations on Base + offchain scoring engine | Postgres tables in Supabase (RLS + realtime) |
| Auth UX: friction for non-crypto users | High (custody wallet, signer delegation) | Low (email → AA wallet, signless) | Lowest (Privy, sponsored gas, no wallet visible) | Low (email magic-link only) |
| Cost per user (signup) | ~$5–10 ETH + $7/yr storage | Effectively $0 (sponsored AA) | Effectively $0 (Privy + paymaster) | $0 |
| Cost per action (post / follow / review) | ~$0.01/cast in Warps; offchain follow free | Sub-cent on Lens Chain (GHO gas) | $0 to user (sponsored) | $0 |
| Network effect (where users are) | ~34K DAU, crypto-native, falling | ~tens-of-thousands DAU across all clients, DeFi-native | <5K active, sharp niche on X | NRG audience is the FRQNCY readership; not portable to any of these |
| Brand fit (cooperation > competition) | Weak (tipping/leaderboard culture) | Mixed (modular but DeFi-coded) | Mixed (review/score primitive is competitive by design; the *vouch* primitive is cooperation-coded) | Native (we own the surface) |
| Encryption story compatibility | Conflict (public-by-protocol, no DM Lexicon) | Improving (XMTP collab Feb 2026) | Orthogonal (not a messaging system) | Native (libsodium spec in this session) |
| Reversibility | Low (brand entanglement, public commitment) | Low (smart-contract migration cost) | High (Ethos is a *layer*, not a substrate) | Highest (we own the stack) |

## Concrete lessons for NRG

1. **Onboarding — copy Ethos exactly.** Privy auth (email/social → embedded smart wallet), platform-sponsored gas via paymaster, the wallet is invisible until the user wants it, optional self-custody upgrade for crypto-curious users. Lens's Family-Wallet flow is a close second but coupled to Lens infrastructure; Ethos's Privy implementation is the unbundled, copyable version. This is already in flight this session and is the right call. Stripe's June 2025 acquisition of Privy makes it more, not less, suitable — Bridge stablecoin rails are a future revenue surface.

2. **Social graph — Farcaster's signed-messages model beats Lens's smart-contract-records model for FRQNCY scale.** Lens v3 made every follow a transaction, then had to build an entire L2 (Lens Chain + Avail DA + GHO gas) to make the economics work; Farcaster kept identity onchain and let everything else be Ed25519-signed offchain records. The pivot proposal's hybrid (Supabase as truth, signed-message export to R2, Lexicon-compatible JSON) lands on the right side of this tradeoff. Don't put follows onchain. Identity-onchain plus signed-records-offchain is the affordance worth keeping.

3. **Reputation — keep it private.** Ethos is the most fully-realized public reputation system in 2026 and it has a structural problem: public scores plus ETH-backed vouching plus a speculation market (Ethos.Markets) creates a status race that runs counter to FRQNCY's cooperation-over-competition rule. The social research paper §0 already arrived at this conclusion; Ethos confirms it empirically. **NRG's reputation surface should be a private calibration view (Brier score, prediction track record), not a public credibility score.** The slashing primitive is interesting and worth borrowing as a *moderation* tool, not as an *identity* signal.

4. **Frames / mini-apps — yes, eventually, but not in v1.** Frames v2 is the most genuinely-2026 primitive in any of these protocols, and the Mini App pattern (full-screen embedded app, persistent state, wallet-aware, push) is going to migrate to other surfaces (Bluesky's custom feeds, iMessage, Telegram). NRG should support Frames v2 as a *target* — i.e., FRQNCY content should render as Frames when posted to Farcaster — but not as a *substrate*. Build the rendering adapter when the cross-post bridge ships, not before.

5. **Cross-posting — write-only bridges, not federation.** No mature multi-chain bridge exists in April 2026 (Bridgy Fed handles Bluesky↔Mastodon and is "considering" Farcaster). The right pattern for NRG is one-way write-only adapters: an `xyz.frqncy.post` record is the source of truth, and per-network adapters serialize it into ATProto's `app.bsky.feed.post`, Farcaster's protobuf cast format, and Lens's record schema as needed. **Don't read from those networks. Don't try to federate a unified inbox. Broadcast outward only.** The cost of bidirectional federation is wildly underestimated by everyone who hasn't built it.

6. **Account abstraction — pick Privy, not Coinbase Smart Wallet, for v1.** Both work; both are good. Privy wins on three axes for FRQNCY: (a) chain-agnostic — it'll run on Base, Optimism, Arbitrum, or whatever the Phase 3 protocol decision lands on — whereas Coinbase Smart Wallet's paymaster is Base-focused; (b) progressive onboarding (email/SMS/passkey/social → embedded wallet → optional self-custody) matches the FRQNCY persona's expected journey better than CDP's "everything-wallet" framing; (c) Stripe ownership post-June-2025 means future fiat onramp rails through Bridge without a vendor swap. Coinbase Smart Wallet is the right pick if you decide Base is the only chain you'll ever support; Privy is the right pick if you want the option to change your mind.

## What NRG should ship next

Building directly on the pivot proposal's sequencing:

1. **Privy auth integration** — already in flight this session. Email/social login, embedded smart wallet provisioned silently, platform-sponsored gas via paymaster on whichever chain Phase 3 lands on (Base is the default until a reason emerges to leave).
2. **`profiles.wallet_address` and `profiles.privy_did` columns on Supabase** — store the Privy DID as the stable identifier, the wallet address as the public-key-derived address, and the libsodium Ed25519 public key alongside (the messaging spec already requires this). One row, three identifiers, all forward-compatible.
3. **Hybrid signed-message export (Q4 2026)** — per pivot §6 Phase 2. Every post and follow is libsodium-signed and mirrored to a private R2 bucket using a Lexicon-compatible JSON shape (`xyz.frqncy.post`, `xyz.frqncy.follow`). Not federation; insurance.
4. **ATProto cross-post adapter (Q1 2027)** — the research paper's primary recommendation, sequenced after the messaging beds in. Write-only. Re-evaluate Farcaster as a secondary cross-post target only if FRQNCY's audience surprises us with crypto-skew.
5. **Defer onchain follow graph indefinitely.** Lens's failure to make it work at small scale and Farcaster's correct decision not to try are both data points. The hybrid export gives 90% of the benefit at 1% of the cost.

## Open questions for Orlando

1. **Privy vs. Coinbase Smart Wallet.** The lessons section recommends Privy. Is Stripe's ownership of Privy a positive (stablecoin rails, fiat onramp, enterprise reliability) or a negative (consolidation risk, tied to Stripe's roadmap) for FRQNCY's positioning?
2. **Reputation surface — private Brier vs. public-but-cooperation-coded.** Ethos's *vouching* primitive (ETH-backed, skin-in-the-game) is closer to FRQNCY's values than its *review* primitive. Worth borrowing? Or is any public reputation signal incompatible with the cooperation rule?
3. **Frames v2 adapter timing.** If we ship the ATProto cross-post in Q1 2027, do we add Farcaster Frames rendering at the same time, or wait until DAU on Farcaster recovers (or doesn't)?
4. **Invite-tree onboarding.** Ethos's invite mechanism has Reed's-Law-aligned incentives and decent sybil resistance. Does FRQNCY want an invite-only growth phase, or is the conscious-living audience too friction-averse for that?
5. **What's the FRQNCY user threshold for adding a second protocol surface?** Pivot §8 asked the wallet-adoption question; this is the federation-surface version. Do we add Bluesky cross-post at 1K NRG users, 5K, 10K?
6. **Is "FRQNCY as a layer on top of an existing network" (Ethos's posture) ever the right move?** Ethos's decision to be a *reputation layer over X* rather than a *new social network* might be the most strategically interesting choice in the cohort. NRG-as-a-Bluesky-layer is a credible alternative to NRG-as-a-destination. Worth a separate proposal if interesting.
