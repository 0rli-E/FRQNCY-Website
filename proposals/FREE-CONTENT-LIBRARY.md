## OPERATING RULES
1. Editions matter: 1926 Holmes not 1938 · 1937 Hill not 1960 · 1946 Yogananda not SRF's · 1975 ACIM not FIP's · 1963 Murphy not revisions · old translations not new.
2. PD text ≠ PD recording: use LibriVox or record yourself; never someone else's audiobook.
3. CC-BY = credit exactly as specified. CC BY-SA = your derivative inherits the licence. CC-NC = not for our monetised channels without a written grant.
4. Everything else: own-words pipeline (ideas are free) or permission pipeline.
