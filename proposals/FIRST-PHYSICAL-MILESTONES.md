# First Physical Milestones

*Plan for FRQNCY's first physical-world surfaces: retreat, events programme, space.*

*Updated: 2026-05-12. Status: planning, not yet committing capital.*

---

## The brief

FRQNCY is digital-first by design — a topic graph that doesn't need pavement to be useful. But the network only fully expresses itself when people gather. This doc sequences the first three physical milestones:

1. The first FRQNCY retreat (task #41)
2. The first FRQNCY events programme (task #86)
3. A semi-permanent FRQNCY space (task #72)

The order matters. Retreats teach us what the network's people actually want. Events let us iterate cadence and format. A space is justified only once both have been tested.

---

## Milestone 1 — The first FRQNCY retreat

### Format

5 days. 20–30 participants. One location, one full programme, one editorial through-line. Not a conference. Not a workshop. A retreat in the original sense — *withdrawal for the purpose of attention*.

### Through-line

"Coherence as practice." Mornings for breathwork and meditation, afternoons for talks and structured conversations, evenings for food and ambient music. The point isn't to load people with content; it's to give them a five-day stretch in a field of higher-frequency attention and let them notice what changes.

### Location candidates

| Place | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Essência, Aljezur, Portugal | Already on the network, sustainable infrastructure, low light pollution, ocean nearby | Capacity may be tight at 30; need to confirm full takeover |
| Costa Rica jungle retreat (TBD) | Year-round warm weather, plant-medicine traditions in walking distance | Logistically heavier for European participants |
| Bali (Ubud area) | Established retreat infrastructure, predictable food/water quality, strong yoga/meditation network | Saturated market — risk of feeling generic |
| Tulum or Sayulita, Mexico | Easy from US/Latam, good logistics | Costa Rica or Costa-Rica-adjacent likely cleaner alignment |
| Sedona, Arizona | Energetic significance, US-friendly, already maps to network (Sedona Method org entry) | Smaller venue pool, weather window narrower |

**Lean**: Essência for the first one. Already curated, already on the network. Use that to set the bar; expand the location set after #1 ships.

### Programme outline (draft)

- **Day 1** — Arrival, welcome dinner, opening circle. No content.
- **Day 2** — Morning practice → talk on "What FRQNCY is for". Afternoon: structured conversations in groups of 6. Evening: founder fireside.
- **Day 3** — Morning practice → talk on a *Research* pillar topic. Afternoon: networking time + free hours. Evening: music (live ambient or curated DJ set).
- **Day 4** — Morning practice → talk on a *Network State* pillar topic. Afternoon: peer-led sessions (anyone can hold one). Evening: shared meal + storytelling.
- **Day 5** — Morning practice → closing circle. Lunch. Departures from mid-afternoon.

### Speakers (to confirm, draft)

- One teacher from c-teachers (Shi Heng Yi or Tolle if reachable; otherwise a tier-2 alignment)
- One operator from the Network State / Charter Cities cluster (Próspera, Infinita, or NS)
- One scientist / researcher from the consciousness research lineage
- The FRQNCY founder (Orlando) for opening + closing

### Pricing model

Tiered. Three pricing tiers:
- **Standard**: covers cost + thin margin
- **Patron**: 2x — funds 1 scholarship per patron
- **Scholarship**: covers room/board for community members who can't pay

Goal: budget-balanced at half-full, profitable at 80%. The first retreat is allowed to break even.

### Open questions

- Do we run this through a partner LLC or directly through FRQNCY?
- Do attendees get a permanent membership (task #61 #82 — Ethos reputation layer pending)?
- Do we record? If yes, in what form? A discreet camera and a small audio archive feels right; a full multi-cam shoot doesn't.

## Milestone 2 — First FRQNCY events programme

### What this is

A curated series of *smaller* gatherings that don't require the retreat budget or commitment. Run on a quarterly cadence. Two formats:

**Format A — FRQNCY Salon** *(monthly, urban, ~15 people)*
A dinner + structured conversation. One host, one topic, one good restaurant. Cities to start: Lisbon, Berlin, New York, Singapore. Run by a host trained in the FRQNCY ethos (initially Orlando, scale via co-host model).

**Format B — FRQNCY Day** *(quarterly, ~80 people)*
A single day of talks, breakouts, and a closing dinner. Less programmed than the retreat, more programmed than the salon. Public ticketing, FRQNCY editorial bar applied to speakers.

### Programme rhythm (year 1)

- **Q1**: Two Lisbon salons + one Berlin salon. Validate format.
- **Q2**: First FRQNCY Day in Lisbon. First retreat (see Milestone 1).
- **Q3**: New York and Singapore salons added. Second FRQNCY Day in Berlin.
- **Q4**: Second retreat. Year-end FRQNCY Day in New York.

This is a rough draft, not a commitment. The cadence will move once we see what fills.

### Tooling

- Luma for ticketing (task #2 needs the embed installed)
- A `/events` page on the network with the live schedule, embedded calendar, and recap notes for past events
- Topic newsletter (task #56) handles invitations + reminders

## Milestone 3 — Semi-permanent FRQNCY space

### What this is

A physical *home* for FRQNCY. Not a retreat property — those rotate. Something more like a coworking-meets-clubhouse: a 200–400m² space in a single city where members can work, meet, host smaller gatherings, and physically anchor the network.

### Why not yet

A space is the most expensive commitment FRQNCY can make. Before signing a lease:

1. The retreat has to ship and prove the in-person editorial bar holds.
2. The salon format has to validate that there's monthly demand for FRQNCY gatherings in *at least* one city.
3. The membership programme (task #82 — Ethos integration) has to be live so members have a relationship with the network beyond ticket-buying.

Realistic earliest: Year 2.

### Candidate cities

| City | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Lisbon | Already a hub for the network state / longevity crowd, tax-friendly | Hot in summer, market is now crowded |
| Berlin | Cultural depth, talent density, good legacy of intentional space | Slower bureaucracy, weather impacts use |
| Singapore | Asia anchor, top-quality logistics, strong rule-of-law | Most expensive of the four |
| Austin | US anchor, Alpha School + Network School adjacent founders | Less aligned with European aesthetic |

**Lean** (pre-signal): Lisbon or Berlin. Decide after Milestone 1 and 2.

### Space programme

If we sign:
- A common room (for talks of up to ~40 people)
- 4–6 small meeting rooms (for salons, recordings, small group work)
- A library wall (the FRQNCY-pick books, physically)
- A small kitchen + dining nook
- Quiet zone for deep work
- A small studio for podcast / video recording
- *No open-plan desks*. Anti-WeWork by design.

### Funding

If it happens: half membership-funded, half FRQNCY treasury, no outside lease guarantees. The space is supposed to belong to the network, not to a landlord with leverage on us.

## What's not on this plan

- A retreat property purchase. We are not buying real estate this cycle.
- A FRQNCY franchise model. The retreats are flagship; no one runs a satellite without FRQNCY editorial approval.
- A FRQNCY-branded conference (the conference *format* is dead; we don't revive it).
