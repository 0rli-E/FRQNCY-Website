## Supplement decisions (Lessons 4, 9)

These two lessons aren't in your filmed batch but they're the two biggest gaps in any beginner crypto curriculum. Three options for each:

- **Lesson 4 — Seed Phrase Security.** Film your own (recommended), or set `youtube_id` to a Whiteboard Crypto / Andreas Antonopoulos video, or drop the lesson (not recommended — learners need it before they start transacting in lesson 5).
- **Lesson 9 — Common Scams.** Same deal. Film your own (recommended), embed a Coin Bureau or Whiteboard Crypto talk, or drop.

If you want to embed a public video instead of filming, just paste the YouTube ID into `courses.json` and re-run `node generate-courses.js` — credit the source in the lesson description.

## Upload order + cadence recommendation

If you upload all 11 at once, schedule the publish dates so they trickle out one a day for ~2 weeks. YouTube's algorithm rewards consistency over volume. Order strictly:

1. Day 1 — Set Up Brave (Lesson 1)
2. Day 2 — Open + Tour Kraken (Lesson 2)
3. Day 3 — Wallets (Lesson 3)
4. Day 4 — Send + Receive (Lesson 5)
5. Day 5 — Deposit + Withdraw (Lesson 6)
6. Day 6 — Defillama (Lesson 7)
7. Day 7 — Titan (Lesson 8)
8. Day 8 — Dexscreener (Lesson 10)
9. Day 9 — Coingecko (Lesson 11)
10. Day 10 — TradingView (Lesson 12)
11. Day 11 — Bungee (Lesson 14)

YouTube Studio → Schedule → set publish dates 24h apart at the same time of day (07:00 UTC catches Europe morning + US-east evening).

If you'd rather drop them all together as a series, that's also fine — just commit to one (binge-launch vs. paced) so the playlist tells a consistent story.
