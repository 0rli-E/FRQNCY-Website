## Handoff protocol

When you pick up an island:

1. Mark it in_progress (in your task tracker or a comment at the top of this doc).
2. Read the relevant cross-reference for that island's layer before starting.
3. Produce work in `proposals/` first (spec or decision doc), not in code, unless the to-do is explicitly an implementation item.
4. Surface blockers fast — Orlando would rather know in 5 minutes that something's stuck than in 5 days.
5. Update `proposals/FRQNCY-V1-ROADMAP.md` when you finish a unit of work.
6. Leave the next agent (human or model) a one-paragraph summary of where you got to.
