## 0. The one why (everything below serves this)

**FRQNCY exists so a person remembers what they are — consciousness, a creator — and then builds their dream life from that place.** Inner first. Outer follows. Remembering, then creating.

- **End goal for a human:** they are living their dream life — and their inner life moved to get there.
- **Who it's ultimately for:** a person roughly **30–45, already established** (career/business works, money isn't the crisis), who senses there is more and is **seeking depth**. When they land on FRQNCY they must feel one thing: *there's a whole world here — and it feels like home.* Not a page. A territory. A resonance at the level of vision and mission.
- **The emotional signature in the first 10 seconds:** depth + homecoming. "This is real, this is for the highest good, and it already knows me."

**The method signature (what makes our reels truth-carrying, not slop):**
1. **Source purity.** Only real masters, real editions, credited. Never paraphrase-of-a-paraphrase. The truth-filter *is* the brand.
2. **For the highest good.** Every piece is made in service of the person's real elevation — never to manipulate, never to farm outrage, never to sell something that doesn't serve them.
3. Supported by: teachings framed as **experiments to test** (not dogma to believe), the actual teaching **free at the source**, and **Orlando as living proof** threaded through the network.

**What is "home" (honest status — still being designed):** the destination is the **app / My FRQNCY**, which includes the **Sanctuary**. Email is the bridge to it. The exact shape of My FRQNCY as the home container is an open design question (see §8) — but the direction is set: the funnel ends inside FRQNCY's own world, not on someone else's platform.

## 2. money.frqncy — "the wealth-builder"

**Persona — "Daniel, 28–38."** Ambitious, employed or freelancing, building his first real resource base. Consumes money content at night. Doesn't yet have wealth — he's *creating* it. Wants principles, not get-rich-quick.

**What this channel actually is:** an education on money as a subject. What *is* money. What is good money and bad money. How wealth is created and kept. The timeless principles, told straight, one per reel, free at the source (Babylon, Hill 1937, Wattles). It is deliberately NOT the "you're rich and empty" channel (that's success.frqncy) and NOT crypto (that's crypto.frqncy).

- **Comes for:** clarity about money most people were never taught.
- **Stays for:** the feeling that wealth is learnable, lawful, and he's early — not late.
- **Free audio:** "The 4,000-year-old money rule" (Babylon).
- **6-month change:** he thinks from plenty, acts on principles, and it starts showing in his numbers.
- **Affiliate fit:** Trudeau success/money programs (framed), Bookshop money classics.
- **OPEN:** the precise end-state deserves more thought (see §8) — the working answer is "wealthy *and* whole," but money.frqncy carries mostly the "wealthy" half; success.frqncy carries "whole."

- **Note:** success.frqncy overlaps heavily with spirituality.frqncy and orlando.frqncy — those two also catch this person. Design it as a distinct surface only if it earns its own voice; otherwise it's a lens applied across spirituality + Orlando. (See §8.)

- **Primary pillars:** **Kevin Trudeau**, **Sai Maa**, **Paramahansa Yogananda**, **Sadhguru**.
- **Spill real truths (use with discernment):** Dolores Cannon, Abraham Hicks, Eckhart Tolle, Birgit Fischer, and the teachings inside the **Global Information Network (GIN)**.
- **The public-domain canon we already own** (foundation for daily volume): Neville Goddard, James Allen, Florence Shinn, Wallace Wattles, Thomas Troward, Ernest Holmes 1926, plus old-translation sacred texts.
- **The filter:** is this from a real, realised source? Is it for the highest good? If yes → it can carry a FRQNCY reel. If it's watered-down, derivative, or serves ego/sales over the person → it doesn't ship.
