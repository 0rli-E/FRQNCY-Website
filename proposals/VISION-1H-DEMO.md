## How to use this doc

When evaluating any new feature, ask: *does this move us closer to one of these states?* If not, it's noise.
When prioritising: pick the bullets that unlock the most others. (The site reading like a book unlocks evaluation; the harness self-optimising unlocks compounding work; the graph alive on its own unlocks scale.)
When stuck: pick the bullet that is closest to true and finish it.

Update this doc when reality moves. Strike-through items as they ship; add new bullets as they emerge. Keep it lean.
