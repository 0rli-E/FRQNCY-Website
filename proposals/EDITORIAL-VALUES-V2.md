# FRQNCY Editorial Values v2

The canonical reference for FRQNCY's slogans, voice, and editorial posture. When something on the site, in the app, or coming out of an agent feels off, this is the document to check it against. This supersedes the values block in `CLAUDE.md` for slogan-level decisions; `CLAUDE.md` remains the operational source of truth for repo conventions.

---

## 1. Slogans (canonical)

These are the lines we write toward. They aren't taglines to plaster everywhere — they're the editorial gravity that should pull every page in the same direction.

### "FRQNCY makes the unable able"

The promise. Practices, teachings, tools — laid out so a person who couldn't do the thing yesterday can do a version of it today. It frames FRQNCY as enabling infrastructure, not aspirational content. **Lands on:** homepage tagline rotation (primary), About page hero, footer of every page (small caps).

### "FRQNCY empowers the empowering"

The network logic. We platform people who already platform others — teachers, builders, practitioners whose work multiplies through them. It's how we choose what to feature without ranking anyone. **Lands on:** About page (mission section), Sanctuary surface (when it ships), the explore-page intro paragraph.

### "Crypto is freedom technology"

Topic-specific. Used on `/v2/crypto/` as the lead-in to the Bitcoin values block (borderlessness, immutability, censorship resistance, permissionless, scarcity, transparency). Not deployed elsewhere — it's a frame for the crypto pillar, not a global slogan. **Lands on:** `/v2/crypto/` hero, the money topic page (as a callback), and any crypto-adjacent topic intro.

## 2. Inherited values (from `CLAUDE.md`)

Restated here so this doc stands alone:

- **Cooperation over competition.** No leaderboards. No "calls" framing. Conviction as self-expression is welcome; ranking *people* is not. Winning systems are cooperative systems.
- **Every teaching lives on the site.** External links are footnotes, not destinations. Profiles, books, practices should be readable here, with the site as the canonical home over time.
- **Conscious about consciousness.** Practices are framed as experiments, not prescriptions. No spiritual cliches. The reader stays the agent.
- **Default "add" target is `/v2/explore.html`.** When in doubt, place new entries on the explore graph where they fit thematically.

## 3. New distinction: spiritual technology vs spiritual materialism

Borrowed from Chögyam Trungpa (*Cutting Through Spiritual Materialism*, 1973), and worth surfacing across the site.

**Spiritual technology** is practice that works on the practitioner — meditation, breath, fasting, attention training, ritual. The instrument and the object are the same person. Progress shows up as less reactivity, less self-deception, more capacity. You can't display it; you can only embody it.

**Spiritual materialism** is the trap of using practice to reinforce the ego it was meant to dissolve — collecting teachers, traditions, certifications, retreats, and inner experiences as accessories. The practice becomes another thing to *have* rather than a way to *become*.

FRQNCY positions on the spiritual-technology side. Topics are presented as instruments. Teachers are referenced for what their practices *do*, not for credentials or follower counts. We resist the urge to treat depth as a status signal.

This distinction also gives us editorial cover: when a contribution drifts toward credential-flexing or hierarchy, "this leans spiritual-materialist" is a cleaner critique than "this feels off."

**Lands on:** the philosophy / consciousness pillar pages (as a sidebar or end-of-page note), and as the editorial filter for any teacher / lineage profile we add.

## 4. How to present values without preaching

The fastest way to violate FRQNCY's voice is to *announce* its voice. A few rules of thumb:

- **Show through structure, not statements.** The absence of a leaderboard says more than a paragraph about why we don't have one. The presence of a free, well-edited topic page says more than a manifesto.
- **Slogans appear once per surface, max.** "FRQNCY makes the unable able" earns its weight through the rest of the page proving it. Repeating it 4 times turns it into noise.
- **Editorial values live in a Colophon.** Add `/v2/colophon.html` (or a section on the About page) titled something like "How this site is made." List the values plainly, sourced (Trungpa for spiritual materialism, etc.), with one sentence each. Linkable, citable, not preached.
- **Footer rotation, not banner.** The footer can rotate one slogan per page-load from the canonical list. Subtle, never the main event.
- **Voice tests.** Before any new page ships, ask: would this read as *enabling* or as *positioning*? If it positions FRQNCY against something instead of opening a door for the reader, rewrite.
