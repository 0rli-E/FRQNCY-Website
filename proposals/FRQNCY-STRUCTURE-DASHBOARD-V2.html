⓪ Executive summary — what 31 agents and an 8-expert panel found

The structure: after scanning ~200 jurisdictions and stress-testing the two finalists, the answer is one Estonian OÜ as the hub — 0% on everything retained and reinvested, ~0% on your living costs via salary-for-work-abroad, 0% on your eventual exit via share sale, 22% only on surplus dividends — with everything else trigger-gated, not day-one: a Wyoming LLC + Stripe Connect when the NRG marketplace launches, a DUNA/MTÜ wrapper before donations go live, a token entity only when a sale or listing has a date, per-deal SPVs (~$6k) instead of a fund shell, and a PropCo before the first property. Day-one cost: ~€900 once, ~€175/month.

The three findings that changed the plan: (1) the marketplace is the real regulatory event — paying coaches means money transmission unless a licensed party (Stripe Connect) holds the funds; Paddle legally can't do it. (2) The whole 0% stack is only as strong as your Paraguay substance — real lease, real presence; the red team ranked German place-of-management and thin founder residency as attacks #1 and #2. (3) Germany's Dec-2025 law change softened Norman's §13 exposure to a quantified ceiling (OÜ passive income under ⅓ of gross and ~€200k) — manageable with quarterly tracking.

The three rules that keep it alive: Norman's 49% issued at par at incorporation and Petra holding zero equity (two German shareholders together crossing 50% = CFC control) · paper matching reality — you sign and decide everything, documented outside Germany · and never the words "tax free" in public — publish the entity map instead; boring transparency is the only tax efficiency that survives contact with auditors, banks, and journalists.

⏰ TIME-CRITICAL — one item now, two gates before launch:
  • NOW — Norman's equity at par. The only genuinely clock-driven item: every month of value accrual makes the par issuance more expensive (German tax event at grant + §6 AStG exit base). Issue at incorporation, this month. Petra: no equity, cash-settled bonus instead.
  • GATE — before donations go live: the multi-sig needs a wrapper first (Wyoming DUNA ~$100 filing + lean legal, or the MTÜ). An unwrapped multi-sig = general partnership — you and Norman personally liable, Norman importing it into German tax. Good news: not live yet, so this is a launch checklist item, not a fire. Keep the placeholders until the wrapper exists.
  • GATE — before selling memberships/courses to EU consumers: the EU withdrawal/cancellation button (mandatory since 19 June 2026, fines to €2m/4%) ships with checkout, and consumer terms carry the immediate-delivery consent checkbox.

① The verdict (panel-weighted)

The architecture survives full scope — with three amendments the panel forced: (1) the NRG marketplace, not the token, is the real regulatory event — it must run on Stripe Connect (a licensed party always holds the funds; Paddle legally cannot do third-party payouts — they were FTC-fined $5m for exactly that in 2025); (2) the structure is only as strong as your residency substance — a paper Paraguay cédula plus 365-day travel is the seam every authority attacks first; give Paraguay real weight (lease, presence, and it doubles as the cheapest Settle pilot: your land is cheapest where your residency lives); (3) phase everything — the correct day-one structure is ONE entity (Estonia OÜ) and ~€175/month, with every other entity formed only when its named trigger fires.

Red team's bottom line, plainly: as originally designed (nomadic director, thin anchor, naked wallets, platform-touched funds) — not defensible. With the three fixes — real substance, Connect + wrapped donations, scrubbed "tax-free" language — it becomes "ordinary and boring, which is the only kind of tax-efficient that lasts."

⑧ Network-state lessons wired in (precedent expert)

Dependency inversion — the one lesson from the wreckage. Próspera bet on a repealable statute ($10.8bn arbitration pending). Cabin bet on a token treasury (DAO voted itself into wind-down 2025). Praxis bet on conditional capital ($525m of mostly unlockable pledges). Edge City survived by keeping the community portable and treating land, tokens, and law as replaceable modules. For FRQNCY: membership = plain subscription contract (no votes attached to money) · governance = three lanes never crossed — money (OÜ/fund, founder-governed), commons (MTÜ, one-member-one-vote, elected council with defined consent rights), signal (polls, later token sentiment — never binding treasury votes, the Ooki/general-partnership trap). If the token dies, the fund stalls, or Paraguay changes its mind — the membership contract, the canon, and the commons continue untouched. The network owns the state, never the reverse.
