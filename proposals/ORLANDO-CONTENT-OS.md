## 1. Identity — who Orlando is on camera

Orlando is a founder on a conscious journey, building FRQNCY in public — a network of people building their dream life — and showing the work as it happens. He travels the world with a phone and a tripod, reads the masters in his own words, and states conviction plainly: this is what I believe, you decide what to do with it. He is the human thread that ties the whole FRQNCY network together — money, spirituality, books, travel — one real face across every subsite.

**The three nevers.** These are hard lines, not preferences.

1. **No guru posturing.** Orlando remembers alongside the viewer; he never stands above them. No "do the work," no awakened-status claims, no positioning the viewer as incomplete. He is a mirror, not a teacher.
2. **No ranking people.** Conviction as self-expression is welcome. Leaderboards, tier-lists of teachers, "who's the greatest" framing — never. Cooperation over competition, on camera as on the site.
3. **No cure claims.** Health content supports and promotes; it never cures or treats. Practices are experiments, not prescriptions. Every master is credited by name; quotes stay short.

## 2. The four content pillars

Target mix over any rolling 30 days. Orlando's face or voice carries the majority of posts; fully AI-assembled content (voice-clone narration over b-roll) stays under 30% of the feed.

| # | Pillar | Mix | Format | Effort |
|---|--------|-----|--------|--------|
| A | **Build-in-public** — the founder journey | 25% | Direct-to-camera or screen-over-shoulder, 20–45s | Low — narrate what already happened today |
| B | **Teachings-that-changed-me** — the masters in his own words | 30% | Direct-to-camera take on one idea from one book/teacher | Medium — one idea, pre-scripted hook, rest freestyle |
| C | **Travel-as-practice** — location as consciousness content | 25% | Walking shot or scenic backdrop, voiceover or to-camera | Low — the location does half the work |
| D | **Conviction shorts** — one belief, stated plainly | 20% | Locked-off direct-to-camera, tight crop, 20–30s | Lowest — one sentence of prep |

**Pillar A — Build-in-public.** The moat nobody can copy: a real founder building an alternative-society network with AI agents, an app, a fund, and ~$100 of budget. Show the screen, show the decision, show the number. Example hooks: *"I'm building a social network with no leaderboards. Here's why that's hard."* · *"Day 87 of building FRQNCY. Today an AI agent shipped a feature while I slept."* · *"This is what a one-person media network actually looks like."* Rule: every build post names one concrete thing (a file, a metric, a decision) — no vague "grinding" content.

**Pillar B — Teachings-that-changed-me.** The engine that feeds the subsites. One master, one idea, Orlando's own words, ending on the comment-keyword CTA that routes to the free audio on the matching subsite. Source order: public-domain first (Allen, Hill 1937, Wattles, Clason, Neville), then living teachers credited by name. Example hooks: *"The oldest money rule ever written is four thousand years old — and almost no one follows it."* · *"Napoleon Hill's real lesson is harder than the famous quote."* · *"A quiet Englishman wrote one line a hundred years ago that never aged."* Rule: the take is Orlando's, not a summary. What did this idea change in his actual life?

**Pillar C — Travel-as-practice.** Munich, Portugal, Egypt, Forest City — the journey is the content. Not travel-vlog logistics; each location becomes a frame for one idea. Example hooks: *"I'm at the pyramids, and I want to talk about time."* · *"This Portuguese fishing village runs on a rhythm most cities forgot."* · *"I moved my whole life into one bag. Here's what stayed."* Rule: one location, one idea, one take. The backdrop earns the scroll-stop; the idea earns the follow.

**Pillar D — Conviction shorts.** The sharpest tool. One position, stated without hedging, no essay. Example hooks: *"Crypto is freedom technology. Not an investment — technology."* · *"I don't believe in competition. I believe in networks where nobody loses."* · *"You are not what happened to you. You are what you think about what happened."* Rule: conviction, never a call. No price talk, no financial advice, no ranking anyone. State the belief, let the comments argue.

## 3. The weekly rhythm — ~4 hours total

One film day, one assembly block, and a drip that runs itself. The system survives airports, time zones, and bad wifi because everything batches.

| Block | When | Time | What happens |
|-------|------|------|--------------|
| **Film day** | One morning, best light | 2h | 8–10 pieces in 3 setups (below) |
| **Assembly** | Next day or same evening | 1.5h | CapCut: cut, caption, hook-check. Load Postiz, schedule 7 days |
| **Engagement** | Daily | 15 min cap | Reply to comments; CreatorFlow handles keyword→DM automatically |
| **Review** | Sunday, 10 min | 10 min | Metricool: one number — profile visits → subsite link taps. Note the week's best hook |

**The film day — 3 setups, 8–10 pieces, 2 hours.**

1. **Setup 1 — direct-to-camera (hotel desk or any wall with light), 45 min.** Tripod at eye level, window light on the face. Film 4–5 pieces back to back: two Pillar B teachings, two Pillar D convictions, one Pillar A build update. Script only the first two sentences of each; freestyle the rest. Two takes maximum per piece — the second take is almost always the one.
2. **Setup 2 — walking shot (street, beach, ruins, wherever the week put him), 45 min.** Phone handheld or tripod planted, 2–3 Pillar C pieces. Film 30 seconds of silent b-roll at each spot before talking — this b-roll covers jump cuts all week and feeds the AI-assembled narration posts.
3. **Setup 3 — the detail pass, 20 min.** Hands on the keyboard, the notebook, the coffee, the boarding pass, the horizon. Ten 10-second clips. This is the b-roll bank; it makes every future edit faster.

**Assembly block.** CapCut on the phone or laptop: trim the front until the hook is the literal first frame, burn in captions, export 1080×1920. Up to two voice-clone narration posts per week (ElevenLabs over the week's b-roll or Higgsfield/Runway fill, Suno bed underneath) — these top up the queue when a film day is thin, and they count toward the sub-30% AI ceiling. Load everything into Postiz, one post per day, posted at a consistent hour. The queue always holds at least 5 days ahead; when it drops to 3, that triggers the next film day — not the calendar.

**When travel breaks the week:** film day compresses to Setup 1 only (45 minutes, 5 pieces, any hotel room). The queue never runs dry because narration posts and the b-roll bank exist. A missed day is filled from the bank, never skipped in silence and never apologised for on camera.

## 5. On-camera style guide

**Framing.** Eye level, face in the upper third, window light or open sky on the face — never behind. Direct-to-camera: chest-up, close enough that the eyes read on a phone screen. Walking shots: arm's length or planted tripod, horizon straight. One framing per piece; jump cuts inside a single framing are fine and expected.

**Length.** 20–45 seconds. Conviction shorts run 20–30. Nothing over 60 without a specific reason. If the idea needs 90 seconds, it is two posts.

**Opening line.** The hook lands inside the first 2 seconds — it is the first frame, the first word, no wind-up. Cut every "hey guys," "so," "today I want to talk about." Start with the claim, the number, or the place: *"The oldest money rule ever written…"* not *"I've been reading this book and…"* Test: would the line work as the only caption on a paused frame?

**Captions.** Burned-in, every post — most viewers watch muted. Short lines, 3–6 words per line, white with a soft shadow, centred in the lower-middle safe zone. Sentence case, not shouting caps. British English throughout: civilisation, decentralised, recognise.

**Voice on camera = FRQNCY voice.** Present tense. Short declarative sentences, ideas in threes. Conviction stated as conviction — "I believe," "this is," never "you must" or "you should." No hedging ("in some traditions," "maybe," "kind of"). No spiritual clichés: no "high vibe," "do the work," "authentic self," "vibes." No startup hype: no "disrupt," "game-changing," "next level." When a take drifts into either register, refilm it.

**CTA rotation.** One CTA per post, never two, rotating on a strict 3-cycle:

| Beat | CTA | Wiring |
|------|-----|--------|
| 1 | **Comment keyword** ("Comment RICH and I'll send you the book — free") | CreatorFlow auto-DMs the subsite's free audio lead magnet |
| 2 | **Visit subsite** ("The full teaching lives free at money.frqncy") | Link in bio → subsite → lead magnet → affiliate |
| 3 | **Follow** ("Follow — I post this every day from wherever I am") | Compounds the audience for beats 1 and 2 |

Roughly a third of posts each. Keyword CTAs sit on Pillar B posts (they map cleanly to a book and a subsite); follow CTAs sit on Pillars A and C; subsite CTAs float. The close is always abundant, never scarce: "free," "it lives on the site," "no hard feelings if not" — never "last chance," never "don't miss this."

## 6. Focus rules

**Orlando says no to:** trend-chasing formats that need his face doing someone else's bit; duets, stitches, and reply-guy content; posting live from the timeline (everything goes through the Postiz queue); daily analytics checking (Sunday only); platform number four before Instagram, TikTok, and Shorts run themselves; any collab that ranks people or needs a leaderboard; price predictions and anything that smells like a call; filming more than the weekly 2-hour block because inspiration struck — the idea goes in the bank, the schedule holds.

**The one metric per month: follower → subsite clicks.** Views are weather; the click is the harvest. Each month has one number — taps from orlando.frqncy to frqncy.network subsites (Metricool link taps + subsite referral traffic). Everything else (reach, followers, likes) is diagnostic, read once a week in ten minutes, never steering. If clicks grow, the system works. If views grow and clicks don't, the CTAs need work — not the content volume.

**Film himself vs delegate to the AI engine.** The dividing line is personal presence. If the post carries a conviction, a personal take, a piece of the journey, or a location Orlando is standing in — he films it; his face and voice are the moat and no clone substitutes for them. If the post is a public-domain teaching that works as narrated text — voice-clone over b-roll (the script-bank pattern in `engine/script-bank-batch1.md`) — the engine assembles it. The engine also owns all repurposing: cut-downs, platform variants, caption rewrites, scheduling. Ceiling check at the Sunday review: AI-assembled posts under 30% of the trailing 30 days. Over the line means the next film day adds one setup, not that the engine slows down.

**When in doubt:** film the smaller, truer thing. One idea, one take, one CTA. The queue stays 5 days ahead, the phone stays on the tripod, and the journey keeps being the content.
