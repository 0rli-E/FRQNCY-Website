**2. Consideration (going deeper)** — Actions: checks profile, watches more, visits the site. Thoughts: "real depth, or does it thin out?" Emotion: cautious interest `(+)`. Touchpoints: profile, more reels, subsite/topic pages. Pain: overwhelm if too much; instant bounce if a pitch appears. **3. Micro-yes (comment keyword → open DM)** — Actions: comments RICH/CREATE/READ, opens the DM. Thoughts: "let me see / let me get the free thing." Emotion: curiosity + mild hope. **Drivers: feeling understood + a clear next step.** Touchpoints: comment, CreatorFlow DM. Pain: the comment→DM friction, distrust of automation. Opportunity: warm, human DM + one clear low-risk step. `[A]`

**4. Email-yes (opt-in for free audio)** — Actions: taps link → subsite → gives email → gets the free audio. Thoughts: "worth my email?" Emotion: small trust *if the gift is real*. Touchpoints: subsite page, free audio, email capture. Pain: off-platform bounce, opt-in wall before value, an ugly/foreign page. Opportunity: deliver value fast, gift-framing not a toll, same-world visual/voice continuity. `[A]`

Emotion: delight OR anticlimax/dip. **Join drivers: belonging + a real path** (early change confirms later). Touchpoints: welcome, email sequence, app, community. **6. Retention / membership deepening** — Actions: returns, practices, uses the coach app, engages community. Thoughts: "part of who I am" vs "I've drifted." Emotion: sustained connection vs plateau. **Churn cliffs: life gets busy + the weeks 3–6 'nothing's changing' dip.** Touchpoints: coach app, community, email, audio (during lifting). Pain: invisible progress, busy life, repetition. Opportunity: **make progress VISIBLE** (the coach app reflects growth back) · **fit a busy life** (audio-first, no shame-streak) · welcome-not-shame re-entry. `[A]`

**7. Advocacy / belonging** — Actions: shares a teaching, invites a friend, contributes/stewards. Thoughts: "these are my people." Emotion: belonging, pride `(+)`. Touchpoints: shareable teachings, community roles. Pain: nothing shareable, referral that feels like marketing. Opportunity: sharing as **generosity / self-expression**, peers not hierarchy, path to becoming a steward. `[A]`
