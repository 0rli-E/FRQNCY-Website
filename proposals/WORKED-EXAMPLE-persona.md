## BANK 2 · EMPATHY MAP — Mara (short)
*Goal first, then observable outside, then inferred inside. The Say-vs-Think gap is the finding.*

- **Goal / Who:** Mara, 37, studio owner, a Tuesday-night "is this it?" after hitting a goal. Resolve: *stop maintaining a life, start inhabiting it.* `[H, anchored to the F spine in Q10]`
- **See:** peers posting retreats + "morning routines"; a market split between shallow calm-apps and $2k funnels; the contradiction she lives — successful outside, hollow within. `[F on the market turn-off · H on peers]`
