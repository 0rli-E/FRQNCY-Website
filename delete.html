delete your data

FRQNCY does not collect personal data.

The FRQNCY mobile app and the FRQNCY website are designed to keep your information on your own device. We do not run a user database, we do not log who reads what, and we do not share data with advertisers, analytics platforms, or third-party trackers.

If you've used FRQNCY, the data that exists about you lives in two places — both under your control:

  • On your phone: alarm settings, downloaded audio, cached content, and your accessibility preferences. Deleted when you delete the app.
  • On your devices' system services: notification history, OS-level alarm registrations, HealthKit / Health Connect permission entries (if granted). Cleared via system settings or when you delete the app.

to delete the app's local data

Android: Settings → Apps → FRQNCY → Storage → Clear data, then Uninstall.

iOS: long-press the FRQNCY icon → Remove App → Delete App. iOS removes all app data automatically.

if you ever shared something with us directly

If you've sent us an email or a message and want it deleted, write to hello@frqncy.network with the email address you wrote from. We'll remove every message we have from that address within 30 days and reply to confirm.

We never use your email address to market to you, and we don't keep a separate marketing list.

future versions of FRQNCY

If FRQNCY adds accounts in a future release (e.g. for cross-device favourites), the same deletion route will apply: send us your account email and we'll remove the account and all associated data within 30 days. Account deletion will also be available from inside the app, per Apple App Store and Google Play requirements.

This page exists because Google Play's Data Safety policy requires every app to publish a deletion path — even apps like FRQNCY that don't collect data in the first place. Last updated 2026-04-29. Contact: hello@frqncy.network.
