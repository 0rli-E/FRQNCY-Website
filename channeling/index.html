Sedona

Sedona, Arizona, USA — Red-rock cathedral country in the high Arizona desert. The vortex sites — Cathedral Rock, Bell Rock, Boynton Canyon, Airport Mesa — draw seekers, mystics, channellers, and the merely curious. A town where the metaphysical bookshop is older than the supermarket, and where the land itself feels charged. Sacred to the Yavapai and Apache long before the New Age found it.
