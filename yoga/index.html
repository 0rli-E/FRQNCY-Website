People
Person

B.K.S. Iyengar

Studied with T. Krishnamacharya in Mysore in the 1930s, then built the precision-alignment school of yoga that bears his name. Light on Yoga (1966) catalogued 200 asanas with the photographic rigour that made him the West's most-translated yoga teacher of the twentieth century.

Visit →Person

Paramhansa Yogananda

The master who brought Kriya Yoga to the West, and whose Autobiography of a Yogi remains one of the most influential spiritual autobiographies ever written. Steve Jobs requested it be distributed at his memorial. A living transmission of communion with the Source.

Visit →Person

Richard Hittleman

Brought hatha yoga to American television in 1961 with Yoga for Health, the first nationally syndicated yoga show in the US. His 28 Day Exercise Plan introduced millions of Americans to yoga before the studio era began.

Places
Place

Intaaya

Regenerative retreat sanctuary on Nusa Penida, near Bali. Off-grid, farm-to-table, living to the rhythm of nature. A living example of the Sanctuary pillar — the physical extension of the FRQNCY network.

Visit →Place

Esalen Institute

Founded 1962 on the cliffs of Big Sur. The hub of the human potential movement — where Gestalt, somatics, encounter groups, and east-meets-west consciousness work were forged. Hot springs, workshops, retreats. Much of the modern body-and-mind vocabulary was first written down here.

Visit →Place

Yogaville

Satchidananda Ashram, founded in 1980 by Sri Swami Satchidananda — the swami who opened Woodstock. Integral Yoga community on 750 acres along the James River, with the LOTUS shrine at its centre: a temple to all faiths. Retreats, immersions, daily satsang. The clearest American expression of integral, all-paths spirituality as a lived practice.
