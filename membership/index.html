FAQ
Is the public site changing?

No. The 146 topic pages, the 770+ curated resources, the explore graph, the Watch library, the chat widget, the chart calculator — all of it stays free and accessible without an account. Membership unlocks the deeper, personal-data layer (Sanctuary dashboard, AI readings on your own chart, practice tracking). The free site exists because we believe the teachings should live somewhere they can be found; membership exists because some people want the tools and want to support the work.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly cancels at the end of the current cycle. Annual cancels at the end of the year (or you can request a prorated refund within the first 30 days). Your data stays yours either way — exports work whether you're a member or not.

What happens at the founding-member tier?

The first cohort of members across the network gets a permanent founder badge on their public profile, locked-in pricing for as long as their membership is continuous, and an open invite to the first FRQNCY Space's founding circle when it opens. There's no scarcity manufactured around this — it's recognition for early belief, not a marketing tactic.

Why one tier instead of three?

Because tier ladders push people toward upgrades they don't need. One tier, one price, everything included. When the first physical FRQNCY Space opens, a separate Space-member tier will exist for in-person access — but the digital network stays one membership.

Will you take crypto?

Yes — the plan is to support crypto payments alongside Stripe. The first version is Stripe-only because it's the fastest way to test whether the offer resonates. Crypto is queued.

What about referrals?

Every member gets a personal referral link. Refer 3 → free month. Refer 10 → invite to the quarterly community gathering. Refer 25 → permanent founder badge. Rewards are membership-shaped, not cash. Details land when membership goes live.

I want to support FRQNCY without becoming a member.

You can. There's also a one-time-contribution path planned, and the Fund pillar (long-horizon) will be a separate way to back the network at the capital level. Reach out via hello@frqncy.network if you want to do that today and we'll find a clean path together.

Will membership ever fund things I disagree with?

The Editorial Standards doc (`proposals/EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md` once public) defines what membership revenue can and can't fund. Short version: it funds free-layer infrastructure, Sanctuary operations, and editorial work. It does not fund sponsored content, paid placements, or anything that compromises the picks themselves. If a Fund-pillar deal flow opens later, that's a separate vehicle with its own governance.
