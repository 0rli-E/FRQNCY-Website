Membership is the economic side of FRQNCY. It funds the editorial work and the infrastructure that keeps the site free for everyone else. Members don't get a secret library — every teaching still lives on the public site. They get a few practical things that come from being closer to how the work is made.

What your support funds
01
Editorial work

The picks, the topic pages, the resource curation, the voice. The hours that turn 766 raw entries into something a reader can trust.

02
Infrastructure

Hosting, search index, AI chat, the chart engine, the Sanctuary backend. Free to use, paid for somewhere — by members.

03
Contributor honoraria

When a teacher's work shapes a topic page or a profile, they get paid. Not always large; always real. Membership funds that line.

04
The Fund seed

A small carve-out underwrites the longer-horizon Fund pillar — the network state's investment vehicle for conscious-business companies the picks surface.
