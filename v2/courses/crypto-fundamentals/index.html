Open + Tour Your Kraken Account

Create a Kraken account and learn your way around the dashboard. We use Kraken because of its long track record of solvency and proof-of-reserves transparency. By the end you'll know where to buy, sell, deposit, withdraw, and transfer.

Two browser extensions, one workflow. Rabby for Ethereum and EVM chains. Phantom for Bitcoin, Solana, and SUI. Self-custody starts here.

Video10 min

Seed Phrase Security — Non-Negotiable

Your seed phrase is your money. Lose it, lose everything. Write it down on paper, never type it into a computer, never photograph it, never share it. This lesson — and doing it properly — is the difference between holding crypto and losing it.
