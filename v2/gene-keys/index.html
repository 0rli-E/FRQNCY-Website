The Three Frequencies

Shadow

The Pattern

The reactive, contracted expression of each Gene Key — the unconscious program running beneath the surface. Not something to overcome, but something to embrace and understand.

Gift

The Potential

When the shadow is held with awareness, its energy naturally transforms into the gift — the creative, functional expression available to everyone without effort.

Siddhi

The Highest

The transcendent, grace-filled expression of the Gene Key — a possibility that dawns spontaneously through deep contemplation and surrender. Not a goal to achieve.
