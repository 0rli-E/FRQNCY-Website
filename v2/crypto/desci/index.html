DeSci

Community-funded science. Open IP. Real-world research.

DAOs, tokenised IP, and open-access publishing protocols — crypto coordination applied to the things science funding has historically neglected.

Academic funding is slow, risk-averse, and concentrated in a small number of institutions. DeSci bets that coordination tools from crypto — DAOs, tokenised IP, and open incentives — can route capital and talent to research the traditional system underfunds: longevity, women's health, psychedelics, open data.

This is the most mission-aligned category in crypto. It also has the hardest time shipping results on blockchain timelines — real science is slow. Patience required.

This is not financial advice. Conviction is stated as conviction. You decide what to do with it. Always do your own work before you put money anywhere.
