Real World Assets

Bringing Treasuries, credit, and real estate on-chain.

Tokenised versions of off-chain assets — US Treasuries, corporate credit, private equity, real estate. The bridge between the biggest markets in the world and the fastest settlement rails.

The RWA thesis is simple: traditional finance has better yields than DeFi, and crypto has better rails than TradFi. Tokenisation lets both sides meet — regulated assets accessible from any wallet, settlement that happens in minutes instead of T+2.

Watch the regulatory layer here carefully. Most RWA products have KYC gates, qualified-investor thresholds, and issuer risk. The tech is crypto; the legal wrapper is very much not.
