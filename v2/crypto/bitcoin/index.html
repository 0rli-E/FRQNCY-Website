Bitcoin

The base layer, and the protocols that extend it. Bitcoin solved sound money. Everything here is about making that money usable — fast, private, everyday.

Bitcoin is the settlement layer. It is deliberately slow, deliberately conservative, deliberately boring — because it is meant to last. That is the feature, not the bug.

The interesting work happens on top. Lightning makes payments instant. Fedimint and Cashu add privacy and community custody. Liquid gives traders faster settlement. Nostr wires identity and speech to bitcoin keys. Together they turn the base layer into something people can actually use.

This is not financial advice. Conviction is stated as conviction. You decide what to do with it. Always do your own work before you put money anywhere.
