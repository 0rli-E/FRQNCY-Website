DeFi

Finance without intermediaries. Code as counterparty.

Lending, exchange, derivatives, stablecoins — rebuilt as open protocols anyone can use, audit, or fork. No branches, no account minimums, no gatekeepers.

DeFi replaces the desk, the broker, and the clearinghouse with smart contracts. Liquidity is pooled, prices are set by curves and auctions, and settlement is atomic. The collateral is visible to anyone who wants to look.

DeFi's real proof point wasn't the 2021 boom — it was surviving 2022. While centralised counterparties blew up (Celsius, 3AC, FTX), on-chain protocols just kept clearing.
