Crypto Neobanks

Banking for people who keep their own keys.

The thin layer between self-custody and everyday life — apps and cards that let you earn, spend, and save in crypto without giving up ownership of it.

Most crypto is stuck in one of two modes: held on a cold wallet you never touch, or trapped on an exchange you don't really own. Neobanks are the attempt to build the third mode — usable rails on top of actual ownership. Spend your bitcoin. Earn yield in your own custody. Pay rent from a DeFi vault.

A bank is a business model, not a building. These projects are rebuilding that business model on open rails — some bitcoin-maximalist, some chain-agnostic, all betting that the bank of the future looks more like an app than a branch.
