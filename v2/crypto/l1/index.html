Layer 1s

Base chains. Independent consensus. Sovereign networks.

Smart contract platforms that provide their own security, their own consensus, and their own economy. The substrate everything else is built on.

An L1 is an independent blockchain — its own validators, its own token, its own rules. It does not inherit security from another chain. The bet on any L1 is a bet on its community, its fee market, and the apps that choose to call it home.

Most L1s will not survive the decade. The ones that do will be the ones where real economic activity — not just incentives — keeps accruing.

This is not financial advice. Conviction is stated as conviction. You decide what to do with it. Always do your own work before you put money anywhere.
