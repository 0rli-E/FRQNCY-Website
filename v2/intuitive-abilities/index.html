Intuitive Abilities

The latent capacities humans have always reported and rarely trained — intuition, claircognisance, remote sensing, energy reading. Treated here as practice, not parlour trick: the more you measure, the more the signal sharpens.

Argentinian teacher and author who speaks from a recovered memory of the Akashic records. Cosmology, multidimensionality, the long history of humans before recorded history. Reads as fiction to the skeptic, as memory to the open. Worth the listen either way for the prose alone.

American psychic, healer, and broadcaster known for his white afro, his bluntness on television, and his decades of close-quarters energy work. Middleway

Mobile app for training intuition as a practice. Daily exercises ask you to predict, sense, or remote-feel something verifiable — then score the result. Treats intuitive capacity the way a gym treats strength: trainable, measurable, sharpenable.
