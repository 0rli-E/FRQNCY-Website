Word Illuminator

Single words, fully attended to. Definitions, etymology, derivatives, and the interpretive layer that connects the word to lived practice. Each illumination is a small map of how a word actually works in the world.

How it works

Synthesised, not scraped

An illumination resembles what a careful editor would write after holding a word against four traditions of reference at once — current usage, historical etymology, classical roots, and the philosophical writing on meaning. The output is structured to read top-down: definitions first, then etymology, then synonyms, then the word family, then the deeper layer at the bottom for readers who want it.

Same disclosure principle as a well-edited Wikipedia article. Grounded in the tradition. Not copy-pasted from any one source.

What's coming

The shelf grows over time

More illuminations get written when a word warrants attention. Candidates queued: presence, integrity, sovereignty, conviction, remembrance, alignment, contemplation, witness, silence, truth. Each becomes its own page. Each one stays. The shelf is the teaching.

Word Illuminator is a tool — but every illumination is a static page on FRQNCY. They are not generated on demand and discarded. They live on the site and link to the topics, books, and people in the network that hold the same word's weight.
