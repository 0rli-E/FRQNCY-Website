Hermes Trismegistus

Legendary figure — part Greek god, part Egyptian sage — traditionally credited with the Emerald Tablet and the Hermetic Corpus. The source of the phrase 'as above, so below' and the foundation of Western esoteric philosophy.

Visit →Person

Manly P. Hall

American esoteric scholar whose Secret Teachings of All Ages (1928) attempted to map the entire Western wisdom tradition in one volume. His Philosophical Research Society in Los Angeles remains the home of his lectures and library — one of the great archives of sacred philosophy.

Three Initiates

Pseudonymous authors of The Kybalion (1908), the modern distillation of seven Hermetic principles — mentalism, correspondence, vibration, polarity, rhythm, cause and effect, gender. Identity unknown. Text foundational.
