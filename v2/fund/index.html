The FRQNCY Fund

Decentralized capital for conscious projects.

A community-driven fund built on the belief that capital should flow toward what elevates — not just what extracts. Powered by decentralized fundraising platforms that put ownership in the hands of the people.

Why a Fund?

The world doesn't lack good ideas. It lacks a way to fund them without selling their soul. Traditional venture capital demands growth at all costs, centralizes control, and optimizes for extraction. The FRQNCY Fund is an experiment in the opposite direction.

We believe that the same communities consuming conscious content — meditation, healing, sovereignty, self-mastery — should also be the ones funding the next wave of projects in these spaces. Not VCs. Not corporations. The people who care.

Decentralized fundraising platforms have made this possible for the first time. Capital formation no longer requires intermediaries. It requires community, conviction, and transparent infrastructure.

◇

Sovereignty

Your contribution, your ownership. No middlemen diluting your stake or making decisions for you.

◈

Transparency

Every allocation onchain. Every decision visible. Trust through mathematics, not promises.

◉

Alignment

Capital directed toward projects that elevate consciousness, build community, and serve the whole.

The Path Forward

Phase 1 · Now

Vision & Community

Building the FRQNCY network, curating the knowledge base, and gathering a community of aligned individuals who believe in conscious capital allocation.

Phase 2 · Foundation

Fund Structure & Partnerships

Establishing the fund's governance framework, partnering with Echo.xyz and Legion for deal flow, and defining the criteria for what "conscious projects" means in practice.

Phase 3 · Launch

First Allocations

Opening the fund to community participation through decentralized fundraising platforms. First capital deployed into projects vetted by the FRQNCY community.

Phase 4 · Evolution

DAO Governance

Transitioning to full community governance. Token holders vote on allocations, project vetting, and fund strategy. The fund becomes owned by those who built it.
