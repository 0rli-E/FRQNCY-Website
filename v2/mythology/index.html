The Book of Enoch — Various (attributed to Enoch)

Pre-flood text excluded from most modern canons but preserved in the Ethiopian Bible. The clearest extant source on the Watchers, the Nephilim, and what the deluge erased — a window into the cosmology that other early texts assume but never explain.
