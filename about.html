The Distinction

Spiritual technology, not spiritual materialism.

Chögyam Trungpa named the trap fifty years ago. Spiritual materialism is using practice to inflate the self — collecting credentials, accessing scenes, signalling depth. It looks like the work, but it makes the seeker more attached, not less.

Spiritual technology is the opposite. A practice, a teaching, a tool that genuinely makes someone more able — clearer, freer, more capable of meeting their life. It dissolves rather than decorates.

FRQNCY is built around this test. A book gets a pick because we used it and it worked. A teacher belongs in the network because their work moves people, not because it moves units. FRQNCY makes the unable able. That is the standard, applied without exception.
