02 · How

How it actually works.

You spend about ten minutes telling a private companion where you are right now. It asks the questions a good friend or a good therapist would — what you keep returning to, what you keep avoiding, what your body says, what your chart says.

It builds a profile that lives with you. Then it walks you through three stages, at your pace. The whole arc is designed for the day you no longer need it.

01 · Find · in VBRTN

Find yourself

You do: answer a short intake. Bring your Human Design chart, your Gene Keys, your story — or none of those, and just speak in your own words.

You leave with: a clearer picture of how you actually decide, what energises you, what drains you. Lenses you chose, held lightly. No diagnosis.

02 · Create · in NRG Social

Create from that

You do: bring an idea you care about. A business, a piece of work, a relationship, a project you keep restarting.

You leave with: the idea shaped around who you now know yourself to be. Real coaches, collaborators, and a marketplace one click away.

03 · Live · out in the world

Step into the world

You do: stop checking the app. Run the habits on your own. Show up in the community because you want to.

You leave with: a life you actually live. We meet you in physical spaces — townhalls, coworking, land. The whole point.

A few promises
Your intake is private to you. Nothing is sold, ranked, or paywalled. The companion suggests, never prescribes. You can leave with your data at any time.

How the network keeps itself honest

Underneath the user journey, FRQNCY operates on eight pillars: Curate, Educate, Research, Broadcast, Sell, Fund, Build, Settle. No ads. No paid placement. No paywalled thesis. The free public layer is load-bearing.

Why They Exist

To let people experience more and different things on this physical journey.

Spiritual technology and spiritual materialism share a single purpose: to enable a human being to encounter more of life — and a different quality of life — while they are here in a body. They do not compete. They serve the same end from two sides.

Spiritual technology is the practice, the teaching, the tool, the piece of software that genuinely makes someone more able — clearer, freer, more capable of meeting their life. The breathwork that opens the chest. The book that reorganises a mind. The retreat that lands a person back in their body. Each one widens the bandwidth of what they can perceive and do.

Spiritual materialism is the physical expression of the same impulse — the beads, the books on the shelf, the linen, the candles, the studios, the well-designed apps, the spaces and the artefacts. Form that holds the practice. Matter is the field where the inner work becomes visible, transmissible, and shareable. Beautiful objects made for real practice are part of the path, because the physical journey is the path.

FRQNCY honours both. The teaching and the artefact. The frequency and the form it takes. A book gets a pick because we used it and it worked. A space, a chart, a wallet — each is held to the same standard. FRQNCY makes the unable able.

The phrase is a claim about removal, not addition. Unable is not capability deficit — it is unsurfaced. What FRQNCY does is remove the obstacle between what a person already is and what shows up. The map, the reading, the network, the Fund: each is a translation device. When the conditions arrive, the able shows up; it was always there.

The Thesis

Once enough of us remember, the field reorganises.

Lyall Watson called it the hundredth monkey. Rupert Sheldrake called it morphic resonance. The folk frame is older than both: when enough of a population learns a new behaviour, the rest of the field picks it up without direct transmission.

Most networks aim for reach. FRQNCY aims for critical mass. The work is to gather the humans who already remember, surface the practices that already work, and hold them in coherent attention long enough for the field to reorganise around them.

Not a movement. Not a brand. A frequency that gets loud enough that the rest of the system tunes in.
