01 · Why

A network of people,
building their dream life.

You're here because the default scripts aren't quite working. The career you were told to want. The metrics you were told to chase. The version of yourself you were sold.

FRQNCY is for the moment after you notice that. When you know there's another way to live, but no one has handed you the map.

The belief is simple. A person who knows who they are — what their body wants, how their mind is shaped, what they're actually for — builds differently. They start work that doesn't burn them out. They love without performing. They make things that don't lie. A new earth begins there.

02 · How

How it actually works.

You spend about ten minutes telling a private companion where you are right now. It asks the questions a good friend or a good therapist would — what you keep returning to, what you keep avoiding, what your body says, what your chart says.

It builds a profile that lives with you. Then it walks you through three stages, at your pace. The whole arc is designed for the day you no longer need it.

01 · Find · in VBRTN

Find yourself

You do: answer a short intake. Bring your Human Design chart, your Gene Keys, your story — or none of those, and just speak in your own words.

You leave with: a clearer picture of how you actually decide, what energises you, what drains you. Lenses you chose, held lightly. No diagnosis.

02 · Create · in NRG Social

Create from that

You do: bring an idea you care about. A business, a piece of work, a relationship, a project you keep restarting.

You leave with: the idea shaped around who you now know yourself to be. Real coaches, collaborators, and a marketplace one click away.

03 · Live · out in the world

Step into the world

You do: stop checking the app. Run the habits on your own. Show up in the community because you want to.

You leave with: a life you actually live. We meet you in physical spaces — townhalls, coworking, land. The whole point.

A few promises
Your intake is private to you. Nothing is sold, ranked, or paywalled. The companion suggests, never prescribes. You can leave with your data at any time.

How the network keeps itself honest

Underneath the user journey, FRQNCY operates on eight pillars: Curate, Educate, Research, Broadcast, Sell, Fund, Build, Settle. No ads. No paid placement. No paywalled thesis. The free public layer is load-bearing.
