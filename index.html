Dordogne, southern France

Plum Village

Thich Nhat Hanh's mindfulness practice centre, founded 1982 in the south of France. The home of Engaged Buddhism in the West — slow walks, silent meals, the bell of mindfulness pulling attention back to the body across an afternoon.

Dartington, Devon, England

Schumacher College

A holistic learning centre founded 1990 on the Dartington estate in Devon. Named for E.F. Schumacher, author of Small Is Beautiful. Ecology, systems thinking, regenerative economics — taught with hands in the soil and a curriculum that treats head and body as one instrument.

Big Sur, California, USA

Esalen Institute

Founded 1962 on the cliffs of Big Sur. The hub of the human potential movement — where Gestalt, somatics, encounter groups, and east-meets-west consciousness work were forged. Hot springs, workshops, retreats. Much of the modern wellness vocabulary was first written down here.

Carmel Valley, California, USA

Tassajara Zen Mountain Center

The first Zen Buddhist monastery established in the West, founded 1967 in a remote Big Sur canyon. Hot springs, silence, traditional Soto Zen training. Home of the Tassajara Bread Book — and proof that monastic form translates without losing what made it work in the original.

Nusa Penida, Bali, Indonesia

Intaaya

Regenerative retreat sanctuary on Nusa Penida, near Bali. Off-grid, farm-to-table, living to the rhythm of nature. A living example of the Sanctuary pillar — the physical extension of the FRQNCY network.

Tamil Nadu, southern India

Auroville

A universal city in southern India, founded 1968 to embody human unity. Inspired by Sri Aurobindo and The Mother, endorsed by UNESCO. A half-century-long experiment in voluntary, post-national community — where the network-state question is already a built environment, not a thesis.

Aljezur, Portugal

Essência

An off-grid regenerative eco-village and holistic healing center on the wild Atlantic coast of southern Portugal. Essência runs as an intentional community curating retreats, courses, and a Healing Center alongside daily life — a natural laboratory for self-exploration, individual realisation, and collective healing. Sustainable living, ecovillage practice, and community living shown as one continuous discipline rather than three separate trends.

Faber, Virginia, USA

The Monroe Institute

Founded 1971 in the Blue Ridge foothills of Virginia. The world's leading institute for structured exploration of consciousness — home of Hemi-Sync audio technology and the Gateway Voyage program. States beyond ordinary waking taught as skills, not visited as accidents.

Moray coast, Scotland

Findhorn Foundation

A spiritual community and ecovillage on the Scottish coast, founded 1962. Famed for the early gardens grown 'in cooperation with the intelligence of nature,' now a working model of regenerative living — half a century of evidence that ecovillage practice is a long game, not a season.

Remember.
Create.

01 / Choose

Curate

 02 / Learn

Education

What is known, transmitted. Your chart read back to you. The masters pointed to, not mimicked. Body, food, breath, practice.

 03 / Know

Research

You ask what a thing is at its very core, then answer. Truth with strong ethics, for the benefit of mankind. Everywhere academia will not go.

 04 / Broadcast

Media

The voice of the network. A swarm of .frqncies all over social media, super aligned with the good in the world. Podcast. Video. Writing.

 05 / Exchange

Sell

 06 / Fuel

Fund

An actual fund. Money gathered, deployed into aligned projects and people. Sovereign custody. We always give in abundance.

 07 / Make

Build

Products, tools, experiences — from the accumulated talent of the network. Aligned projects supported. FRQNCY-native things built.

 08 / Inhabit

Network State

A parallel society of people at similar frequency. From the digital into the physical. Coworking first. Then a building. Then several. Then land.
