The Physical Space

A Place to Think, Rest, and Connect

FRQNCY Space is a minimal, intentionally designed sanctuary. Coworking. Breathwork. Community. The first physical node of the Sanctuary you are becoming.

What It Is

A Counterpoint to Noise

Most spaces are loud, fractured, and demand your attention. FRQNCY Space is the opposite. Calm coworking areas. Quiet rooms for deep work. Events. Breathwork sessions. Community dinners. A place designed for the people who want to think, not just exist.

Calm Coworking — Focus-friendly environment with intentional design.
Quiet Rooms — Deep work spaces free from distraction.
Breathwork Sessions — Regular guided sessions for grounding and presence.
Community Events — Dinners, talks, and gatherings for members.

Retreat Space

Forest City — The FRQNCY Retreat

Forest City is the vision for FRQNCY's dedicated retreat destination — a space designed for deep immersion, away from the rhythm of daily life. Multi-day retreats combining breathwork, meditation, movement, conscious technology, and community in a setting built for transformation.

Not a resort. Not a hotel. A place where the FRQNCY community comes together for extended practice — where conversations become collaborations, and ideas become action.
