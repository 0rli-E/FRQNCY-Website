## HOUSE VOICE RULES
*(locked as of Section 2.1 — 2.2)*

**Register:** warm + reverent + metaphysical-but-plain + addressed-to-you. Not mystical-abstract. Not startup-casual. Reference examples Orli liked: options A, B, and D from the register test.

**Tense:** Present tense is mandatory. FRQNCY *is*, not *will be*. "You are" > "you can become." No future-promise or past-nostalgia copy.

**Power phrases:** *I am, we are, you are, this is, now, here.*
**Avoid:** *we will, someday, in the future, when we're ready.*

**Self-reference:**
- **"We"** → warm, communal. Use in About, CTAs, welcome copy.
- **"FRQNCY"** → institutional, declarative. Use in pillar descriptions, thesis statements.
- **"The network"** → collective, emergent. Use in Space / Community / distributed-ownership copy.
- Chosen per context, not mixed randomly.

**Sentence shape:** Short, declarative. Lists of three. The pattern Orli naturally uses: *"Unconditional love. Eternal light. Burning passion."*

**Signature words Orli uses (keepers):** *remembering, knowingness, expression, wholeness, abundance, bliss, magical, burning, eternal, unconditional, frequency, vibration, energy, love, light.*

**Trajectory context (not for copy yet):** Team now → governance token later. The "we" eventually becomes token-holders. This is why structural language ("a community becoming a society") is the right frame even now.

**Abundance rule (added during 3.3):** FRQNCY **always gives in abundance.** Core thesis, core research, core curation, core pillar pages — free and open. Monetisation (referrals, affiliates, subscriptions, the Fund, eventually the token) lives at the edges and in the depths, never gates the center. Never paywall the thesis. Never scarcity-frame access to awakening. This is a direct echo of 1.6b — *create from wholeness, abundance, bliss*, not from lack.

**"FRQNCY the token" (noted during 3.3):** Orli explicitly referenced "FRQNCY the token" as an incentive mechanism for research contributors. Token is no longer just a future governance mechanic — it's becoming an active reward layer for valuable work across the network. Still not for public site copy yet, but locked as a real trajectory.

**Win-win only rule (added during 3.4):** FRQNCY **only supports winning systems.** There cannot be a loser in any transaction FRQNCY makes. Every partnership, investment, referral, curation, and piece of copy must create **upward momentum spirals** — positive-sum loops that lift every party involved. Rules out extractive partnerships, affiliate arrangements that exploit the audience, fund bets that profit at the expense of portfolio companies, and any framing that pits people against each other. Tracks with 2.3's "cooperative" and the pre-existing cooperation-over-competition value. Phrase for copy: **"upward momentum spirals."**

**Library has content (added during 3.4):** The "library" (kept in 2.3) now has concrete contents — curated existing research, teachers, courses, media — placed into the FRQNCY network library. FRQNCY curates, supports, and hosts existing work rather than requiring everything to be produced from scratch.

**Naming architecture — three layers (expanded during 3.5):**
1. **Parent brand layer:** FRQNCY → VBRTN → NRG (physics-spiritual bridge, vowels dropped).
2. **Topic channel layer:** `xyz.frqncy` — every isle of passion in Explore earns a branded social/media channel (e.g., `crypto.frqncy`, `publicspeaking.frqncy`), similar in concept to `.eth` signalling Ethereum-native identity.
3. **(Possibly later) Individual identity layer:** hinted at in 3.5 ("everyone in the network will be xyz.frqncy") — pending how /social/ identities are implemented.
The naming system itself *is* the branding. It signals alignment at every layer, physics and spirit held simultaneously.

### 2.5 — Reference voices (whose tone FRQNCY borrows from)
> "The five personas on the FRQNCY website, then books like *Ask and It Is Given* — there's a few — Neville Goddard, Dr. Wayne W. Dyer."

**The five personas** (from `/v2/people/` — the actual teachers FRQNCY points to):

1. **Osho** (Bhagwan Shree Rajneesh, 1931–1990) — Spirituality, Meditation, Philosophy
   Voice: provocative, paradoxical, poetic, direct. Uses silence as punctuation. Challenges the reader rather than comforting them. Unafraid to offend in service of waking someone up.

2. **Sadhguru** (Jaggi Vasudev) — Yoga, Spirituality, Ecology
   Voice: disarming humor, practical-mystical, grounded, sharp analogies. "Yoga as technology." Never solemn. Speaks as if this is all obvious.

3. **Kevin Trudeau** — Self-Mastery, Manifestation, Hidden Knowledge
   Voice: intimate, teacher-to-you framing, revelatory. "What they don't want you to know." Declarative. Treats the listener as a potential master with hidden access.

4. **Sai Maa** (Her Holiness Sai Maa Lakshmi Devi) — Enlightenment, Energy Healing, Neuroscience
   Voice: elevated, reverent, bridges ancient wisdom and modern science. Speaks from embodied divinity. Never apologetic about the metaphysics.

5. **Gary Spivey** — Psychic, Healing, Consciousness
   Voice: everyman mystic. Warm, immediate, direct. Speaks *to* you, reads you right now. No academic distance whatsoever.

**Shared register across all five:**
- Direct, declarative, present-tense address
- Experience over theory — each teaches from direct knowing, not scholarship
- Treats the listener as already whole / already capable, never as a beginner being instructed
- Unembarrassed mystical content delivered plainly
- Warmth without condescension
- Confidence that borders on controversy — none of them soften themselves for mainstream palatability
- Each positions the reader as a potential master, not a student

**Book / teacher lineage (stated):**
- *Ask and It Is Given* (Abraham-Hicks)
- Neville Goddard
- Dr. Wayne W. Dyer

**The through-line across personas + books:**
All eight sources are **conscious-creation tradition**. "You are already the thing you seek." Manifest from being. Create from wholeness. Present-tense "I AM" and "you are." This is *not* Buddhist detachment, *not* Stoic restraint, *not* academic religious studies. It's the tradition that says *you are the creator, remember that, and create from there.*

**What this locks for the site:**

*Rules out, permanently:*
- Striving language ("work toward," "earn," "discipline yourself into")
- Lack-frame copy ("you are missing," "you need," "without this")
- Academic / distanced metaphysics ("one might consider," "in some traditions…")
- Softening the metaphysical content for mainstream palatability
- Anything that positions the reader as a beginner being instructed

*Rules in:*
- "You are." "We are." "This is."
- Repetition of core truths instead of new clever phrasings
- Trust in the reader's existing knowingness
- Plain speech about non-plain things
- Confidence bordering on provocation — FRQNCY should be willing to be "too much" for someone who's not ready
- Humor is allowed (Sadhguru) — reverence doesn't mean solemn
